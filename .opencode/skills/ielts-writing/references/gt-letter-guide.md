# GT 书信（General Training Task 1）完整指南（2026 版）

> 适用：General Training 考试 Task 1（书信写作，约 150–180 词，20 分钟）。
> 与 Academic 小作文（图表）的评分标准、字数、结构都不同。
> **2026 年新增模块**：原 skill 文档未覆盖 GT，本文件补足。

## 一、GT vs Academic 关键差异

| 维度 | GT Task 1 | Academic Task 1 |
|---|---|---|
| **题型** | 书信（信件） | 图表描述 |
| **字数** | 150–180 词（建议不低于 150，不超过 200） | 170–190 词 |
| **时间** | 20 分钟 | 20 分钟 |
| **评分项** | Task Achievement（目的 + 要点 + 语气） | Task Achievement（数据覆盖 + Overview） |
| **段落** | 3–4 段（含信头 + 落款） | 4–5 段（Intro + Overview + 2–3 Body） |
| **语气** | 三档：正式 / 半正式 / 非正式 | 单一客观语气 |
| **高频考题** | 投诉、申请、建议、道歉、邀请、感谢、求职 | 动态图、静态图、地图、流程、混合 |

## 二、三种语气判定与模板

### 1️⃣ 正式信（Formal Letter）

**适用场景**：
- 投诉信（向公司 / 机构）
- 求职信（向陌生雇主）
- 申请信（学校 / 政府 / 签证）
- 建议信（向机构 / 部门）

**信头 / 落款**：
- 信头：`Dear Sir or Madam,` / `Dear Mr. Smith,`
- 落款：`Yours faithfully,`（不知收信人姓名）/ `Yours sincerely,`（知道姓名）

**关键特征**：
- ❌ 不用 contractions（I'm / don't / can't）
- ❌ 不用 slang（"gonna"、"stuff"、"a lot of"）
- ✅ 用复杂结构与正式词汇
- ✅ 一段一段清晰分（寒暄 / 目的 / 主体 / 结尾）

### 2️⃣ 半正式信（Semi-formal Letter）

**适用场景**：
- 给不太熟的熟人（如朋友的同事、新邻居）
- 工作中的同事 / 上级
- 给学校老师的建议（已认识）

**信头 / 落款**：
- 信头：`Dear Mr. Brown,` / `Dear Ms. Smith,`
- 落款：`Yours sincerely,` / `Best regards,` / `Kind regards,`

**关键特征**：
- ⚠️ contractions 可少量使用（I'm / I'd / we'd）
- ⚠️ 避免 slang，但口语化副词 OK（"really"、"quite"、"fairly"）
- ✅ 比正式信略轻松，但依然保持礼貌

### 3️⃣ 非正式信（Informal Letter）

**适用场景**：
- 给朋友的信
- 给熟悉同事的信
- 偶尔给亲戚的建议信

**信头 / 落款**：
- 信头：`Dear Tom,` / `Hi Sarah,` / `Hey John,`
- 落款：`Best wishes,` / `Regards,` / `Take care,` / `Cheers,` + 名（不带姓）

**关键特征**：
- ✅ contractions 大量使用
- ✅ 可以用 slang（少量："a bit"、"sort of"、"loads of"）
- ✅ 语气接近口语但仍要清晰
- ✅ 可加 emoji / 表情符号（仅在 IELTS 模拟练习中；正式考试不建议）

## 三、六段式结构（所有 GT 书信通用）

```
段落 1：信头 + 寒暄 + 写信目的
段落 2：主体 1（题目要点 1 + 必要细节）
段落 3：主体 2（题目要点 2 + 必要细节）
段落 4：主体 3（题目要点 3，可选）+ 礼貌结尾
段落 5：落款
```

> 字数控制：150–180 词。**GT 书信宁短勿长**，超 200 词会被认为冗余。

## 四、常见题型模板

### ① 投诉信（Complaint）

**典型题目**：
> You recently bought a product online but found it defective. Write a letter to the company to complain. In your letter:
> - Describe the product and where you bought it
> - Explain what was wrong with it
> - Say what you want the company to do

**正式信模板**：

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am writing to express my strong dissatisfaction with a recent purchase from your online store. I would appreciate it if you could look into this matter promptly.
>
> On 15 March, I ordered a Model X electric kettle (order number #A12345) from your website, which arrived within the promised three-day delivery window. However, upon opening the package, I discovered that the kettle's lid was cracked and the heating element appeared to be faulty, as it failed to function during a test run.
>
> Given that the kettle is clearly unfit for its intended purpose, I would request either a full refund or a replacement unit sent at no additional cost. I have retained the original packaging and would be happy to return the defective item upon your instructions.
>
> I look forward to your swift response and a satisfactory resolution to this matter. Please do not hesitate to contact me should you require any further information.
>
> Yours faithfully,
> Li Jianghua

**关键点**：
- ✅ 开头直接表明写信目的（"I am writing to express..."）。
- ✅ 提供订单号 / 日期 / 具体缺陷 — 真实感强。
- ✅ 明确诉求（refund / replacement）。
- ✅ 礼貌结尾（"I look forward to..."）。
- ❌ 不骂人、不夸张、不带情绪（即便很愤怒）。

---

### ② 求职信（Job Application）

**典型题目**：
> You saw an advertisement for a part-time job at a local bookstore. Write a letter to apply. In your letter:
> - Explain why you are interested in the job
> - Describe your relevant experience
> - Mention when you can start

**正式信模板**：

> Dear Mr. Thompson,
>
> I am writing to apply for the part-time sales assistant position advertised in last week's City News. The opportunity to work at Bookmark Books aligns closely with my passion for literature and my desire to develop customer service experience.
>
> I am currently a first-year undergraduate at the University of Oxford, studying English Literature. Over the past two years, I have worked part-time at Waterstones during term breaks, where I honed my skills in book recommendation, stock management, and handling customer queries. Additionally, I volunteered at our university library, assisting with shelving and organising reading events.
>
> I am available to start immediately and can commit to at least 20 hours per week, including weekends. I would be grateful for the chance to discuss how my background could contribute to your store's welcoming atmosphere.
>
> Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to hearing from you.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Jianghua

**关键点**：
- ✅ 开头说明从何处得知职位。
- ✅ 工作经验 + 教育背景 + 与岗位的匹配。
- ✅ 可开始时间具体（"immediately"、"20 hours per week"）。
- ✅ 礼貌收尾 + 感谢。

---

### ③ 道歉信（Apology）

**典型题目**：
> A friend invited you to their party, but you could not attend because of a family emergency. Write a letter to apologize. In your letter:
> - Apologize for not attending
> - Explain what happened
> - Suggest a way to make it up to them

**非正式信模板**：

> Dear Sarah,
>
> I'm really sorry I missed your birthday party last Saturday. I feel terrible about letting you down, especially since you put so much effort into planning the evening.
>
> As you know, my father had a sudden health emergency that morning and needed to be rushed to hospital. I spent most of the day at the A&E with him, and by the time we got home, I was completely exhausted and didn't want to risk missing your call or message. I should have texted you earlier to let you know, and I'm sorry I didn't.
>
> To make up for it, I'd love to take you out for dinner at that new Italian restaurant you've been raving about, sometime next week—any day that suits you. Please let me know what works.
>
> Hope you had a wonderful party, and I promise I'll be at the next one!
>
> Best wishes,
> Jianghua

**关键点**：
- ✅ 开头直接道歉（"I'm really sorry..."）。
- ✅ 解释原因（真实可信、不过度戏剧化）。
- ✅ 补救方案（具体、可执行）。
- ✅ 结尾保持朋友间的轻松语气。

---

### ④ 建议信（Suggestion）

**典型题目**：
> Your friend is planning to move to your city for work. Write a letter giving advice. In your letter:
> - Recommend suitable neighborhoods
> - Suggest things to do in your city
> - Mention transport options

**半正式信模板**：

> Dear Mark,
>
> Thanks for letting me know about your upcoming move to Shanghai in October! I thought I'd write down some suggestions that might help you settle in quickly.
>
> Regarding accommodation, I'd strongly recommend the Xuhui or Jing'an districts, both of which are centrally located and well-connected to most business hubs. Xuhui tends to be more residential and family-friendly, while Jing'an offers a livelier nightlife and dining scene. Rents are higher than in the outer districts, but the time you save on commuting more than justifies the cost.
>
> As for leisure, Shanghai has plenty to offer beyond the standard tourist trail. I'd suggest exploring the French Concession's leafy lanes on weekends, joining a local cycling group along the Suzhou Creek, or sampling the city's famous xiaolongbao at Din Tai Fung. If you're into art, the Power Station of Art hosts excellent contemporary exhibitions.
>
> Public transport here is excellent—the metro is clean, punctual, and covers almost everywhere you'd want to go. I'd recommend getting a monthly transport card as soon as you arrive; it saves both time and money.
>
> Let me know your arrival date, and I'll make sure to be around to help you get settled in.
>
> Best regards,
> Jianghua

**关键点**：
- ✅ 三段分别对应题目三要点。
- ✅ 提供具体信息（地名、品牌名、线路）。
- ✅ 个人化语气（"I'd strongly recommend"）。
- ✅ 主动提供后续帮助（"Let me know your arrival date"）。

---

### ⑤ 邀请信（Invitation）

**典型题目**：
> You want to invite a foreign friend to visit your country. Write a letter:
> - Invite them and suggest when to come
> - Recommend places to visit
> - Offer to host them

**半正式信模板**：

> Dear Emma,
>
> I hope this letter finds you well. I'm writing to invite you to visit me in Hangzhou next spring—I think you'd find it a fascinating contrast to life in London!
>
> May or June would be the ideal time, as the weather is pleasantly warm and the West Lake area is at its most beautiful. You could stay with me at my apartment, which has a spare bedroom with a view of the canal.
>
> During your visit, I'd love to take you to the UNESCO-listed West Lake, where you can take a boat ride among the willow-lined shores. We'd also explore the ancient Lingyin Temple, stroll through the historic Hefang Street, and—if you enjoy hiking—climb to the top of Ling Shan Feng for panoramic views of the city.
>
> I genuinely believe you'd fall in love with Hangzhou's blend of natural beauty and rich history. Please let me know your thoughts, and we can start planning the details.
>
> Best wishes,
> Jianghua

## 五、评分要点（GT Task 1）

### TA（Task Achievement）

| Band | 描述 |
|---|---|
| **9** | 完全满足任务要求；语气始终得体；清晰呈现并图示关键要点。 |
| **8** | 充分覆盖所有要求；语气得体一致；清晰呈现关键要点。 |
| **7** | 覆盖任务要求；语气一致；呈现关键要点但偶有不充分展开。 |
| **6** | 回应任务要求；语气基本一致；要点齐全但偶有不相关 / 不准确。 |
| **5** | 一般性回应；语气偶有不一致；要点部分覆盖但不够充分。 |

**TA 关键扣分点**：
- ❌ **语气错配**：投诉信用 slang / 朋友信用"I demand..." → 卡 6。
- ❌ **目的不清**：开头不说明"为什么写这封信"。
- ❌ **要点缺失**：三个要点只写两个。
- ❌ **诉求不清**：投诉信无明确"我想要什么"。
- ❌ **落款缺失**：GT 书信必须有落款（"Yours sincerely, Name"）。

### CC（Coherence & Cohesion）

- ❌ **不分段**：整封信一段 → CC 直接卡 5。
- ❌ **寒暄 + 主体混合**：开头寒暄太长，挤压主体。
- ✅ **每段一个要点**，段间衔接自然。

### LR（Lexical Resource）

- ✅ 正式信用正式词汇（"I would appreciate..."、"I look forward to..."）。
- ❌ 重复用词（连续三段都用 "I am writing to..." → 模板感过重）。

### GRA（Grammatical Range & Accuracy）

- ❌ **残句 / 主谓不一致**：与 Academic 同。
- ✅ **正式信不用 contractions**：I'm / don't 出现过多会被认为 GRA 略扣（语气不符）。

## 六、GT 书信自检清单（生成 7 分范文后）

- [ ] **TA**：开头明确写信目的 + 三要点齐全 + 语气一致 + 有落款？

- [ ] **LR**：正式信无 slang + 用正式词汇 + 不重复模板？
- [ ] **GRA**：无残句 + 时态一致 + 正式信无过多 contractions？
- [ ] **字数**：150–180 词？
- [ ] **格式**：信头 + 落款正确？

## 七、常见错误与避坑

| 错误 | 后果 | 修正 |
|---|---|---|
| 写信不署名 | TA 扣分 | 落款必须有名（"Yours sincerely, Jianghua"） |
| 投诉信用 "I demand..." | 语气过于强硬 | 用 "I would appreciate if you could..." |
| 朋友信用 "Dear Sir or Madam" | 语气错配 | 用 "Dear Tom," |
| 寒暄写 3 句话 | 挤压主体 | 寒暄 1–2 句就转主题 |
| 三要点挤在一段 | CC 卡 5 | 每要点独立成段 |
| 求职信只写"我对这个工作感兴趣" | TA 不充分 | 加经验 + 时间 + 个人优势 |
| 道歉信不补救 | TA 不完整 | 必须提补救方案 |

## 八、2026 年 GT 书信新趋势

1. **情境更具体**：近年题目常给出具体场景（订单号、航班号、课程名），学生需要在信中引用这些细节，否则 TA 卡 6。
2. **复合型题目**：越来越多题目包含 3+ 要点（如"道歉 + 解释 + 补救 + 后续建议"），需要合理分段。
3. **语气区分更严格**：2024 年起，formal / informal 错配被明确扣分，AI 模拟评估中此点命中率最高。
4. **机考占主导**：GT 多数考点已转机考，需打字熟练（建议 ≥ 25 wpm）。
