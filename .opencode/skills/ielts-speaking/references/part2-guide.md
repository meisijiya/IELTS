# Part 2 话题卡片（Long Turn / Cue Card）完整指南

> 适用：雅思口语 Part 2（2 分钟陈述 + 1 分钟准备 + 1–2 个追问，共 3–4 分钟）。
> 数据来源：IDP Part 2 官方指南、IELTS.org、IDP Speaking Guide 2025 PDF、IELTS Simon、IELTS Liz、Magoosh、Test Prep Istanbul。

---

## 一、格式与时长

| 项目 | 细节 |
|---|---|
| 总时长 | 3–4 分钟（含准备） |
| 准备时间 | **1 分钟**（考官提供铅笔 + 纸） |
| 陈述时间 | **1–2 分钟**（考官会在 2 分钟时打断） |
| 追问 | 1–2 个问题（与话题相关） |
| 卡片内容 | 1 个主话题 + 3 个 bullet + 1 个 "explain why/how" bullet |

**官方 IDP 卡片标准格式**：

> "Describe a [person/place/object/event/experience] [you… / that… / you have…].
> You should say:
> - [first bullet — usually a Wh- question]
> - [second bullet]
> - [third bullet]
> and explain [why / how you feel / what difference it made].
> You will have to talk about the topic for 1 to 2 minutes.
> You have one minute to think about what you are going to say.
> You can make some notes to help you if you wish."

---

## 二、1 分钟准备的「黄金 60 秒」

### 不该做的事

- 写完整句子 ❌
- 写想说的所有点 ❌
- 拼写错误不纠 ❌

### 该做的事

- 写**关键词**（每个 bullet 1–2 个关键词）
- 用**缩写 / 符号 / 箭头**
- 跳过冠词 / 介词 / 助动词
- 留**最后 15 秒**在脑里串一遍

### PARTS 框架（Test Prep Istanbul）

| 字母 | 含义 | 1 分钟内做的事 |
|---|---|---|
| **P** | Purpose | 看清卡片是描述人 / 地 / 物 / 事 / 活动 |
| **A** | Aim for specifics | 立刻在脑中挑一个真实细节 |
| **R** | Rough ideas | 4 个 bullet 各写 1–2 个关键词 |
| **T** | Timing | 心算 20–25 秒 / bullet |
| **S** | Signals | 标注 2–3 个衔接词用到 |

### 4W + 感受 系统（IELTS Tutor）

| 列 | 写什么 |
|---|---|
| **Who / What** | 人物 / 物品 |
| **When** | 时间 |
| **Where** | 地点 |
| **What happened** | 事件 |
| **+ Feelings / Result** | 感受 + 结果（最重要） |

### IDP 官方示范笔记（描述「a piano」）

| Where | How long | What | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Music store, Dublin | 33 yrs | to play | love music |
| Ireland | after university | entertain | nice furniture |
| | | teach | only item from home |

> Source: IDP "How to perform at your best in Part 2" https://ielts.idp.com/prepare/article-ielts-speaking-test-long-turn-part-2

## 三、答题结构（2 分钟分期）

| 阶段 | 时长 | 任务 |
|---|---|---|
| **开场白** | 15–20 秒 | 切入主题，说明要讲什么 |
| **主体 1** | ~30 秒 | 覆盖 bullet 1 |
| **主体 2** | ~30 秒 | 覆盖 bullet 2 |
| **主体 3 + explain** | ~40 秒 | 覆盖 bullet 3 + explain why |
| **收尾** | 10–15 秒 | 概括感受 / 总结 |

**总字数**：约 200–250 词（按 **110–130 wpm 实际口语语速**含自然停顿约 1:50–2:05）。**纯朗读 130–150 wpm 计算时约 1:20–1:55**——所以好的 Part 2 答案会用 natural pauses / fillers / self-corrections 拉长到接近 2 分钟。

## 四、六种故事框架

### 1. **Chronological（按时间）**

适合：事件 / 经历

> "To start with... / At the beginning... / After that... / Eventually... / Looking back..."

### 2. **Thematic（按主题）**

适合：人物 / 地点 / 物品

> "One thing I really like about ... / Another reason ... / What stands out most is..."

### 3. **STAR（Situation-Task-Action-Result）**

适合：成就 / 决策 / 技能

> "I remember a time when... / The main challenge was... / In the end, what I did was... / As a result, ..."

### 4. **Past-Present-Future（过去-现在-未来）**

适合：仍在生活中的地点 / 活动

> "When I first started... / Nowadays... / In the future, I hope to..."

### 5. **2-Minute Blueprint（万能）**

> **Opening**（主题句）→ **Background**（背景）→ **Story**（故事）→ **Feelings+Result**（感受 + 结果）→ **Extra**（补充，若有时间）

*（来源：IELTS 通用教学模板，非课程原创）*

### 6. **汉堡结构（Burger Structure）—— 6 层综合框架**

> **来源**：课程核心框架（见 part1-guide.md 第三节"汉堡结构"详解）。
> **什么时候用**：Part 2 不知道该用什么框架时，**默认用汉堡**——它把"答案 + 词汇 + 语法 + 细节 + 流畅 + 逻辑"6 维打包，覆盖所有评分项。

| 层 | Part 2 应用（描述"一位对我重要的人"为例） |
|---|---|
| 1 面包（直接答） | "I'd like to talk about my grandfather, who passed away five years ago." |
| 2 肉（词汇） | 用 `compassionate / insightful / hardworking` 等 less common 词 |
| 3 生菜（语法） | "He was a man **who believed that** patience is the foundation of wisdom."（定语从句） |
| 4 芝士（细节） | 补充 when / where / 具体故事片段 |
| 5 酱料（流畅） | "well, you know, actually" 等 fillers 自然填入 |
| 6 竹签（逻辑） | "because / which is why / so that" 串起整个故事 |

**与五种框架的关系**：
- 框架 1–5 是**侧重某一维度**的（时间 / 主题 / STAR / 过去-现在-未来 / 万能）
- 框架 6（汉堡）是**横切所有维度**的综合框架
- 实操建议：**先用 4W 笔记填素材（part2-guide 第二节），再用汉堡 6 层串成 2 分钟**

汉堡结构完整指南（6 层定义 + 决策树 + Part 1/2/3 应用范例 + 同题三框架对比 + 训练动作 + 5 个常见陷阱）见 `references/burger-structure.md`。

## 五、必用过渡词（signposting）

让 2 分钟叙述有清晰结构：

| 阶段 | 过渡词 |
|---|---|
| **开场** | "I'm going to talk about..." / "I'd like to describe..." / "The person / place / thing I'd like to talk about is..." |
| **第一点** | "First of all, ..." / "To start with, ..." / "I'd like to begin with..." |
| **第二点** | "As for [topic], ..." / "Another thing I remember is..." / "What I also want to mention is..." |
| **第三点** | "The reason I mention this is..." / "What struck me most was..." / "Most importantly, ..." |
| **解释 why** | "The reason this matters to me is..." / "Looking back, I think..." |
| **收尾** | "So that's why..." / "All in all, ..." / "It's an experience I won't forget." |

## 六、6 大话题类别（必背）

| 类别 | cue card 例子 |
|---|---|
| **人** | someone who taught you something; a person you admire; a friend from childhood; a family member |
| **地** | a place you want to visit; a quiet place; a city you visited; a place where you study |
| **物** | something you own that is important; a gift; a piece of technology; an item of clothing |
| **事** | a happy childhood memory; a time you helped someone; a difficult decision; a memorable journey |
| **活动** | a hobby; a sport; something new you learned; an activity to stay healthy |
| **抽象** | a goal you achieved; a positive change; a rule you disagreed with; an ambition |

> Source: IELTS Simon 6 类话题 https://ielts-simon.study/ ; Lingo Copilot 2026 话题 https://speaking.lingo-copilot.com/blog/ielts-speaking-part-2-topics-2026

## 七、必覆盖 4 个 bullet（漏 1 个就掉 1 档）

**考官评分最关注的**：是否完整覆盖所有 bullet。

- **Bullet 1**：通常是 Wh- 问题（who/what/when/where）。
- **Bullet 2**：通常是扩展维度（how often / what it looks like）。
- **Bullet 3**：通常是另一个扩展维度（what you do / how you use it）。
- **Bullet 4**（"and explain..."）：**最重要**——讲感受、原因、影响。

> 💡 **Be Real 提示**：Part 2 故事必须是真实经历——背稿痕迹会被考官综合判断识别并压分（Pronunciation / Interaction 两项扣分）。详见 `references/part1-guide.md` 第九节末尾 Be Real 哲学。

**最后一 bullet 永远要给最多时间**。理由：考官在判断 LR / GRA 时，往往最后听到的论述最完整。

## 八、考官最常扣分的 5 个错误

| 错误 | 后果 | 修正 |
|---|---|---|
| **不到 2 分钟就停** | FC 扣分 | 心里默数 8–10 句，确保 2 分钟 |
| **漏 1 个 bullet** | TR/LR 扣分 | 准备时 4 列全填 |
| **读笔记（flat 语调）** | P 扣分 | 只看关键词，不看句子 |
| **太抽象（"a good experience"）** | GRA 扣分 | 加具体细节（数字 / 颜色 / 名字） |
| **背稿痕迹** | 考官综合判断能识别（参见 IDP examiner tips / British Council 公开口径）→ 综合扣分 | 自然停顿、self-correct |

> Source: IDP examiner tips, E2 IELTS Band 9, Lee et al. 2021 VCS 研究（ielts.org/cdn/Research/development-of-ielts-video-call-speaking-test-lee-et-al-2021.pdf）。

## 九、Part 2 必须学会的 5 个高分表达

| 类别 | 表达 |
|---|---|
| **强调强烈感受** | "What really struck me was..." / "The most memorable thing was..." |
| **讲故事** | "I'll never forget the moment when..." / "It was a turning point in my life." |
| **解释原因** | "The reason I ... is that..." / "This is because..." / "I think it's down to..." |
| **描述细节** | "It was a bright sunny day..." / "I could still remember the smell of..." |
| **总结** | "On the whole, ..." / "Looking back, ..." / "All in all, ..." |

## 十、Part 2 自检清单（生成答案后）

- [ ] **覆盖 4 个 bullet**：每个 bullet 都展开 ≥ 1 句？
- [ ] **时长**：是不是 200–250 词？约 2 分钟？
- [ ] **真实经历**：素材是否来自用户提供的真实故事？
- [ ] **结尾有感受**：最后 10 秒讲的是感受，不是 "It was good"？
- [ ] **有具体细节**：1 个数字 / 1 个名字 / 1 个颜色 / 1 个地点？
- [ ] **过渡自然**：是否使用了 signposting？
- [ ] **句式多样**：简单句 + 复合句混合？
- [ ] **目标分数对应**：是否反映了目标分数段（5.5 / 6.5 / 7）的语言特征？

## 十二、Part 2 范例（学习参考用）

**题目**：Describe a piece of technology you often use (but not your phone). You should say: what it is / how long you have used it / what you use it for / and explain why it is important to you.

> "I'd like to talk about my laptop, which I've been using for almost four years now. I bought it during my first year of university, and it has been an essential companion for both my studies and personal life.
>
> Actually, I use it for almost everything. Every day I take notes during lectures, write essays, do research, and even watch movies in my dorm room. The screen is large enough for me to read articles comfortably, and the battery lasts for about eight hours, which is perfect for a long day on campus.
>
> The reason it matters so much to me is that, without it, I probably wouldn't have passed half of my assignments. Last year, I had to finish a 5,000-word essay in two days, and my laptop was the only tool that helped me meet the deadline. I remember staying up until 3 a.m. with the screen glowing in the dark — it was exhausting, but I got it done.
>
> Looking back, I think a laptop is more than just a tool for me. It's become part of how I study, work, and even relax."

**分析**：
- 覆盖 4 个 bullet（what / how long / what for / why important）。
- 总词数 ~210，按 110 wpm（含停顿）约 1:54，加上自然停顿接近 2 分钟陈述。
- 使用 signposting（"I'd like to talk about..."、"Actually"、"The reason it matters is..."、"Looking back"）。
- 真实细节（"8 hours"、"5,000-word essay"、"3 a.m."）。
- 结尾是感受（"part of how I study"），不是空洞总结。
- 适合 7 分档：复杂结构（which I've been using / without it / that helped me）控制好。
