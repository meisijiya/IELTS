# 汉堡结构（Burger Structure）权威指南

> **定位**：本文件是"汉堡结构"在 ielts-speaking skill 中的**主要权威入口**。其他文件（part1-guide / part2-guide / part3-guide）讲汉堡时，统一交叉引用到本文件。
>
> **什么是汉堡结构**：雅思口语答题的**6 层综合思考框架**——把"答案 + 词汇 + 语法 + 细节 + 流畅 + 逻辑"6 维度打包成一个答题模板，**显式对应 4 项评分标准**。
>
> **来源**：课程核心方法。SEER / STAR / OREO 是单维度框架（侧重时间 / 故事 / 论证），汉堡是**横切所有评分项的综合框架**——学生不知道该用什么框架时，**默认用汉堡**。

---

## 第一节 6 层定义（与 part1-guide.md 第三节一致，本节为权威）

| 层 | 名称 | 对应评分项 | 作用 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 面包（Bread） | FC | 直接回答问题（1 句） |
| 2 | 肉（Meat） | LR | 用替换词 / less common 词汇 |
| 3 | 生菜（Lettuce） | GRA | 引入复合句 / 复杂语法 |
| 4 | 芝士（Cheese） | FC + LR | 补充 where / who / how / when 等细节 |
| 5 | 酱料（Sauce） | FC | 用填充词让回答自然流畅 |
| 6 | 竹签（贯穿） | Coherence | 用 because / so / which is why 串起逻辑 |

**为什么叫"汉堡"**：因为 burger（牛肉汉堡）比 hamburger（猪肉汉堡）短——名字越短越好记。

---

## 第二节 决策树：汉堡 vs SEER vs OREO vs STAR

> **什么时候用什么框架**——这是学生最容易混乱的地方。

```
Part 1 短题（15 秒，Yes/No / 偏好 / 频率）
  → SEER（Short → Extend → Example → Reflect）
  → 汉堡太重，SEER 更快

Part 1 长题（30+ 秒，why / how / 评价 / describe）
  → 汉堡结构
  → SEER 撑不起 30 秒

Part 2 任何题目（2 分钟陈述）
  → 汉堡结构（默认）
  → 如果是事件/经历类，可以用 STAR 当骨架 + 汉堡当调料
  → 如果是人物/地点/物品类，可以用 thematic 当骨架 + 汉堡当调料

Part 3 观点题（opinion / 评价 / 应该不应该）
  → OREO（Opinion → Reason → Example → Other）
  → 但 OREO 是 4 步，缺"汉堡 5–6 层"的"流畅 + 逻辑"细节
  → 想要 7+ 分：用 OREO 当骨架 + 汉堡 5–6 层填充

Part 3 因果 / 对比 / 推测题
  → 直接用汉堡 6 层（不需要 OREO）

Part 3 让步讨论 / Problem-Solution 题
  → 也用汉堡作底（两层框架叠加，比单用 OREO 更稳）
```

**一句话总结**：
- **汉堡 = 万能默认**（不知道用什么就用它）
- **SEER = Part 1 短题专用**
- **OREO = Part 3 观点题专用**
- **STAR = Part 2 事件类专用**

## 第三节 三大应用场景范例

### 3.1 Part 1 应用（题目："Where is your hometown?"）

❌ 只有 SEER 的 5.5 答案：
> I live in Shenzhen. It's a big city.

✅ 用汉堡的 7 分答案：
> I live in Shenzhen, **which is one of the most modern cities I have ever seen.** ①**（肉：万能句型）** **The reason I love it is that** everything is nearby — my office, the subway, even the gym. ②**（竹签：because 解释）** I usually meet my neighbors in the morning when we walk our dogs, **you know what I'm saying?** ③**（酱料：句尾填充）** It's basically designed for convenience. ④**（面包：直接收尾）**

**汉堡 6 层检查**：
- ① 肉层：用了万能句型
- ② 竹签层：The reason... is that 解释因果
- ③ 酱料层：句尾填充
- ④ 面包层：直接收尾
- 还缺：生菜（复合句 which...）+ 芝士（具体细节 subway / gym / neighbors）

---

### 3.2 Part 2 应用（题目：Describe a person who influenced you）

❌ 只有 STAR 的 5.5 答案：
> My grandfather influenced me. When I was young, I stayed with him. He taught me patience. He died five years ago.

✅ 用汉堡的 7 分答案（STAR 当骨架 + 汉堡当调料）：
> **I'd like to talk about my grandfather, who passed away five years ago.** ①**（面包 + 肉：直接答 + 高级词汇）**
>
> **He was a man who believed that** patience is the foundation of wisdom. ②**（生菜：定语从句）** I used to spend every summer with him on his small farm in Hunan, **where** he would tell me stories about his life during the war. ③**（芝士：where + 具体场景）**
>
> **The reason his influence stayed with me is that**, **well**, he never lectured me — he showed me through his actions. **Actually**, I still use his advice every time I face a tough decision. ④**（酱料：well / actually + 解释）** **All in all**, he's the reason I'm who I am today. ⑤**（竹签 + 收尾：All in all）**

**汉堡 6 层检查**：
- ✅ 面包：I'd like to talk about...
- ✅ 肉：passed away / wisdom / influence
- ✅ 生菜：who believed that / where he would tell me
- ✅ 芝士：Hunan / summer / war stories
- ✅ 酱料：well / actually
- ✅ 竹签：The reason... is that + All in all

---

### 3.3 Part 3 应用（题目："Do you think hobbies are important?"）

❌ 只有 OREO 的 5.5 答案：
> Yes, hobbies are important. They help us relax. They also help us make friends. For example, I play badminton.

✅ 用汉堡的 7 分答案（OREO 当骨架 + 汉堡填充）：
> **Yes, I think hobbies are genuinely important** for everyone. ①**（面包 + 肉：opinion）**
>
> **The main reason is that**, **in my country**, most people work long hours and face constant stress — hobbies offer a mental escape **that is essential** for emotional balance. ②**（生菜 + 芝士：抽象化 + 复合句）**
>
> **For example**, in cities like Shenzhen, **you see** badminton courts, hiking groups, and music classes **packed** every weekend, **which suggests** that people actively seek out these outlets. ③**（竹签：举例 + which suggests 因果）**
>
> **Having said that**, hobbies **can become** a waste of time if they're not balanced, **but on balance**, I believe they're crucial for a healthy life. ④**（酱料：Having said that + on balance 让步）**

**汉堡 6 层检查**：
- ✅ 面包 + 肉：Yes, I think hobbies are genuinely important
- ✅ 生菜：that is essential / that people actively seek
- ✅ 芝士：Shenzhen / badminton / hiking / music
- ✅ 酱料：in my country / Having said that
- ✅ 竹签：The main reason is that / which suggests / but on balance

## 第四节 同一话题三种框架对比

> **目的**：让学生直观看到"汉堡 vs STAR vs OREO"在同一道题上答出来什么样。

**题目**：Describe a journey that was important to you. / Do you think travel is important for young people?

### 4.1 用汉堡（综合）

> I'd like to talk about my trip to Yunnan last summer, which was **one of the most meaningful journeys** I have ever taken. **The reason** it mattered is that I went with my best friend from high school, and we hadn't seen each other for years. **Actually**, we just talked and walked and ate, no schedule. **Well**, what really struck me was that the slow pace of life there made me realize how rushed my daily life is. **All in all**, that trip changed how I think about time and friendship.

**特征**：6 层全部覆盖；综合自然；适合 7+ 分。

### 4.2 用 STAR（事件类骨架）

> **Situation**: Last summer, I went to Yunnan. **Task**: My friend and I wanted to reconnect. **Action**: We traveled together for ten days. **Result**: We became close again, and I realized the value of slowing down.

**特征**：结构清晰但单调；适合 Part 2 应急；6.5 分水准。

### 4.3 用 OREO（Part 3 论证）

> **Opinion**: Yes, travel is important. **Reason**: It exposes young people to different cultures. **Example**: For example, Chinese students who study abroad often return with broader perspectives. **Other side**: Having said that, travel can be expensive.

**特征**：Part 3 必备；缺"汉堡 5–6 层"的流畅与逻辑细节；6.5 分水准。

### 4.4 三个对比

| 框架 | 答题骨架 | Part 2 适合 | Part 3 适合 | 综合得分潜力 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **汉堡** | 6 层横切评分项 | ✅ 默认 | ✅（+ OREO） | 7+ 潜力（取决于执行质量） |
| **STAR** | 事件 4 步 | ✅ 应急 | ❌ | 6.5 |
| **OREO** | 论证 4 步 | ❌ | ✅ 默认 | 6.5 |
| **SEER** | 短答 4 步 | ❌ | ❌ | Part 1 短题 |

## 第五节 训练动作（30 分钟掌握汉堡）

### 5.1 第一周：记住 6 层 + 套 3 个话题

每天 1 个 Part 2 cue card，用汉堡 6 层串成 2 分钟：
1. **写作版**（不计时间）：写 6 行笔记，每行对应一层
2. **录音版**（2 分钟）：看笔记录音，回听检查 6 层是否都覆盖
3. **重复 1 次**：换另一个 cue card

### 5.2 第二周：对比 SEER / OREO

同一话题用汉堡和 SEER（或 OREO）各答一遍，比较：
- 哪个更长？
- 哪个更自然？
- 哪个评分维度被忽略？

形成"什么时候用哪个框架"的判断力。

### 5.3 考前一周：默认汉堡

任何话题都先想"汉堡 6 层"，再用 SEER / OREO 简化。**目标：5 秒内决定用哪个框架**。

## 第六节 常见 5 个陷阱

| 陷阱 | 反例 | 修正 |
|---|---|---|
| **只用了面包 + 芝士**（直接答 + 细节） | "I live in Shenzhen. There are many subways." | 必须有"肉"（高级词）+ "生菜"（复合句） |
| **竹签断了**（没有 because / which is why） | "I live in Shenzhen. I work there. I like it." | 加 "because my company is there" |
| **酱料过度**（每句都有 well / actually） | "Well, I live in Shenzhen. Actually, I like it. Actually, it's modern." | 酱料全篇 1–2 次就够 |
| **汉堡太厚**（Part 1 短题硬套 6 层） | Part 1 "Do you like coffee?" 答 30 秒 | Part 1 短题用 SEER 即可 |
| **肉 = 生词堆砌** | "I live in Shenzhen, an ultra-modern megalopolis..." | 肉 = 替换词（高频词升级），不是偏僻词 |

## 附：1 分钟极简版（贴在墙上的版本）

```
🎤 汉堡 6 层
1 面包：直接答（1 句）
2 肉：  高级词（替换 very/good/happy）
3 生菜：复合句（which / because / although）
4 芝士：6W 细节（who/where/when/why/how/what）
5 酱料：1-2 个填充词（well / actually / you know）
6 竹签：because / which is why / so 串逻辑
```

任何话题——不知道用什么框架——**默认用汉堡**。框架只是脚手架，决定分数的还是具体词汇、语法、流畅度的执行质量。
