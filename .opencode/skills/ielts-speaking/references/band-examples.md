### Band 5 范例：Tina（Vietnam）— Part 2

**题目**：Describe a hobby you enjoy. You should say: what it is / when and where you do it / how long you have been doing it / and explain why you enjoy it.

**Tina 的回答**（节选）：

> "I like playing badminton. I play badminton with my friends every weekend. We usually play in the park near my house. I started playing badminton about two years ago. I enjoy it because it's fun and I can spend time with my friends."

**考官评语**：

> "Tina maintains fluency throughout her response, and she uses a range of basic cohesive devices. However, she relies on simple structures, and her vocabulary is limited to high-frequency items. She uses some basic sentence forms with reasonable accuracy but rarely attempts complex grammar. Her pronunciation is generally clear, although some words are mispronounced."

**→ Band 5 特征**：

- ✅ 流畅（但简单）
- ✅ 基础衔接词
- ❌ 简单结构为主
- ❌ 词汇有限
- ❌ 复杂语法少
- ❌ 个别词发音不清

---

### Band 6 范例：Stephen（China）— Part 3

**题目**：Do you think hobbies are important?

**Stephen 的回答**（节选）：

> "Yes, I think hobbies are important for everyone. For example, when I'm stressed from work, playing badminton helps me relax. Hobbies also give us a chance to meet new people and make friends. Some people might say hobbies are a waste of time, but I think they are necessary for a healthy life."

**考官评语**：

> "Stephen speaks at length and uses a range of cohesive features. His vocabulary is sufficient to discuss the topic, and he attempts to use some less common items. However, his grammar becomes less accurate when he uses complex structures, and he occasionally relies on memorised phrases."

**→ Band 6 特征**：

- ✅ 答够长，cohesive 较自然
- ✅ 词汇够用
- ✅ 尝试用 less common items
- ❌ 复杂结构准确率下降
- ❌ 偶尔依赖背过的短语（考官能识别）

---

### Band 6.5 范例：Michal（Poland）— Part 2

**题目**：Describe a person who has influenced you.

**Michal 的回答**（节选）：

> "The person who has had the biggest influence on my life is my grandfather. He was a very kind and hardworking man who taught me the value of patience. I used to spend every summer with him on his small farm, and he would tell me stories about his life during the war. He passed away five years ago, but I still think about the lessons he taught me every day."

**考官评语**：

> "Michal speaks fluently and at length, with clear organisation and a good range of vocabulary. He uses some complex structures accurately, and his pronunciation is clear. He sometimes hesitates and self-corrects, which affects fluency slightly. Overall, his performance falls between band 6 and band 7."

**→ Band 6.5 特征**：

- ✅ 流利 + 自然长度
- ✅ 组织清晰
- ✅ 词汇有一定范围
- ✅ 复杂结构基本准确
- ❌ 偶尔停顿 + 自我修正

## 二、6/7/8 分对照范例（同一 Part 1 题目）

### 题目：Do you like your hometown?

#### 5 分答案

> "No, I don't like my hometown. It's very small. There's no entertainment. People are boring. I want to leave."

**特征**：
- 答得过短（不展开）
- 重复"I'm bored" 类肤浅表达
- 句式单一
- 没有具体细节

#### 6 分答案

> "No, not really. My hometown is quite small and there's not much to do there. I do miss my family and friends, but in terms of entertainment, the city is much better. I think young people in my hometown often leave to find jobs."

**特征**：
- 答够长，有对比
- 基本词汇（small, better, jobs）
- 简单/复合句混合
- 有一般化（"young people..."）

#### 7 分答案

> "Honestly, I have mixed feelings about my hometown. On the one hand, I miss the sense of community — everyone knows everyone, and there's a real feeling of belonging. On the other hand, the job market is limited, which is why most of my old classmates have moved to bigger cities. So I'd say I appreciate where I come from, but I don't think I could go back to living there permanently."

**特征**：
- 双面立场（"mixed feelings"）
- 抽象词汇（"sense of community", "belonging"）
- 让步（"On the other hand"）
- 真实细节（"old classmates"）
- 复杂结构（"which is why..."）

#### 8 分答案

> "To be honest, my relationship with my hometown is complicated. I grew up in a small coastal town, and while I have fond memories of long summers on the beach and tight-knit neighbours, I also felt quite constrained as a teenager — there was very little cultural diversity, and I often felt like I had to leave in order to grow. Now that I live elsewhere, I appreciate the slower pace of life, but I don't think I could move back. In a way, my hometown represents both where I come from and why I left."

**特征**：
- 复杂叙事（"complicated relationship"）
- 多层结构（"while.../ there was.../ and I..."）
- 抽象表达（"cultural diversity", "represents both where I come from and why I left"）
- 自我反思（"In a way..."）
- 几乎没有错误

## 三、Part 2 不同分数段对比（同一题目）

### 题目：Describe a book that has influenced you.

#### 6 分答案（节选）

> "I want to talk about a book called 'The Little Prince'. I read it when I was a child. The story is about a prince who travels to different planets. I like it because it's very interesting and teaches us about love and life. I think everyone should read this book."

**特征**：
- 基本结构和信息齐全
- 但表达简单（"very interesting", "love and life"）
- 缺乏具体细节
- 没有个人观点深度

#### 7 分答案（节选）

> "The book that has had the deepest impact on me is 'The Little Prince' by Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, which I first read when I was about twelve. What struck me most was the idea that 'what is essential is invisible to the eye' — a line that has stayed with me ever since. It made me realise that the most meaningful things in life — relationships, kindness, honesty — are not the things you can see or buy. I still re-read it every few years, and each time I discover something new."

**特征**：
- 抽象表达（"deepest impact", "essential is invisible to the eye"）
- 真实引用 + 解释
- 复杂结构（"which I first read"）
- 多次回顾（"each time"）

#### 8 分答案（节选）

> "I'd like to talk about a book that fundamentally changed the way I think about adulthood — 'The Little Prince'. I first encountered it when I was eleven, far too young, I suspect, to understand its full weight. The passage that has stayed with me ever since is the fox's secret: that 'what is essential is invisible to the eye' — a deceptively simple line that, on reflection, captures something genuinely profound about how we form bonds with people and places. Rereading it as an adult, I find it almost unbearably poignant. The prince's childlike sincerity exposes the rather absurd rituals we adults construct around status and productivity. In a way, the book has become a quiet benchmark for me — a reminder of what's worth holding on to."

**特征**：
- 三层结构（背景 + 引用 + 反思）
- 复杂抽象（"childlike sincerity exposes the absurd rituals we construct"）
- 高级句式（"far too young, I suspect, to understand"）
- 几乎无错误

## 四、Part 3 不同分数段对比（同一题目）

### 题目：Do you think reading books is still important in the digital age?

#### 6 分答案

> "Yes, I think reading books is important. Even though we have many digital devices, books are still valuable. For example, when you read a book, you can focus better than on a phone. Books also have a lot of knowledge that you can't find on the internet. So I think books will always be important."

**特征**：
- 立场明确 + 简单论证
- 基本对比（books vs digital）
- 表述简单（"a lot of knowledge", "always important"）

#### 7 分答案

> "Absolutely, I think books remain important, although the way we read has changed. The tactile experience of holding a physical book, turning pages, and not being distracted by notifications creates a different kind of focus — what some researchers call 'deep reading'. While audiobooks and e-readers have made literature more accessible, studies suggest that comprehension and retention tend to be higher with traditional print. So I would say books aren't dying; they're just evolving."

**特征**：
- Abstract 词汇（"tactile", "deep reading", "comprehension and retention"）
- 让步（"while audiobooks..."）
- 引用研究（"some researchers", "studies suggest"）
- 结尾有立场（"aren't dying, just evolving"）

#### 8 分答案

> "I would argue that books are more important than ever, precisely because the digital age has fundamentally altered how we process information. The rise of social media and short-form content has, in many ways, shortened our attention spans — and reading, particularly long-form reading, is one of the few activities that actively counteracts this trend. Beyond mere content delivery, books offer something that algorithm-driven media cannot: sustained argument, nuance, and the discipline of following a single line of thought over hundreds of pages. The irony is that the more digitally saturated our lives become, the more valuable the slow, deliberate engagement of reading becomes. So I'd say books aren't competing with technology — they're providing something it can't."

**特征**：
- 多层论证（"one of the few activities that..."）
- 复杂结构（"precisely because..."、"Beyond..."、"The irony is..."）
- 抽象表达（"sustained argument", "nuance", "irony"）
- 强立场（"aren't competing... they're providing"）

## 五、中国学生最容易踩的 5 个话术坑

### 1. 每题都用 "I think..."

**6 分**："I think hobbies are important. I think they help us relax. I think we need hobbies."
**7 分**："Hobbies are important, in my view. They help us relax, and I believe we all need them."

### 2. 不会 paraphrase

**6 分**："Many people like reading books. Reading is good."
**7 分**："A great number of people are drawn to reading. It's a fantastic way to unwind."

### 3. 全程简单句

**6 分**："I like cooking. I cook every day. My mother taught me. The food is good."
**7 分**："I really enjoy cooking, which I do on a daily basis. My mother, who is an excellent chef, taught me the basics, and now I find it quite therapeutic."

### 4. 自造中式英语

**6 分**："I very like this book." / "This book is very good for me."
**7 分**："I'm really into this book." / "This book really resonates with me."

### 5. 背过的模板

**6 分**："With the development of society, more and more people..."
**7 分**："In contemporary society, an increasing number of people..."

## 六、生成模式自检清单（不同分数段）

### 5 分答案自检

- [ ] 答够长度（Part 1 每题 2–3 句）
- [ ] 题目关键词都有回应
- [ ] 没有机械重复考官问题
- [ ] 至少 1 个具体细节（数字 / 名字 / 地点）
- [ ] 没有完全跑题

### 6.5 分答案自检

- [ ] 5 分自检全部
- [ ] 同义替换 ≥ 30%
- [ ] 至少 1 个复合句
- [ ] 至少 1 个 discourse marker
- [ ] 有 1 个一般化（"people in my country"）

### 7 分答案自检

- [ ] 6.5 分自检全部
- [ ] 复杂结构 ≥ 3 种（定语 / 条件 / 让步 / 被动）
- [ ] 至少 1 个 less common 搭配
- [ ] 至少 1 个 hedging 但立场明确
- [ ] 结尾有概括 / 立场 / 升华

### 8 分答案自检

- [ ] 7 分自检全部
- [ ] 抽象论证 ≥ 2 步
- [ ] 多个让步 / 对比
- [ ] 几乎无错误
- [ ] 段落逻辑链清晰

## 七、From 5.5 to 7 in 12 Weeks: 高频动作清单

| 阶段 | 周数 | 核心动作 |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1（5.5 → 6）** | Week 1–4 | 1. 每天 1 个 Part 1 录音 30 秒；2. 学会 20 个 discourse markers；3. 答够长度（每题 +1 句） |
| **Phase 2（6 → 6.5）** | Week 5–8 | 1. 每天 1 个 Part 2 录音 2 分钟；2. 学会 20 个搭配 + 20 个 less common 词；3. 引入 1 个复杂结构 / 句 |
| **Phase 3（6.5 → 7）** | Week 9–12 | 1. 每天 1 个 Part 3 录音 60 秒；2. 强化 hedging + 抽象化；3. 引入 1 个让步 + 1 个对比组合 |

## 八、Diagnostic cheat sheet（诊断模式用）

| 现象 | 卡分档 | 升档动作 |
|---|---|---|
| 答 1–2 词 | FC ≤ 5 | 强制每题 2–3 句 |
| 每题都用 "I think" | LR ≤ 6 | 替换为 "from my perspective / I suppose / it seems to me" |
| 答太长被考官打断 | FC ≤ 6 | 答 1 个例子就主动停 |
| 复杂结构必错 | GRA ≤ 6 | 改为先主谓后右扩 |
| 全文平调 | P ≤ 6 | 引入简单升降调 |
| 个人例子过载（Part 3） | TR ≤ 6 | 用 "in my country / generally" 转向一般化 |
| 答案无结尾 | LR / FC ≤ 6 | 强制加 1 句 "looking back / on the whole" |
| 填空 "um" / "ah" 过多 | FC ≤ 6 | 替换为 "well / I mean / actually" |
| 自我修正失败 | FC ≤ 6 | 学会 "I mean / actually / sorry" 三件套 |
| 口音重但可懂 | P 不扣 | 维持原状，把精力给 FC / LR |
| 出现 "I went to..." "people don't know" | GRA ≤ 5.5 | 消灭中国学生 5 个语言陷阱 |
