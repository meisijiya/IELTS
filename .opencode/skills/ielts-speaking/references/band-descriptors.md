# 官方口语四项评分标准详解（Speaking Band Descriptors）

> 数据来源：IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors, Public Version（British Council / IDP / Cambridge 联合发布）
> 原文 PDF：https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf
> 配套文档：https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-key-assessment-criteria.pdf
> 数据采集：2026 年最新版
>
> ⚠️ **使用范围说明**：本文件的 4 项评分描述（FC / LR / GRA / P 在 5–9 档）来自官方公开版 PDF；本文件中各档的「关键扣分点 / 升档关键 / 6→7 升级关键 / 中国学生陷阱」等条目为**教学经验启发**，非官方评分细则。**官方按整体描述匹配评分（holistic descriptor fitting），不是按错误次数机械扣分。**

---

## 评分框架

口语的 **4 项评分标准各占 25%**，每项独立 0–9 分（半档），最终 Speaking 分 = 4 项平均，按 .25 / .75 规则取整。

```
Speaking 口语分 = (FC + LR + GRA + P) / 4
```

**.25 规则**（同写作）：
- **.25 → 进位到上半档**（如 6.25 → 6.5）
- **.75 → 进位到下一整数档**（如 6.75 → 7.0）

**官方注释**（PDF 末尾）：
> "A candidate must fully fit the positive features of the descriptor at a particular level."
> "A candidate will be rated on their average performance across all parts of the test."

→ 不要"按缺口"评分，要"按正向特征"评分；不分子分数（每项最终只有 1 个 band）。

## 一、Fluency and Coherence（FC）流利度与连贯性

> 衡量：speech rate + speech continuity + logical sequencing + cohesive devices.

| Band | 描述 |
|---|---|
| **9** | Fluent with only very occasional repetition or self-correction. Any hesitation that occurs is used only to prepare the content of the next utterance and not to find words or grammar. Speech is situationally appropriate. A wide range of cohesive features are fully acceptable. Topic development is fully coherent and appropriately extended. |
| **8** | Fluent with only very occasional repetition or self-correction. Hesitation may occasionally be used to find words or grammar, but most will be content related. Topic development is coherent, appropriate and relevant. |
| **7** | Able to keep going and readily produce long turns without noticeable effort. Some hesitation, repetition and/or self-correction may occur, often mid-sentence and indicate problems with accessing appropriate language. However, these will not affect coherence. Flexible use of spoken discourse markers, connectives and cohesive features. |
| **6** | Able to keep going and demonstrates a willingness to produce long turns. Coherence may be lost at times as a result of hesitation, repetition and/or self-correction. Uses a range of spoken discourse markers, connectives and cohesive features though not always appropriately. |
| **5** | Usually able to keep going, but relies on repetition and self-correction to do so and/or on slow speech. Hesitations are often associated with mid-sentence searches for fairly basic lexis and grammar. Overuse of certain discourse markers, connectives and other cohesive features. More complex speech usually causes disfluency but simpler language may be produced fluently. |

### FC 关键扣分点

- **重复开头句** "I think..." / "I like..." 5 次以上 → 卡 5.5。
- **每句话都自我中断**（"I went to... no, I..."） → 卡 6。
- **长时间死寂**（5 秒以上）→ 卡 5。
- **没有清晰分段**：Part 2 像一锅粥，从头到尾讲完无停顿、无信号词 → 卡 6。

### FC 6 → 7 升档关键

- 学会**自然 fillers**：`well`、`actually`、`you know`、`I mean`、`to be honest`、`the thing is`、`let me think` — 这些不是"卡顿"，是"思考间隙的自然填空"。
- **话题标记词**（signposting）：`first of all`、`to begin with`、`as for that`、`on top of that`、`moving on`。
- **不打断自己的语法**：用 `I mean`、`or rather`、`sorry, let me rephrase` 优雅自我修正。
- **连续说话 2 分钟不歇气**（Part 2 关键）。

## 二、Lexical Resource（LR）词汇资源

> 衡量：variety + adequacy + circumlocution ability（解释能力）。

| Band | 描述 |
|---|---|
| **9** | Total flexibility and precise use in all contexts. Sustained use of accurate and idiomatic language. |
| **8** | Wide resource, readily and flexibly used to discuss all topics and convey precise meaning. Skilful use of less common and idiomatic items despite occasional inaccuracies in word choice and collocation. Effective use of paraphrase as required. |
| **7** | Resource flexibly used to discuss a variety of topics. Some ability to use less common and idiomatic items and an awareness of style and collocation is evident though inappropriacies occur. Effective use of paraphrase as required. |
| **6** | Resource sufficient to discuss topics at length. Vocabulary use may be inappropriate but meaning is clear. Generally able to paraphrase successfully. |
| **5** | Resource sufficient to discuss familiar and unfamiliar topics but there is limited flexibility. Attempts paraphrase but not always with success. |

### LR 关键扣分点

- **机械重复**：`very`、`really`、`a lot of`、`big`、`good` 反复出现 → 卡 6。
- **搭配错误**：`make a decision` → 不会，`do a decision` → 卡 5.5。
- **不会 paraphrase**：想说"丰富"但不会 `diverse / a wide range of` → 卡 6。
- **过简单**：全程只用 1000 基础词 → 卡 5.5。

### LR 6 → 7 升档关键

- **围绕一个意思学 3 个替换词**：
  - `important` → `essential / crucial / vital / of great significance`
  - `good` → `helpful / beneficial / favourable / positive`
  - `bad` → `harmful / detrimental / unfavourable / negative`
- **课程强调的高频替换词**（性价比最高）：
  - `delicious`（食物）→ `mouth-watering` / `satisfying`
  - `happy` → `thrilled`（强烈）/ `over the moon`（极开心）/ `pretty chuffed`（英式）
  - `very + adj` → `absolutely / genuinely / truly / incredibly + adj`
  - `great` → `fabulous / exceptional`
  - `much / many` → `a great deal of / a wealth of / plenty of`
  - `because`（每句都用）→ `The main reason is that / This is largely because / What drives this is`

> 完整替换词清单见 `references/speaking-toolkit.md` 第三部分（7 大类共 50+ 替换词）。
- **搭配优先于单词**：
  - `make a decision`（√），而不是 `do a decision`（×）
  - `pose a threat`（√），而不是 `give a threat`（×）
  - `raise awareness`（√），而不是 `open awareness`（×）
- **学会 circumlocution**：不会说 `gym`，可以说 `a place where people go to exercise and build strength`。
- **idiomatic / less common items**：偶尔用 `by and large`、`a blessing in disguise`、`on the fence`、`up in the air`。

### 中国学生 LR 常见陷阱

| 想表达 | 错误 | 正确 |
|---|---|---|
| 学习知识 | `learn knowledge` | `acquire knowledge` / `gain knowledge` |
| 适应 / 习惯 | `adapt the environment` | `adapt to the environment` |
| 做决定 | `make a decision` 没错，但不要 `do a decision` | `make / reach / arrive at a decision` |
| 受益 | `benefit from` 不接 it | `benefit from something` / `be beneficial to` |
| 解决 | `solve the problem` 没错 | `tackle / address / resolve the issue` |
| 影响 | `affect` 没错 | `have an impact on / influence / shape` |

## 三、Grammatical Range and Accuracy（GRA）语法范围与准确度

> 衡量：sentence length + complexity + subordinate clauses + variety + error rate。

| Band | 描述 |
|---|---|
| **9** | Structures are precise and accurate at all times, apart from 'mistakes' characteristic of native speaker speech. |
| **8** | Wide range of structures, flexibly used. The majority of sentences are error free. Occasional inappropriacies and a few basic errors may persist. |
| **7** | A range of structures flexibly used. Error-free sentences are frequent. Both simple and complex sentences are used effectively despite some errors. A few basic errors persist. |
| **6** | Produces a mix of short and complex sentence forms and a variety of structures with limited flexibility. Though errors frequently occur in complex structures, these rarely impede communication. |
| **5** | Basic sentence forms are fairly well controlled for accuracy. Complex structures are attempted but these are limited in range, nearly always contain errors and may lead to the need for reformulation. |

### GRA 关键扣分点

- **时态错配**：讲过去的故事全用现在时 → 卡 5.5。
- **第三人称单数漏 -s**：`he go`、`she like` → 卡 5.5。
- **主谓不一致**：`The list of items are long` → 卡 6。
- **介词错误**：`I'm good in English`（应为 `at`）→ 卡 6。
- **句式单一**：全程简单句 + and 堆叠 → 卡 5.5。
- **复杂句必错**：每用 1 个定语从句就 1 个错 → 卡 5.5（不够 6）。

### GRA 6 → 7 升档关键

- **每段至少 1 个复杂结构**：
  - 定语从句：`the person who taught me / the place where I grew up`
  - 状语从句：`Although / Because / When / If / While`
  - 条件句：`If I had the chance, I would...`
  - 被动语态：`It is widely believed that...`
  - 分词结构：`Having lived there for years, I...`
- **复合句右扩**：先主谓后右扩（写作同样技巧）。
  - ✓ `The teacher, who taught me for three years, was very strict.`
  - ✗ `The teacher who taught me for three years and was very strict and always helped me.`
- **避免"自造新词"**：不存在的英语单词（如 `ungood`、`fullfully`）→ 卡 5。

### 中国学生 GRA 常见陷阱

| 错误 | 例子 | 修正 |
|---|---|---|
| 中式英语造词 | `ungood`、`fullfillable` | 改用标准词 |
| 介词错（受中文影响） | `at this aspect`（应为 `in this aspect`） | 复习介词搭配 |
| 漏 be 动词 | `I good at English` | `I am good at English` |
| 时态错 | `Yesterday I go to...` | `Yesterday I went to...` |
| 主谓不一致 | `The list are...` | `The list is...` |
| 冠词多余 | `I want a water`、`in the society` | `I want water` / `in society` |
| 动词-介词搭配错 | `discuss about`（应为 `discuss`） | 死记搭配 |

## 四、Pronunciation（P）发音

> 衡量：intelligibility + strain on listener + phonological features + L1 influence。

| Band | 描述 |
|---|---|
| **9** | Uses a full range of phonological features to convey precise and/or subtle meaning. Flexible use of features of connected speech is sustained throughout. Can be effortlessly understood throughout. Accent has no effect on intelligibility. |
| **8** | Uses a wide range of phonological features to convey precise and/or subtle meaning. Can sustain appropriate rhythm. Flexible use of stress and intonation across long utterances, despite occasional lapses. Can be easily understood throughout. Accent has minimal effect on intelligibility. |
| **7** | Displays all the positive features of band 6, and some, but not all, of the positive features of band 8. |
| **6** | Uses a range of phonological features, but control is variable. Chunking is generally appropriate, but rhythm may be affected by a lack of stress-timing and/or a rapid speech rate. Some effective use of intonation and stress, but this is not sustained. Individual words or phonemes may be mispronounced but this causes only occasional lack of clarity. Can generally be understood throughout without much effort. |
| **5** | Displays all the positive features of band 4, and some, but not all, of the positive features of band 6. |

### P 关键扣分点

- **L1 口音过重**：考官需要反复听才能懂 → 卡 5.5。
- **完全无语调**：从头到尾平调，像在念稿 → 卡 6。
- **重音错**：把 `important` 重音放在 `por-` 而非 `-por-` → 卡 6.5。
- **连读过度或全无**：要么完全不做连读（每个词孤立），要么连读过头（将不同单词黏在一起）→ 卡 6。

### P 6 → 7 升档关键

- **句重音**：内容词（动词 / 名词 / 形容词 / 副词）重读，功能词（介词 / 助动词 / 连词）轻读。
  - `I think it is / important to study / English every day.`
- **词重音准确**：双音节词重音位置、`-tion` 结尾词重音靠前。
- **语调自然**：陈述句降调，yes/no 问题升调，wh- 问题降调。
- **节奏 stress-timed**：不是 syllable-timed（中文特性）。中文每个字等长；英语重音节长，非重音节短。

### P vs Accent

> **关键事实**：官方明确说"accent has no effect on intelligibility"。**口音本身不扣分，听不懂才扣分**。
> 中国学生有口音 ≠ 5.5 分；中国学生有口音 + 考官听不懂 = 5.5 分。

详见 `references/pronunciation-guide.md`。

## 五、四项分数组合对总分的影响

| FC | LR | GRA | P | 平均 | 总分 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 7 | 7 | 7 | 7.0 | 7.0 |
| 7 | 6 | 7 | 7 | 6.75 | 7.0 |
| 7 | 6 | 6 | 7 | 6.5 | 6.5 |
| 6 | 6 | 6 | 6 | 6.0 | 6.0 |
| 8 | 7 | 7 | 7 | 7.25 | 7.5 |
| 8 | 8 | 7 | 7 | 7.5 | 7.5 |
| 6 | 7 | 6 | 7 | 6.5 | 6.5 |

> ⚠️ **木桶效应**：四项中最低项往往决定总分上限。要冲 7，**最弱项至少要 6**（如 7/6/7/7 = 6.75 → 7.0），但**单项 6 不会自动封顶 7**——参见上方表格。
> ⚠️ **口语特殊性**：P 项在文本评估中无法精确判断，必须有录音。即便如此，LR / GRA 在文本中足以评估，FC 部分可从填词和停顿标注推断。

## 六、整体口语分数 → 能力对照

| 总分 | 能力描述 |
|---|---|
| 9 | Expert：完全掌握，精准、流利、复杂论证。 |
| 8 | Very good：完全掌握，仅偶有非系统性不准确；处理复杂论证良好。 |
| 7 | Good：操作掌握，偶有不准确；处理复杂语言良好。 |
| 6 | Competent：尽管有不准确仍有效；熟悉情境有合理复杂语言。 |
| 5 | Modest：部分掌握；处理整体意义；错误多；自身领域基本沟通。 |
| 4 | Limited：基础能力限于熟悉情境；经常出错。 |
| 3 | Extremely limited：仅传达一般意义；频繁沟通中断。 |
| 2 | Intermittent：理解英语有严重困难。 |
| 1 | Non-user：除几个孤立词外无能力。 |
| 0 | Did not attempt. |

## 七、Examiner 实际评估的子项（演讲 / 应试技巧）

来源：IELTS Speaking Research Report（Seedhouse et al., 2014）— ielts.org 公开文献。

| 评分项 | 实际评估的子项 |
|---|---|
| **Fluency & Coherence** | speech rate, pause patterns, length of turns, logical sequencing, signposting, cohesive devices |
| **Lexical Resource** | variety, appropriacy, accuracy, ability to paraphrase (circumlocute) |
| **Grammatical Range & Accuracy** | sentence length, complexity, subordination, error frequency, error effect on communication |
| **Pronunciation** | strain on listener, unintelligible segments, L1 influence, individual sounds, word/sentence stress, intonation, rhythm, connected speech |

## 八、口语 vs 写作 评分对照（考生易混淆点）

| 维度 | 写作 | 口语 |
|---|---|---|
| **任务回应** | Task Achievement / Task Response | 无对应项（用 FC 隐含） |
| **连贯性** | Coherence & Cohesion | Fluency & Coherence（**加了流利度**） |
| **词汇** | Lexical Resource | Lexical Resource（相同） |
| **语法** | Grammatical Range & Accuracy | Grammatical Range & Accuracy（相同） |
| **发音** | 无 | **Pronunciation**（口语独有） |
| **总评公式** | (T1 + T2×2) / 3 | (FC + LR + GRA + P) / 4 |
| **可改** | 写作可以改 5 遍 | 口语说出来就定 |

> 口语比写作多的硬性要求：**流利度（不放录音）、发音（无文字）**。口语比写作多两份"无法修改"的压力。
