Task 2 — Time away from families (problem-solution)

In many countries, people spend more and more time away from their families. What causes this trend? What effect do you think has on themselves and their families? (2022.4.30)

立场: 远因在工作模式，近因在数字化娱乐; 理由1: 长工时与远程办公模糊生活边界; 理由2: 个体孤独感上升与家庭凝聚力削弱

The aim of this essay is to explore the main causes of the growing amount of time that people spend away from their families and to examine the effects that this trend produces on both individuals and their households.

The leading cause is the changing nature of work itself. Globalisation has created multinational teams whose meetings stretch across time zones, while the rise of remote work has blurred the boundary between the office and the home in subtle ways. Many employees now respond to messages late into the evening or travel for conferences that last several days, and their physical presence at family dinners or weekend gatherings quietly disappears. A second cause is the spread of personal digital entertainment, from social media feeds to immersive video games, which gives individuals attractive solo activities that compete with ordinary family conversations for attention.

These causes produce significant effects. On an individual level, prolonged absence from family life is closely linked to rising rates of loneliness, sleep disturbance and mild depression, because the emotional support that families provide is gradually replaced by shallow online contact. For the family itself, the consequences are equally serious: children whose parents are rarely present miss everyday guidance and may develop weaker attachment patterns, while spouses who live almost parallel lives report lower marital satisfaction over time. Were these patterns to continue for another generation, communities would lose the close-knit character that historically sustained them.

In conclusion, the time spent away from families is driven mainly by demanding work patterns and personal digital habits, and its harmful effects on both individuals and households deserve far more public attention.
