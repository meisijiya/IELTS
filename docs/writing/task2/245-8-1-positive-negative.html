Task 2 — Affordable overseas travel (positive-negative)

It has become easier and affordable for people to visit other countries. Is it a positive or negative development? (2021.2.6)

立场: 倾向积极，因文化交流与个人视野的收益大于环境代价; 理由1: 出境游促进跨文化理解与个人视野; 理由2: 低碳航空与游客教育可缓解环境与文化冲击

Cheap flights and online booking platforms have made it possible for ordinary households to travel abroad, and this rise in affordable tourism has become a defining feature of modern life. While concerns about overcrowding and emissions are legitimate, I would argue that, on the whole, the trend is a positive one, because cross-cultural contact and personal growth outweigh the costs when properly managed.

The first benefit is that wider international travel broadens minds and strengthens mutual understanding. When students from a small town visit a temple in Thailand, share a meal with a family in Morocco or watch history being performed in Greece, they break down the stereotypes they absorbed from films and gain a deeper appreciation of how others actually live. This kind of direct encounter is far more persuasive than any textbook or documentary, and it produces citizens who are less likely to demonise foreign cultures in public debate.

The drawbacks, however, deserve honest attention. Mass tourism does inflate local housing prices, exhaust fragile ecosystems and place pressure on customs and infrastructure in small destinations. Carbon emissions from short-haul flights, if left unchecked, will steadily erode the very landscapes visitors travel to admire. These risks can, nevertheless, be mitigated through greener aviation fuel, visitor caps in sensitive areas and tourist taxes that fund conservation, meaning the negative effects are serious but not uncontrollable.

In conclusion, easier overseas travel brings genuine cultural and educational gains to ordinary citizens, and although it places real strain on host communities and the climate, careful regulation allows the world to enjoy the benefits without dismantling the very places that make travel worthwhile for future generations.
