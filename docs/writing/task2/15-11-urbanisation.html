Task 2 — Urbanisation (discuss-both-views)

Some people think rapid population growth in cities only benefits people who live there while others believe that there are disadvantages. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. (2025.2.8)

Today, more people than ever are moving into large cities, and this rapid urban growth has sparked debate. While some believe that this trend only brings advantages to those who live in cities, others argue that there are clear drawbacks. This essay will discuss both sides and explain why I partly agree with the first view.

On the one hand, city residents do gain a great deal from population growth. A larger population creates stronger demand for goods and services, which encourages businesses to expand and brings more job opportunities, particularly in technology, healthcare, and education. With more tax revenue, local governments can also upgrade public transport, build new hospitals, and improve other facilities that directly benefit the people who already live there. For young professionals and skilled workers, cities therefore remain attractive places to build a career. It also attracts cultural venues such as theatres and restaurants.

On the other hand, rapid urbanisation does create serious problems. Housing demand pushes up property prices and rents, so even working families may struggle to afford a decent home near the city centre. Traffic congestion, air pollution, and overstretched schools and hospitals reduce the quality of life for existing residents. Because of these pressures, I believe that the benefits are not enjoyed equally, and that the disadvantages cannot be ignored. It also widens the gap between rich and poor.

In my view, although population growth does bring real advantages to city dwellers, governments should also invest in affordable housing, public transport, and green spaces so that the disadvantages do not outweigh the gains. Balanced urban planning is the key to making cities work for everyone.

TA：本文为 Task 2「双方讨论」题型，开头用 While some believe… others argue… 明确引出两种观点，并以 This essay will discuss both sides and explain why I partly agree with the first view 给出立场（部分同意第一方），结尾 In my view 再次呼应立场，TA 满足字数与立场要求。
