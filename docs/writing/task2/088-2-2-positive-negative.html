Task 2 — Ambition (positive-negative)

Ambition is a positive quality for people to have in many societies today. How important is it for people who want to succeed in life? Is it a positive or negative characteristic? (2023.12.30)

立场: 野心是积极特质; 理由1: 驱动长期目标与突破; 理由2: 推动社会进步与集体成就

The question of whether ambition is a positive or negative characteristic has generated considerable debate in many modern societies today. From my perspective, ambition is largely a positive quality, and it is essential for anyone who wishes to achieve meaningful success in life.

Firstly, ambition provides the sustained motivation that individuals need in order to set long-term goals and persist through extended difficulty. A clear example can be found in the early careers of countless entrepreneurs, who worked for years on modest salaries before establishing companies that later created thousands of jobs. Without an inner drive to improve their circumstances, such individuals would have abandoned their plans at the first serious setback, and their potential contributions would never have materialised. Secondly, ambition exerts a positive influence on the wider community, because the breakthroughs that ambitious people pursue often lift the standard of living for everyone around them. When researchers aim to cure a chronic disease or engineers strive to build cleaner energy systems, the resulting innovations benefit society far beyond the original vision of the person who started the work, opening doors that previous generations had considered permanently closed.

Admittedly, ambition can become destructive when it is pursued without moral restraint, leading to corruption, exploitation or chronic burnout. Nevertheless, these problems arise from a lack of ethical checks rather than from ambition itself, and ambitious people who remain accountable to their colleagues, families and communities tend, on balance, to produce admirable outcomes that command lasting respect.

In conclusion, I consider ambition to be a positive characteristic, because it fuels personal achievement and generates wider progress that benefits society as a whole.
