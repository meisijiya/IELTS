Task 2 — Animal protection (agree-disagree)

Many animals are endangered. Some people argue that we should only protect animals that are useful to humans. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.3.4)

Some people suggest that, since many animal species are now endangered, governments and charities should only protect those species that bring clear benefits to humans. In my opinion, I strongly disagree with this view. Every kind of wildlife has its own value, and a narrow human-centred approach would ultimately damage both nature and people themselves.

There are two main reasons why I hold this position. First, ecosystems depend on a wide variety of species. Insects, birds and small mammals that seem useless to us often play essential roles, such as pollinating crops, controlling pests or recycling nutrients in the soil. If we let them die out simply because they are not directly useful, the natural systems that support human food production and clean water will quietly collapse. Second, protecting wildlife also reflects an ethical responsibility. Human beings have the power to destroy habitats, so they also have the duty to care for the other creatures that share the planet. Allowing a species to vanish because it brings no profit is a form of cruelty, and future generations will judge us harshly for it.

Admittedly, limited resources do force governments to set priorities. Saving a large, attractive animal such as a panda can also raise public awareness and attract funding for wider conservation work. Nevertheless, this does not mean that less popular species such as insects, frogs or small bats should be abandoned, because their loss can also disrupt the balance of nature.

In conclusion, I believe that wildlife should be protected as a whole, not only the parts that humans find convenient. A healthy natural world is the most valuable inheritance we can leave to future generations.

TA：本文为 Task 2「同意与否」题型，开头以 In my opinion, I strongly disagree 明确表态，结尾用 I believe 再次呼应，整文立场清晰、回答完整，TA 满足要求。

CC：采用四段式：观点—双理由—让步—结论；衔接词 First / Second / Admittedly / In conclusion 过渡自然，段落功能划分合理，符合 6 分水平。LR：用词以常见话题词为主（endangered、wildlife、ecosystem），搭配少量替换（pollination、ethical responsibility、conservation），未出现低频高难词。GRA：以复合句和状语从句为主，结构清楚、无明显语法错误，是一份稳定的 6 分范本。
