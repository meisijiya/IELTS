Task 2 — Employee dress code (agree-disagree)

Some people believe that employers should not be concerned about the way their employees dress but be concerned about the quality of work. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.10.23)

立场: 不同意放任穿着; 理由1: 服装影响客户信任与专业形象; 理由2: 着装塑造员工自我约束与团队氛围

It is often said that employers should focus solely on the quality of their employees' work and pay no attention to the way they dress in the workplace. I disagree with this view, because appropriate dress codes exert a meaningful influence on a company's professional image and on the self-discipline of its staff, both of which are closely tied to long-term performance.

The first reason is that an employee's appearance shapes the impression that customers and business partners form of the entire organisation. When a client enters a law firm or a financial consultancy and meets staff in casual T-shirts and trainers, the firm immediately appears less reliable than one whose employees wear smart business attire. A clear example of this can be seen in the banking sector, where strict dress codes remain in force precisely because clients expect visible signals of seriousness when entrusting large sums of money to advisers. The second reason is that a reasonable dress code helps employees themselves develop a sense of discipline and shared identity, qualities that improve both their behaviour at work and the results they deliver. Putting on a uniform or formal outfit each morning signals a clear shift from private life to professional responsibility, encouraging the focused mindset that difficult tasks require. Were all dress expectations to be removed entirely, that subtle psychological cue would disappear, and many workers would find it noticeably harder to maintain the concentration their jobs actually demand.

In conclusion, I do not believe that employers should ignore the way their employees dress, because appropriate clothing reinforces professional credibility and quietly supports the very quality of work that the original statement claims to prioritise.
