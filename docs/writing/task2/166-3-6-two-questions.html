Task 2 — Spending on youthful appearance (two-questions)

People spend a lot of money on appearance because they want to look younger. Why does this happen? Do you think this is a positive or negative development? (2024.10.26)

立场: 揭示老龄焦虑与社交媒体压力驱动外貌消费，并判断整体为负面发展; 理由1: 老龄焦虑与社交媒体引发外貌焦虑; 理由2: 负面为主因消耗资源、固化年龄歧视

The aim of this essay is to examine why so many people are willing to spend heavily on products and treatments that promise a younger appearance, and to consider whether this widespread behaviour should be regarded as a positive or a negative development.

Two related forces drive the trend. The first is anxiety about ageing. In societies that equate physical attractiveness with professional success, growing older is often portrayed as a personal failure, so ordinary adults feel pressured to hide visible signs of age. The second force is the media environment. Image-led platforms reward polished selfies, while celebrity culture normalises cosmetic procedures and surgical enhancements, making such spending appear routine and aspirational. Together these pressures create a commercial incentive for an entire industry to convince consumers that looking younger is both achievable and socially necessary.

I would argue that this development is, on balance, a negative one. The financial cost can be enormous, particularly for young adults who divert money that could otherwise go towards housing, education or saving for emergencies into endless beauty routines. The psychological cost is just as serious: when a youthful appearance becomes the principal source of self-worth, people of every age become vulnerable to anxiety, low mood and body dissatisfaction. The trend also reinforces age discrimination in workplaces and social settings, marginalising older citizens whose wisdom and experience deserve greater respect. While sensible grooming and health-conscious care are perfectly reasonable, the relentless pursuit of looking younger tends to undermine rather than enrich individual well-being.

In conclusion, the modern obsession with youthful appearance stems from cultural anxiety about ageing and image-driven social media, and the overall impact is negative because it drains household budgets, harms mental health and entrenches prejudice against older citizens.
