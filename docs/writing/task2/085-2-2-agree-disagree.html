Task 2 — Computer skills for success (agree-disagree)

If you want to be successful at work, you should have high-level computer skills. Do you agree or disagree? (2025.4.12)

立场: 部分同意, 计算机技能重要但非唯一; 理由1: 高级计算机技能已成多数岗位基础; 理由2: 沟通与领导力等人际能力同样关键

In many discussions about career success, the importance of advanced computer skills is strongly emphasised. While I accept that such skills have become increasingly valuable, I only partially agree with the claim that they alone are enough to guarantee professional achievement.

The case for high-level computer skills is straightforward. Almost every modern workplace now relies on digital tools, from data analysis platforms to project management software, and workers who cannot use these tools effectively risk being left behind as their industries change around them. A clear example is the growing demand for professionals who can write code, automate routine processes or interpret large datasets, roles that frequently command both higher salaries and faster promotion than comparable non-technical positions. Were a candidate to ignore such skills, their career options would shrink considerably over time, regardless of how talented they might be in other respects, and they would find it hard to climb into senior leadership where data-driven decisions dominate.

Nevertheless, computer skills are not the only ingredient of success. Communication, teamwork, leadership and creative problem-solving remain essential, particularly in roles that depend on building trust with clients, persuading stakeholders or managing complex human dynamics across borders. Many senior managers who barely use a spreadsheet still thrive because they can inspire colleagues and negotiate effectively with clients, while technically brilliant employees can stall if they cannot share their ideas clearly or lead a team through a difficult transition.

In conclusion, I still believe that high-level computer skills are highly useful but not in themselves sufficient, and that they should be combined with strong interpersonal abilities if a worker truly wants to succeed.
