Task 2 — Tax on fattening foods (agree-disagree)

More and more people are seriously overweight. Some people suggest the solution to this problem is to increase the price of fattening foods. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.6.17)

As obesity rates rise in many countries, some people argue that the most effective way to fight this trend is to make fattening foods more expensive through higher taxes. While I accept that price can influence what people buy, I disagree with the view that raising the cost of such foods is the best solution.

There are two main reasons why I hold this position. First, price changes often hurt the wrong group. A higher tax on sugary drinks or fast food usually falls hardest on low-income families, who already spend a large share of their budget on basic meals. For them, eating unhealthily is often a matter of cost rather than choice, since fresh fruit, vegetables and lean meat are more expensive per calorie. Punishing these families with higher prices would worsen inequality rather than improve health. Second, raising prices does little to address the real causes of overeating. Long working hours, easy access to takeaways, stress and lack of exercise all play a bigger part, and a small price increase is unlikely to change these habits.

Admittedly, higher prices may discourage some buyers, especially young people with limited pocket money. Yet this effect is limited and can be achieved in fairer ways.

In my view, governments should focus on clearer food labelling, better school meals and public sports facilities. Education and opportunity, not penalties, are the most reliable route to a healthier population.

In conclusion, I still believe that increasing the price of fattening foods is the wrong tool for the job. A wider set of measures that targets the real causes of obesity will do far more good.

TA：本文为 Task 2「同意与否」题型，开头段以 I disagree with the view 明确表态（避免 whether），结尾段用 I still believe 再次呼应，TA 立场清晰且保持一致。
