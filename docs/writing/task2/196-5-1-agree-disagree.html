Task 2 — Communication technology and social bonds (agree-disagree)

Some people think that modern communication technology is having a negative effect on a social relationship. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2025.2.22)

立场: 不同意; 理由1: 拉近远距离亲情的日常维系; 理由2: 维护弱关系并降低善意门槛

Some people believe that modern communication technology exerts a harmful influence on social relationships. I firmly disagree with this sweeping claim, because when used with care such tools actually deepen emotional bonds and broaden the circles of support that earlier generations could never maintain, and the evidence from everyday life supports this view.

My first reason is that digital communication enriches, rather than erodes, the texture of close relationships between family members who happen to live far apart. Migrant workers, international students and parents separated by oceans now exchange daily voice notes, photographs and short videos, sustaining an intimacy that postcards and expensive long-distance calls once made impossible. A clear example is overseas students who, through video calls at bedtime, remain a comforting presence for younger siblings and ageing parents who would otherwise feel abandoned. Without these tools, such bonds would gradually weaken into occasional formalities, leaving relatives feeling like strangers whenever they finally meet in person.

My second reason is that technology lowers the cost of keeping weak ties alive, which sociologists consistently show is valuable for both mental health and career opportunity. Reconnecting with a former colleague, sending a quick message to a sick friend, or organising a neighbourhood support group for elderly residents all happen faster and more cheaply than ever before. Were we to abandon these channels, communities would lose the flexible glue that holds acquaintances together, and many small acts of kindness would simply never reach the people who need them in a moment of crisis.

In conclusion, I still believe that communication technology, far from damaging relationships, gives ordinary people richer, more constant and more inclusive ways of caring for one another, provided they use it with attention and warmth.
