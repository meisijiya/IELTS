Task 2 — Noise pollution (problem-solution)

The level of noise around us is constantly increasing and is affecting the quality of our lives. What causes the noise? What should be done about it? (2022.10.22)

立场: 噪音主要源自交通、建筑与娱乐业, 需要城市规划与立法协同治理; 理由1: 交通流量、建筑施工、夜间娱乐与工业设备共同推动噪音水平上升; 理由2: 解决方案包括低噪音城市规划、严格分时段管制、推广电动车与公众教育。

The aim of this essay is to discuss the main causes of the rising background noise that affects modern urban life, and to suggest what governments, businesses and individuals can do to bring it under control.

Several overlapping human activities contribute to the steady increase in everyday noise. The most obvious is road traffic: the growing number of cars, buses and delivery vans in cities produces an almost constant hum, while older aircraft taking off and landing near residential areas add bursts of intense sound. Construction is another major source, since cities are continually rebuilt with new towers, roads and metro lines, often working around the clock to meet deadlines. A third factor is the expansion of nightlife; bars, gyms and loudspeakers at private gatherings generate noise that travels easily through thin modern walls. Together these sources ensure that even people who live quietly indoors are rarely free from background disturbance.

The solutions must therefore match the diversity of the problem rather than focus on any single source. Urban planners can introduce buffer zones between busy roads and housing, plant more trees and require sound-insulating glazing in new buildings. Authorities should set strict, time-based limits on construction and entertainment noise, enforce them with meaningful fines and display noise readings in public spaces. The switch to electric vehicles, quieter aircraft engines and well-maintained industrial equipment further reduces volume at source, and individuals can use headphones at low volume and keep televisions away from shared walls, helping to make considerate behaviour a recognised part of community life.

In conclusion, noise pollution arises from a combination of traffic, construction and social activity, but a mix of planning, regulation and everyday courtesy can significantly improve the quality of urban life.
