Task 2 — Having children later in life (problem-solution)

Nowadays, more and more people decide to have children later in their lives. What do you think are the reasons for this? Do the advantages of the trend outweigh the disadvantages? (2024.8.10)

立场: 揭示经济与价值观转变推迟生育，并权衡利弊给出父母成熟与人口压力双重判断; 理由1: 经济压力与教育延长推迟生育; 理由2: 利为父母成熟与家庭资源，弊为老龄化压力

The aim of this essay is to identify the main reasons why more adults are choosing to have children later in life, and to weigh the resulting advantages against the disadvantages to decide whether the trend is, on balance, a positive one.

Three factors explain the shift. The most powerful is economic pressure: housing in major cities has become prohibitively expensive, and young couples feel unable to provide a stable home before welcoming a child. Career considerations compound the problem, since men and women now invest many additional years in education and professional training before they feel financially secure. Cultural change is equally significant: arranged marriages have given way to individual choice, and many adults prefer to travel or build a savings buffer before taking on the long-term responsibilities of raising a child.

On the positive side, older parents usually have greater emotional maturity, more stable incomes and a clearer sense of the values they wish to pass on, which often translates into patient child-rearing. The drawbacks, however, are considerable. Society-wide, an ageing population and a shrinking workforce place enormous strain on pension systems and healthcare budgets. Within families, older parents may have less physical energy for the most demanding kinds of parenting. On balance, though, the personal advantages of later parenthood are genuine and the demographic costs can be addressed through sensible immigration and pension reform, so the advantages outweigh the disadvantages.

In conclusion, later parenthood is driven mainly by economic and cultural forces, and although it creates real demographic pressures, the benefits of more mature and financially prepared parents make the trend a positive one for individual families.
