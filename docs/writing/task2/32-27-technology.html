Task 2 — Robots and future society (discuss-both-views)

Some people believe that robots are important to humans' future development, while others think that it is a dangerous invention that will impact society negatively. Discuss both views and give your opinion. (2022.9.24)

Robots are becoming more common in factories, hospitals and homes, and people disagree about what this means for the future. Some see robots as a key to human progress, while others worry that they will harm society. In my opinion, both concerns are reasonable, but with careful management robots will on balance benefit humanity.

Those who welcome robots often point to their practical value. In industry, robots can do repetitive and dangerous work that humans find tiring or unsafe, such as welding car bodies or handling toxic chemicals. In medicine, surgical robots help doctors operate with greater precision, and service robots can assist elderly people who live alone. By taking over such tasks, robots free people to focus on more creative and meaningful work, which in turn can raise productivity and improve living standards across society.

However, critics raise fair concerns. The most common worry is job losses: if a machine can do the work of three workers, many ordinary employees may struggle to find new employment quickly. There is also the fear that advanced robots could be misused, for example in modern weapons, or that people may become too dependent on machines and lose basic skills. These risks are real and should not be ignored.

My view is that robots are a powerful tool rather than a threat in themselves. If governments update education and retraining programmes, and if clear laws control the use of robotic technology, the benefits can be enjoyed while the dangers are kept in check.

In conclusion, robots can support human development in many ways, yet they also bring social risks that need careful handling. A balanced approach, not blind praise or fear, offers the best path forward.

TA：本文为 Task 2「双方讨论+给出观点」题型，开头段用 Some see ... while others ... In my opinion 明确表态，正文分别论述两种观点，末段再次重申立场并收束，TA 立场清晰且四段式结构完整。
