Task 2 — Celebrities set bad example (agree-disagree)

Nowadays, there are many celebrities who are famous for their glamour and wealth rather than achievements and this sets a bad example for young people. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2023.5.13)

立场: 同意，明星以名利为重确实带坏青少年; 理由1: 消费主义与外貌崇拜扭曲青少年的价值观; 理由2: 喧宾夺主使有实质贡献的榜样被边缘化

Some people argue that modern celebrities, who are celebrated mainly for their glamour and wealth rather than for genuine achievements, set a harmful example for young people. I firmly agree with this view, because the relentless focus on appearance and luxury distorts young people’s values and sidelines more meaningful role models.

The first reason is that the constant promotion of fame and wealth encourages young people to equate personal worth with superficial markers such as designer clothing, luxury cars and social-media followers. Adolescents who absorb this message may abandon long-term goals in favour of chasing internet virality, and they can become demoralised when their ordinary lives fail to match the polished images they see online every day. Studies of teenage mental health repeatedly link heavy social-media use to rising rates of anxiety and body-image dissatisfaction, which suggests that the modern glamour culture is far from harmless to the current generation of young people.

The second reason is that celebrity obsession drowns out voices that deserve greater public attention. Teachers, scientists, community volunteers and skilled craftsmen rarely attract the same headlines as film stars, yet their daily contributions typically do far more good for society as a whole. Were young people to measure success by lasting accomplishment rather than by the number of online admirers, they would find more durable sources of inspiration and, in turn, produce more meaningful work of their own over time.

In conclusion, I still believe that the current cult of glamour-rich celebrity exerts a damaging influence on young people, because it corrupts their sense of value and eclipses the more substantive role models they should be encouraged to admire.
