Task 2 — Multinational companies (agree-disagree)

The spread of multinational companies and the result in increase of globalization will have positive effects on everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.1.09)

立场: 总体同意，因技术转移与消费受益压倒部分行业阵痛; 理由1: 跨国企业加速技术转移与本地技能升级; 理由2: 全球竞争压低价格并扩大商品选择

Many observers claim that the rise of multinational firms and the globalisation they drive will benefit everyone. While critics rightly point to job losses in some sectors and rising inequality within countries, I largely agree with this view, because the spread of technology, the rise in purchasing power and the wider range of consumer goods have lifted ordinary living standards across most regions of the world.

The first reason is that multinational companies accelerate technology transfer and skill development across borders. When a global manufacturer opens a plant in a developing country, it trains local engineers, introduces modern production methods and pushes suppliers to meet international quality standards. A clear example is the migration of advanced electronics assembly from South Korea and Japan into Vietnam and India, an investment that has moved millions of workers into stable middle-class employment and inspired a new generation of local entrepreneurs.

The second reason is that global competition lowers consumer prices and broadens product choice. Shoppers in smaller economies can now buy smartphones, medicines and household appliances at prices once reserved for wealthy citizens, while local brands are forced to innovate rather than hide behind protection. Were governments to shut their borders to multinational trade, ordinary families would lose affordable goods and the pressure that drives domestic firms to improve their standards, leaving entire economies trapped in slow decline.

In conclusion, I still believe that the spread of multinational companies and the resulting globalisation are largely positive, because they spread technology, raise purchasing power and give consumers a genuine choice in everyday life, and these tangible practical gains deserve protection rather than outright rejection.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段结构，开头以 I largely agree 明确表态（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 与 Body 2 分别给出两条独立理由（技术转移 / 价格与选择），结尾以 I still believe that the spread ... are largely positive 再次呼应立场，全文立场贯穿无摇摆，字数落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段式结构清晰，每段一个主题句 + 展开理由 + 例证或反事实假设（Were governments to shut their borders to multinational trade）；段内以 The first reason / The second reason / A clear example is / While critics rightly / When a global manufacturer 等显式逻辑连接过渡，行文连贯且推进有层次，CC 估测 7 档。LR：使用 technology transfer / skill development / international quality standards / purchasing power / global competition / domestic firms 等话题搭配，搭配准确且有同义替换（multinational firms ↔ global manufacturer / goods ↔ products），LR 估测 7 档。GRA：复合句占比较高，包含定语从句（the globalisation they drive）、过去虚拟条件倒装（Were governments to shut their borders）、现在分词短语（rather than hide behind protection）；主谓一致、时态与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
