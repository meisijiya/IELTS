Task 2 — Family vs outside influence (agree-disagree)

The family has a great influence on children's development, but the influence outside the home plays a bigger part in children's life. Do you agree or disagree? (2021.6.5)

立场: 不同意,家庭仍是首要且长期的影响; 理由1: 家庭奠定价值观与情感依附基础; 理由2: 外部影响虽重要,但居于家庭影响之下

It has been argued that while the family plays an important role in shaping children, influences outside the home exert an even greater impact on a young person's life. While I accept that schools, peers and the media become increasingly important as children grow up, I disagree with the claim that these outside forces outweigh the influence of the family.

The first reason is that the family provides the earliest and most durable framework for a child's values, emotional security and sense of identity. Long before a child meets a teacher or makes a close friend, they have absorbed attitudes towards honesty, hard work and respect from their parents, and these early lessons tend to persist well into adulthood. A clear example is the consistent finding that young people raised in supportive households are better able to cope with peer pressure and recover from setbacks, no matter how chaotic their school environment may be.

The second reason is that outside influences, however powerful, are typically filtered through the values that the family has already installed. A teenager whose parents have taught them to question advertising is far less likely to be swayed by an online celebrity promoting risky behaviour, while a child raised to value learning will treat classroom instruction as an opportunity rather than a chore. Were outside influences truly dominant, then adolescents from similar schools and media environments would develop in broadly similar ways, yet research continually shows that family background remains the strongest predictor of long-term outcomes.

In conclusion, I firmly believe that the family exerts the deepest and most lasting influence on children's development, and that outside factors, while significant, generally operate within the boundaries that the home has already set.
