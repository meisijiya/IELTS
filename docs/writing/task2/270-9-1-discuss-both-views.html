Task 2 — Space vs Earth (discuss-both-views)

Some people think governments should spend money on looking for life on other planets, while others think that there are many unsolved problems on earth. Discuss. (2025.9.27)

立场: 太空研究应继续但设上限; 理由1: 探索拓展科学与技术边界; 理由2: 地球问题紧迫不可忽视

It is sometimes argued that governments should pour money into the search for life on other planets, while critics insist that the many unsolved problems on Earth should take priority. After considering both views, I believe that space exploration deserves continued funding, but only as long as it does not crowd out essential social services.

Supporters of space programmes emphasise the scientific and inspirational value of looking beyond our own world. Discoveries about distant planets deepen our understanding of the universe and may one day reveal whether humanity is alone, a question of fundamental importance. The technology developed for spacecraft, from miniaturised electronics to new materials, often finds practical applications in everyday life. A clear example is memory-foam, which was originally created for astronaut seats and now helps patients with pressure sores in hospitals.

Opponents, however, point out that poverty, disease and climate change already pose a threat to millions of people and cannot be ignored. When a city is flooding or a hospital is understaffed, citizens reasonably ask why their taxes are funding rockets. They also argue that private companies, rather than the public purse, are now the main drivers of space research, so the state should redirect its resources to social welfare, education and disaster prevention.

In my view, a sensible compromise is to set a fixed ceiling for the space budget and ring-fence the rest for urgent terrestrial needs. Such a model allows the long-term benefits of exploration to continue while ensuring that no citizen suffers from neglect at home.

In conclusion, I still believe that the search for life beyond Earth has its place in public funding, but governments must first guarantee that basic social problems are properly addressed.
