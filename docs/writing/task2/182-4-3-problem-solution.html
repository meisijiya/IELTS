Task 2 — Water scarcity (problem-solution)

Water scarcity is a serious problem in many countries. What are the causes of this? And what solutions can be done by individuals and the government? (2021.2.27)

立场: 缺水主要由人为浪费与污染 + 气候变化引起，需要个人与政府协同应对; 理由1: 农业工业过度用水、低效灌溉、生活浪费以及水体污染共同造成紧缺; 理由2: 个人应节水减污，政府应立法、改善基建、推广再生水与雨水收集。

The aim of this essay is to discuss the main causes of water scarcity in many parts of the world and to suggest practical solutions that can be pursued jointly by individuals and national governments.

A range of human activities helps to explain why water shortages are becoming more frequent in regions once considered water-rich. Agriculture remains by far the biggest consumer of fresh water, yet many farms still rely on flood irrigation, with much of the water lost to evaporation before reaching crops. Heavy industries such as textiles, paper and steel further withdraw enormous volumes from rivers that are already under pressure, while untreated sewage and chemical waste from cities regularly poison the supplies that nearby communities depend upon for drinking and washing. Compounding the problem, climate change is shifting rainfall patterns, lengthening droughts and shrinking the glaciers that feed many of the world's major rivers.

The solutions must therefore match the scale and diversity of the problem rather than being left to goodwill alone. Individuals can install low-flow fittings, repair dripping taps, choose shorter showers and avoid pouring cooking oil or harsh chemicals down the drain, all of which meaningfully reduce household demand and household pollution at the same time. Governments, for their part, should upgrade ageing water infrastructure, subsidise drip-irrigation systems for farmers, enforce strict limits on industrial discharge and invest in treated wastewater recycling and large-scale rainwater harvesting. Public campaigns explaining how small daily habits add up to large savings can help to turn these policies into a shared social norm across entire cities.

In conclusion, water scarcity reflects both wasteful consumption and ecological strain, and only a combination of careful government planning and responsible individual behaviour can ease the pressure on this vital shared resource.

TA：本文采用 problem-solution 标准四段式：Intro 以 The aim of this essay is to discuss the main causes ... and to suggest practical solutions ... 一次性点出两个问题，Body 1 回应「原因」（农业用水、工业污水、气候变化），Body 2 用 Individuals can ... Governments, for their part, should ... 明确两层主体分层，结尾 only a combination of government planning and responsible individual behaviour can ease the pressure 总结并指明方向，字数 280 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
