Task 2 — Housing shortage (agree-disagree)

The shortage of housing in big cities can cause severe consequences, and only government action can solve the problem. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2022.3.26)

立场: 不同意仅靠政府; 理由1: 需公共与私人资本并行; 理由2: 城市规划与租客权益同样关键

It is often claimed that only government action can remedy the housing shortage in major cities. While I accept that the state must play the leading role, I disagree with the suggestion that its efforts alone will be enough, because private investment and better planning are equally necessary to ease the pressure on urban accommodation.

The first reason is that no public budget, however generous, can meet demand single-handedly. Building thousands of new units requires land, engineers and long-term financing that even wealthy ministries struggle to provide at scale. When governments invite private developers to share the cost, projects can be completed in years rather than decades, and the supply of affordable flats grows more quickly. Public-private partnerships in cities such as Singapore have shown that combining state subsidies with private capital delivers housing far faster than purely state-led schemes.

The second reason is that a compelling argument can also be made for smarter planning and stronger tenant rights. A government may build plenty of homes, yet if zoning rules still favour luxury towers over social housing, the poor will remain excluded. Likewise, without legal protection against unfair rent hikes, even existing tenants can be pushed out by sudden redevelopment, and the cycle of displacement can quickly spread to nearby neighbourhoods. Clearer land-use rules, capped rent increases and waiting lists for new flats are non-monetary tools that any serious housing policy must contain.

In conclusion, I still believe that government leadership is indispensable, but it must be combined with private capital and intelligent urban planning, otherwise the shortage in big cities will only deteriorate further and undermine social stability for decades.
