Task 2 — Wealth and happiness (agree-disagree)

Some experts suppose that when a country is already rich, any additional increase in economic wealth does not make its citizens happier. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.9.18)

立场: 不同意边际财富无补于幸福; 理由1: 额外财富可投入公共服务与社会保障; 理由2: 经济增长常伴随寿命与安全感提升

Some experts argue that once a country has reached a high level of prosperity, further economic growth no longer brings any extra happiness to its citizens. I disagree with this position, because additional wealth in rich nations can still be channelled into essential services and personal security that genuinely raise subjective well-being across society.

The first reason is that extra national income can be redirected towards public goods whose benefits are felt by every household. In Scandinavian countries, higher tax revenues collected from continued economic growth have funded universal childcare, generous parental leave and high-quality elder care, which together reduce the daily pressures that working families face. Were this surplus to disappear, parents would struggle to balance work and home life, and the overall quality of life would decline noticeably even though the average citizen would still appear affluent on paper.

The second reason is that continued growth often correlates with longer life expectancy and a stronger sense of personal security, both of which quietly shape how content people feel in their daily routines. Investments in modern hospitals, cleaner air and safer neighbourhoods, which wealthier governments can readily afford, extend healthy years and reduce anxiety about the future. A clear example of this pattern is the steady rise in life satisfaction reported in South Korea over the past two decades, a period during which the country's per-capita income continued to climb steadily upward.

In conclusion, I still believe that further economic growth in rich countries does add to citizens' happiness, because it can finance better public services, reduce daily anxieties and lengthen healthy, secure lives for the population as a whole.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段结构，开头以 I disagree with this position 明确表态（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 与 Body 2 分别给出两条独立理由（公共服务投资 / 寿命与安全感），结尾以 I still believe that further economic growth ... does add to citizens' happiness 再次呼应立场，全文立场贯穿无摇摆，字数 280 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
