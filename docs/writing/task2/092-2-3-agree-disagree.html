Task 2 — Long working hours (agree-disagree)

Countries with a long average working time are more economically successful, but they are also likely to suffer from negative social consequences. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2023.5.20)

立场: 部分同意; 理由1: 长时间工作确实提升短期产出; 理由2: 家庭与社会成本削弱长期收益

Many observers claim that nations in which employees work particularly long hours tend to enjoy stronger economic growth, while paying a clear price in social wellbeing. I partly agree with this observation, because the link between long hours and output is real, yet the social costs such patterns generate often undermine the very prosperity they seem to create in the first place.

On the one hand, longer working hours can indeed lift a country's economic performance in the short term. When more labour is poured into factories, hospitals and offices, total production rises, and the additional output can finance better infrastructure, new technology and higher public investment that would otherwise be impossible. A clear example lies in several East Asian economies, where long working hours have historically been associated with rapid industrial growth and impressive gains in national income over a single generation. On the other hand, the social consequences of such demanding work routines frequently erode the foundations of that same prosperity in ways that are slow but difficult to reverse. Workers who rarely see their children struggle to transmit stable values across generations, and rising rates of stress, divorce and mental illness place growing pressure on healthcare systems and on public budgets. Were governments to celebrate long hours without addressing these deeper costs, the long-term vitality of their economies would deteriorate, and the headline figures would mask a quieter social decline that no spreadsheet can easily capture.

In conclusion, I believe that the connection between long working hours and economic success is only partial, because the social damage such patterns cause ultimately weakens the human capital on which lasting prosperity depends.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段结构，开头以 I partly agree with this observation 明确表态（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 与 Body 2 分别给出两条独立理由（短期产出 / 长期社会代价），结尾以 the connection between long working hours and economic success is only partial 重申立场，全文立场清晰且前后一致，字数 274 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
