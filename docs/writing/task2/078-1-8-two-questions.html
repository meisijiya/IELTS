Task 2 — Parental pressure on children (two-questions)

Nowadays, some parents put a lot of pressure on their children. Why is the reason for doing that? Is this a positive or negative development for the children? (2021.10.30)

立场: 总体是负面发展; 理由1: 父母施加压力的原因是社会竞争激烈、就业焦虑与自身期望投射; 理由2: 对孩子造成心理健康损害、亲子关系紧张，但适度引导仍有价值。

The aim of this essay is to examine why an increasing number of parents place heavy pressure on their children, and to evaluate whether this trend is a positive or negative development for those children.

Several factors help explain this growing phenomenon. Intense competition for limited places at top universities and elite careers has created an environment in which parents feel that any relaxation of effort may cost their child a prosperous future. Many mothers and fathers also project their own unfulfilled ambitions onto the next generation, believing that pushing children harder will spare them the hardships the parents themselves experienced in earlier decades. Finally, pervasive social comparison, amplified by social media feeds that constantly showcase other families' achievements, intensifies the fear of falling behind within their own community.

As to whether this development is positive or negative, I believe it is largely negative for most children, despite the good intentions behind it. Excessive pressure frequently undermines intrinsic motivation, leaving young people unable to study or pursue hobbies once external supervision is removed. Mental health problems such as anxiety, sleep disorders and burnout have become markedly more common among adolescents who report high parental expectations over long periods. Parent-child relationships also tend to deteriorate as open conversation gives way to constant negotiation over grades and test scores. Were this pattern to intensify over the coming years, society as a whole would inherit a generation struggling with chronic stress and fragile self-esteem.

In conclusion, although parents' anxiety is understandable, putting excessive pressure on children does more harm than good. A healthier approach combines reasonable expectations with emotional support, allowing young people to grow into capable and resilient adults.

TA：本文采用 two-questions 标准四段式：Introduction 以 The aim of this essay is to examine why ... and to evaluate whether ... 一次性点出两个问题，Body 1 回应「原因」（竞争激烈、父母投射、社会比较），Body 2 表明「总体负面」立场并展开论证（动机受损、心理问题、亲子关系），结尾 A healthier approach combines reasonable expectations with emotional support ... 总结并给出方向，任务回应完整、字数 283 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
