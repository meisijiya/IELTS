Task 2 — Work-life balance (problem-solution)

Currently, many people fail to balance work with other parts of their lives. What are the reasons and how to solve them? (2023.7.15)

立场: 失衡源于工作扩张与界限模糊; 理由1: 数字技术模糊边界与超时文化; 解决方案: 制度保护与个人边界谈判

The aim of this essay is to examine the main reasons why so many people today struggle to balance their work with other parts of their lives, and to propose some practical solutions that could help them to restore a healthier equilibrium.

There are two main reasons for this widespread imbalance. Firstly, digital technology has blurred the boundaries between the office and the home, so employees are expected to read messages, approve documents and attend calls long after their official hours have ended. A clear example is the way mobile email has turned the living room sofa into an extension of the workplace, leaving almost no period that is genuinely free from work. Secondly, the culture of long hours in many competitive industries quietly rewards those who sacrifice their personal time, making it risky for ambitious staff to switch off without damaging their careers. The pressure to appear indispensable drives people to accept heavier workloads than they can sensibly manage.

Two solutions could help to mitigate this problem. Governments should strengthen labour laws that limit after-hours contact and require employers to respect clear rest periods, while companies would benefit from auditing workloads to ensure that no team is consistently overstretched. On a personal level, individuals can negotiate realistic boundaries, such as agreeing not to reply to emails after a certain hour, and learning to prioritise sleep and family time. Were these reforms to be implemented together, ordinary workers would finally regain the breathing space that a balanced life requires.

In conclusion, the main reasons for poor work-life balance are blurred digital boundaries and a culture that glorifies overwork, and a combination of stronger regulation and firmer personal limits would be the most effective way to address them.

TA：本文采用 problem-solution 标准四段结构，开头以 The aim of this essay is to examine the main reasons ... and to propose some practical solutions 一次性点明两个问题（原因 / 解决方案），Body 1 以 digital technology / culture of long hours 回应原因，Body 2 以 labour laws / personal boundaries 回应解决方案，结尾以 the main reasons are ... and a combination ... would be the most effective way 总结两句答案，任务回应完整、字数 285 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
