Task 2 — Computer skills for success (agree-disagree)

If you want to be successful at work, you should have high-level computer skills. Do you agree or disagree? (2025.4.12)

It is often claimed that advanced computer skills are essential for anyone who wants to succeed in the modern workplace. While I accept that digital competence is now a basic requirement, I disagree with the idea that high-level computer skills are the only path to professional success, because many successful careers depend on other qualities instead.

There is no doubt that computers have reshaped almost every industry, and basic digital skills are now expected of nearly every employee. Knowing how to use common software, manage files online or handle electronic communication is no longer a special advantage but a minimum standard. However, calling for 'high-level' computer skills sets the bar much higher, and this overlooks the value of other core abilities that workers bring to their jobs every day.

In many professions, success depends on qualities that simply cannot be replaced by technical know-how. For doctors, nurses and care workers, the ability to listen carefully to patients, show genuine empathy and make calm decisions under pressure is what truly defines good performance in their daily routines. For teachers, classroom presence and the talent to explain ideas clearly matter more than any software they have mastered. Even in business, strong communication, leadership and creativity tend to be the skills that distinguish an outstanding manager from a merely competent one, and these qualities are not produced by upgrading one's computer ability alone.

In conclusion, I believe that digital literacy is necessary in today's workplace, but high-level computer skills are not the key to success for most people. A balanced set of human and practical skills will serve workers far better than narrow technical expertise.

TA：本题是 agree-disagree 题型，立场必须在引入段明确表态。开头用 "I disagree with the idea that..." 否定"高水平计算机技能是唯一成功路径"，结尾用 "I believe... high-level computer skills are not the key" 再次回扣立场，前后立场一致。

CC：四段分别承担"立场 / 反驳前提 / 替代因素 / 重申立场"的功能，段落之间用 However / Even in business / In conclusion 等衔接，链条清楚但稍显机械，符合 6 分档表现。LR：使用 digital competence / minimum standard / technical know-how / empathy / outstanding manager 等话题搭配，搭配准确且重复少；未出现明显的低频词硬塞。GRA：以定语从句 + 让步状语为主，主谓一致、被动语态、时态控制基本无误，没有出现严重语法错误，整体是 6–6.5 分档的稳定输出。

  • digital competence — 数字能力，替换 computer skills 的更书面表达。
  • modern workplace — 现代职场，全文背景词。
  • minimum standard — 最低门槛，对应基本数字技能。
  • high-level computer skills — 高水平计算机技能，题干核心词。
  • technical know-how — 技术诀窍 / 技能，与 human qualities 对比。
  • show empathy — 表达共情，护理 / 服务行业关键词。
  • classroom presence — 课堂表现力，教师职业关键能力。
  • outstanding manager — 出色管理者，对比 merely competent。
  • digital literacy — 数字素养，与 high-level computer skills 区分。
  • narrow technical expertise — 狭窄的技术专长，结尾与 balanced skills 对照。
