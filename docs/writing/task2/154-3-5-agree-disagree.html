Task 2 — Plan for future vs focus on present (agree-disagree)

Some people say that it is a waste of time to plan for the future and that it is more important to focus on the present. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2024.1.13)

立场: 不同意放弃未来规划; 理由1: 未来规划赋予当下方向感与安全感; 理由2: 即时满足导向易导致长期后悔

Some people argue that careful planning for the future is essentially a waste of time and that attention should remain firmly focused on the pleasures of the present. I strongly disagree with this view, because thoughtful planning and present enjoyment are not enemies but natural partners in a well-lived life.

The first reason is that future planning gives the present a much-needed sense of direction. A student who knows which career they want is far more motivated to study each day than a peer who drifts through subjects without a target. Financial planning works in the same way: a young adult who saves a modest portion of each salary avoids the anxiety that comes from sudden emergencies and gains the confidence to take reasonable risks. Planning, in other words, is not a denial of the present but the very tool that makes present effort feel meaningful and worthwhile at every stage of life.

The second reason is that an exclusively present-focused life tends to produce regret when irreversible decisions catch up with the individual. People who never think about retirement, family health or skill development often discover, in their forties or fifties, that they would have made different choices had they paused to plan. Were this pattern of pure present-focus to become widespread across society, social problems such as elderly poverty and mid-career unemployment would steadily deepen, and individuals would ultimately bear costs that no amount of short-term enjoyment could ever compensate for.

In conclusion, I still believe that thoughtful planning for the future is a wise investment of time, and that it actually enriches the present in subtle ways rather than detracting from it in any sense.
