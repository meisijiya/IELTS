Task 2 — Public libraries vs computer technology (agree-disagree)

Maintaining public libraries is a waste of time since computer technology is now replacing their function. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.7.1)

立场: 不同意; 理由1: 图书馆提供平等公共空间与免费资源; 理由2: 图书馆筛选文化内容并凝聚社区

Some people argue that maintaining public libraries is a waste of time because computer technology is replacing their function. I firmly disagree with this view, since libraries offer community value and educational support that screens alone cannot deliver, and abandoning them would seriously harm the citizens who depend on them most.

My first reason is that public libraries serve as equalising public spaces, where any citizen can read, study or use the internet without paying a fee. For children from low-income families, the library is often the only quiet place where homework can be completed after school, and where a kind librarian can recommend a book that sparks a lifelong reading habit. A clear example is the way public lending systems have launched countless readers who later became writers, scientists and teachers. Without libraries, this democratic gateway to knowledge would quietly close for millions of people who already struggle to afford private resources.

My second reason is that libraries curate and preserve cultural memory in a way that scattered online content cannot. Trained librarians select quality material, organise local archives, lead children's story hours and host author readings that bring neighbourhoods together in shared experience. Were governments to defund these institutions on the grounds that search engines have replaced them, communities would lose a trusted institution that filters information, models careful reading and passes shared stories from one generation to the next, leaving citizens more vulnerable to misinformation and cultural amnesia.

In conclusion, I still believe that public libraries remain an essential public service, and computer technology should complement rather than replace the irreplaceable role they play in our communities.
