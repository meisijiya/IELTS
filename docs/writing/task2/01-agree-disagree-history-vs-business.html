Task 2 — History vs business (agree-disagree)

Some people think that there could be more benefit to society if more people studied business than history. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2025.1.18 / 2022.12.17)

Some people argue that society would benefit more if a greater number of students chose to study business rather than history. In my opinion, I disagree with this view, because history provides knowledge and values that commercial subjects cannot replace.

There are two main reasons why I hold this position. First, studying history helps people understand the present by looking at the past. Every decision a government, a company, or an individual makes today is shaped by events that came before. For example, a country that learns from the economic mistakes of the 1930s is less likely to repeat them in a financial crisis. Without this kind of historical awareness, business leaders might treat short-term profit as the only goal and ignore long-term consequences such as environmental damage or social inequality.

Second, history trains the mind in a way that business courses often do not. Reading primary sources, comparing different accounts, and writing analytical essays all require careful thought and clear expression. These skills are useful not only in business but also in law, medicine, journalism, and public service. A business graduate who has never studied the past may know how to read a balance sheet, yet may struggle to explain a complex idea to colleagues or to judge a company policy's wider impact.

I do accept that business studies have practical value, especially for those who wish to start their own companies or work in finance. Nevertheless, this practical edge does not make history less important for society as a whole.

In conclusion, I still believe that history should continue to receive attention in education. It teaches citizens how to think, how to learn from failure, and how to build a wiser future.

TA：本文为 Task 2「同意与否」题型，立场在开头以 In my opinion, I disagree 明确表态（避免 whether），并在结尾用 I still believe 再次呼应，TA 满足字数与立场要求。

CC：段落分明、每段一个主题；衔接词 First / Second / I do accept / In conclusion 清晰但偏机械，符合 6 分水平。LR：以基础话题词（society、benefit、students、knowledge）为主，配少量替换（commercial subjects、historical awareness），未使用低频高难词。GRA：以简单复合句为主，从句与并列结构控制尚可，整体清晰、无明显语法错误，是一份稳定的 6 分范本。
