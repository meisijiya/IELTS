Task 2 — Museums local vs foreign works (agree-disagree)

Museums and art galleries should concentrate on local works rather than showing the cultures or artworks from other countries. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2022.12.10)

立场: 不同意仅聚焦本土; 理由1: 跨文化展品拓宽视野、培养国际理解; 理由2: 文化交流提升软实力与开放形象

Many people argue that museums and art galleries should focus exclusively on local works rather than displaying artworks from other countries. I firmly disagree with this view, because such a narrow focus would impoverish public understanding of the wider world and weaken the cultural exchange that museums exist to encourage.

The first reason is that exposure to foreign art broadens visitors' horizons and cultivates cross-cultural understanding, a benefit local-only collections simply cannot deliver. When a schoolchild in Beijing stands before an African mask or a Renaissance altarpiece, the encounter plants a seed of curiosity about distant peoples and traditions, an attitude that classrooms alone rarely produce. Were museums to remove these objects, audiences would be left with a parochial view of human creativity, assuming artistic merit ends at their own borders. A clear example is the British Museum, whose Egyptian and Assyrian galleries draw larger crowds than its British rooms, showing that global displays attract visitors rather than repel them.

The second reason is that exhibiting foreign works positions a country as a hub of cultural dialogue, conferring both soft power and diplomatic goodwill. Nations that welcome global art signal openness, intellectual confidence and respect for other civilisations, qualities that resonate strongly in an interconnected world. When the Louvre hangs Chinese scroll paintings beside European masterpieces, it tells audiences that great art is a shared inheritance, not the property of any single nation. Were a country to retreat into purely local collections, it would forfeit this influence and project a parochial image abroad.

In conclusion, I still believe museums should continue displaying works from many cultures, because doing so broadens minds, fosters mutual understanding and projects an image of cultural confidence benefiting the host nation.
