Task 2 — Hard work for money (agree-disagree)

The only reason for people working hard is to earn money. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.10.17)

立场: 不同意, 金钱并非唯一动力; 理由1: 自我实现与精通感驱动努力; 理由2: 家庭责任与社会贡献同样重要

It is sometimes argued that the sole reason people work hard is to earn money. While financial reward is undeniably a powerful motivator, I firmly disagree with this view, because there are several other compelling reasons that drive people to put in sustained effort at work.

The first reason is that many people work hard to achieve a sense of personal fulfilment and self-respect. Completing a demanding task or mastering a difficult skill can in itself be deeply satisfying, regardless of the pay attached to it, and this intrinsic reward often sustains effort over many years. A clear example is the long hours that researchers, artists and athletes willingly devote to their craft, even when their fields rarely lead to financial security. Were pay to be their only goal, they would long since have switched to more lucrative careers and abandoned their chosen pursuits.

The second reason is that work often serves a social purpose, allowing individuals to support their families and contribute to the wider community. Parents frequently take on demanding jobs so that their children can enjoy better education and a safer upbringing, while doctors and engineers work tirelessly to mitigate problems that affect millions of strangers. These motivations have little to do with personal gain and a great deal to do with a sense of duty that many professionals carry throughout their working lives, shaping their willingness to make sacrifices whenever difficult circumstances demand it.

In conclusion, I still maintain that money is far from the only reason for working hard, since fulfilment, family responsibility and broader social contribution are equally powerful forces behind people's professional effort.
