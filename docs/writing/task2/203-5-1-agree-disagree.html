Task 2 — Smartphones: disadvantages vs advantages (agree-disagree)

Many people think technological devices such as smart phones, tablets and mobile phones bring more disadvantages than advantages. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2023.7.1)

立场: 不同意, 总体利大于弊; 理由1: 沟通效率与终身学习得以大幅提升; 理由2: 移动支付与公共服务接入惠及普通民众

Some critics argue that smartphones, tablets and similar mobile devices have brought more harm than good to our daily lives. I firmly disagree with this view, because such gadgets have made communication faster, learning more accessible and essential services available to people who previously had almost no access to them at all.

My first reason is that these devices have transformed how we communicate and learn. A parent stuck in traffic can now join a virtual parent-teacher meeting, while a rural student can watch recorded lectures from top universities without leaving home. A clear example is the surge in online micro-courses taken on smartphones, which has allowed millions of working adults to gain new qualifications at a fraction of the cost of traditional programmes. Without these portable tools, lifelong learning would remain a privilege of the few rather than a realistic option for the many.

My second reason is that smartphones give ordinary citizens practical access to finance, healthcare and government services that were once locked behind office doors. Mobile payment apps allow market vendors and small farmers to receive money safely without needing a bank branch, while health-booking platforms let elderly patients consult a doctor from their living room at any hour of the day. Were governments and businesses to roll back these services, the poorest communities would lose a genuine lifeline, widening the gap between rich and poor.

In conclusion, I still believe that smartphones and similar devices offer clear benefits to individuals and society, and their disadvantages can be mitigated through sensible screen-time habits, careful digital education and patient parenting rather than wholesale rejection of the technology.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段结构，开头以 I firmly disagree 明确表态，Body 1 与 Body 2 分别给出两条独立理由（沟通学习 / 公共服务），结尾以 I still believe 再次呼应立场，全文立场贯穿无摇摆；正文 274 词落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
