Task 2 — Changing careers (positive-negative)

An increasing number of people are changing careers during their working life. What are the reasons for this? Is it a positive or negative development? (2025.1.4)

In recent years, it has become increasingly common for adults to switch careers several times during their working life rather than stay in one profession for decades. This essay will first examine the main reasons for this trend and then explain why I believe it is a largely positive development.

There are several reasons why more people choose to change careers today. First, the rapid growth of technology means that many traditional jobs are disappearing or being reshaped, so workers often have to learn new skills and move into different fields simply to stay employed. Second, younger generations tend to value personal fulfilment more than their parents did, and they are willing to leave a well-paid position if it no longer matches their interests. Third, modern communication tools and online courses have made it much easier to retrain at any age, lowering the practical barriers to starting again in a new industry.

I consider this trend to be a positive development for both individuals and society. For individuals, changing careers allows people to discover their true strengths, avoid the boredom of doing the same task for thirty years, and recover from a wrong choice made early in life. For society, a workforce that can move between sectors is more flexible and innovative, which helps economies adapt to change. Workers who switch jobs also tend to bring fresh ideas from their previous experience, enriching the teams they join.

In conclusion, although career changes can be stressful in the short term, the underlying reasons are closely tied to technological progress and a healthier attitude towards personal growth. Overall, the benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks, and I welcome this development.

TA：本题是 positive-negative 复合题型，必须先答"原因"再给"正面 / 负面"判断并展开。本文 Body 1 用三条原因回答 What are the reasons（技术替代 / 个人追求 / 培训门槛降低），Body 2 从个人 + 社会两层面给出 positive 判断并在结尾再次明确表态"In my view... a positive development / Overall, the benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks"，结构完整。

CC：四段分别承担"引入 / 原因 / 利弊 / 结论"的功能，段内主题—理由—例证—小结的链条清晰；衔接词 First / Second / Third / For individuals / For society / In conclusion 均属基础连接，没有刻意炫技，符合 6 分档"清晰但机械"的特征。LR：使用 switch careers / personal fulfilment / retrain / workforce / sectors / innovative 等话题搭配，搭配合理，没有重复堆叠相同词根。GRA：复合句以定语从句 + 让步状语为主，主谓一致、only 位置、时态控制基本无误，没有出现严重语法错误，属于 6–6.5 分档的稳定输出。

  • switch careers — 转换职业，替换 change jobs 的更书面表达。
  • working life — 职业生涯，全文主轴词。
  • personal fulfilment — 个人成就感，对应"个人追求"。
  • retrain — 重新培训，指工作后再学习新技能。
  • traditional jobs — 传统岗位，与新兴岗位对比。
  • workforce — 劳动力 / 员工队伍，整体描述就业人群。
  • flexible and innovative — 灵活且具创新性，形容劳动力素质。
  • technological progress — 技术进步，解释结构性原因。
  • benefits outweigh the drawbacks — 利大于弊，结尾总结句型。
