Task 2 — Schools obsolete with internet (agree-disagree)

Since people can study through the internet, schools have become unnecessary. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.12.30)

立场: 学校仍然不可或缺，internet 只能辅助; 理由1: 学校提供结构化教学与教师引导，帮助筛选和消化在线海量信息; 理由2: 学校培养社交能力与性格发展，是屏幕学习无法替代的。

It is often claimed that, with the wealth of information now available online, traditional schools have become redundant. I disagree with this assertion because, although the internet is undoubtedly a valuable educational resource, schools provide structured learning and crucial social development that virtual platforms cannot fully replicate.

The first reason schools remain indispensable is that they offer a guided and disciplined learning environment. The internet presents learners with an overwhelming volume of material, much of which is unverified, biased or simply unsuitable for young audiences. Without a qualified teacher to explain concepts, correct misunderstandings and pace instruction, many students would struggle to distinguish reliable sources from misleading ones. A compelling argument can therefore be made that formal schooling, whose curriculum is shaped by experienced educators, equips young people with the critical judgment required to evaluate online content themselves. Schools, in this sense, do not compete with the internet; rather, they teach students how to use it wisely.

Furthermore, schools foster social and emotional growth in ways that solitary screen-based study cannot. When children spend their formative years in classrooms with peers, they gradually learn cooperation, patience and how to resolve disagreements, qualities that employers and universities continue to value. Extracurricular activities such as team sports, music ensembles and debating societies shape character and forge lasting friendships. Were students to remain at home studying only through a screen, their interpersonal skills would almost certainly deteriorate, leaving them ill-prepared for adult collaboration.

In conclusion, I firmly believe that schools remain essential despite the rapid growth of online learning. The internet may supplement classroom education, but it cannot replace the structured guidance and shared social environment that only physical schools can provide.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段式：Introduction 以 I disagree with this assertion because ... 一次性表明立场（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 从「结构化教学与信息筛选」回应 internet 是否替代 schools 的命题，Body 2 从「社交与情感成长」切入，结尾 schools remain essential ... the internet may supplement ... but it cannot replace 重申立场；任务回应完整、字数 280 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段衔接以 The first reason ... / Furthermore, ... / In conclusion, ... 显式逻辑词过渡，每段单一主题，分段合理。LR：使用 redundant / overwhelming volume / compelling argument / critical judgment / extracurricular activities / deteriorate / interpersonal skills 等 7 分段话题词，有 redundant ↔ unnecessary / structured learning ↔ guided and disciplined learning environment 等同义替换，未堆砌难词。GRA：复合句使用准确：定语从句（schools, whose curriculum ...）、虚拟条件（Were students to remain ...）、现在分词短语（A compelling argument can therefore be made that ...），定冠词、介词与单复数使用稳定，GRA 估测 7 档。
