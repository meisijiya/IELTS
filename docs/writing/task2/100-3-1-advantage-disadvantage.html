Task 2 — Zoned city planning (advantage-disadvantage)

In many cities, planners tend to arrange shops, schools, offices, and homes in specific areas and separate them from each other. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? (2022.4.23)

立场: 优点大于缺点; 理由1: 功能分区减少工业污染外溢，提高居住舒适度; 理由2: 但缺点是出行距离增加、街区失去活力

The question of separating residential, commercial, educational and office functions into distinct zones has both supporters and critics. While strict zoning creates clear drawbacks, there are also compelling benefits that deserve careful consideration in any modern city that hopes to remain healthy and liveable.

The main advantage is that functional separation protects residents from the worst effects of industrial pollution, traffic noise and late-night congestion in their daily lives. When factories, busy roads and crowded nightlife venues are kept at a deliberate distance from family homes, air quality improves noticeably, sleep is far less disturbed and property values remain stable over time. A clear example is the post-war reconstruction of Stockholm, where planners ring-fenced heavy industry along the harbour and reserved quiet inland districts for housing, an arrangement that still supports one of Europe's healthiest and most contented urban populations.

The main disadvantage is that strict zoning lengthens the distances people must travel each day and can gradually erode the vitality of local neighbourhoods. Once shops, workplaces and homes are placed in separate pockets, residents depend heavily on cars, rush-hour traffic intensifies on major roads, and street life thins out after office hours, leaving residential blocks feeling deserted and isolated. This pattern is familiar in many North American suburbs, where the absence of corner shops and small parks has been linked by researchers to higher rates of loneliness and sedentary lifestyles among both adults and children alike.

On balance, I believe the advantages of functional separation outweigh the disadvantages, because the health benefits of clean residential districts are long-lasting and affect everyone, while the social drawbacks can be mitigated by adding parks, schools and small convenience stores back into housing zones.
