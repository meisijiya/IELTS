Task 2 — Children given more freedom

In many parts of the world, children are given more freedom than in the past. Is this a positive or negative development? (2025.4.19)

In many parts of the world, parents today give their children far more freedom in everyday life than previous generations received. Although this shift inevitably causes concern among older relatives and educators, I would argue that, on the whole, it is a positive development that benefits both individuals and society.

The principal advantage is that autonomy helps children develop independence and stronger judgement. When youngsters are trusted to manage their own time, choose their hobbies or travel short distances without constant supervision, they gradually learn to weigh options, handle small setbacks and accept the consequences of their decisions. These are precisely the skills that classroom instruction alone cannot easily teach. In addition, modern workplaces increasingly value initiative, adaptability and creative problem-solving, so children who have practised making decisions on their own tend to adapt more easily to university life and future careers.

Admittedly, granting children more freedom does carry genuine risks: parents naturally worry about exposure to harmful online content, unhealthy peer pressure and even physical safety in busy public spaces. Yet these concerns are better addressed through open communication and sensible boundaries than by returning to the highly restrictive parenting styles of previous decades. Children who are never allowed to take even a calculated risk often grow into anxious adults who struggle with ordinary choices, from managing money to forming close relationships. A balanced approach, offering clear guidance while respecting a child's growing autonomy, therefore produces better long-term outcomes than either extreme.

In conclusion, despite the genuine worries it raises, the trend towards giving children greater freedom is largely positive, because it builds the resilience and self-reliance that young people need in today's society.
