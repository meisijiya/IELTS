Task 2 — Protecting only useful animals (agree-disagree)

Many animals are endangered. Some people argue that we should only protect animals that are useful to humans. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.3.4)

立场: 不同意仅保护有用动物; 理由1: 每种动物在生态系统中都有不可替代的作用，盲目取舍会引发连锁危机; 理由2: 物种的科学医学价值常在意料之外，灭绝亦违背人类对生命的道德责任。

Many of the world's animal species are currently endangered, and some people argue that we should only protect those species that are useful to humans. While this proposal has a certain practical appeal, I firmly disagree with it, because every species contributes to natural systems on which human wellbeing ultimately depends.

The first reason is that animals often judged useless today play irreplaceable roles in maintaining healthy ecosystems. Bees, earthworms and many so-called pests recycle nutrients, pollinate crops and regulate plant growth, quietly sustaining the agricultural productivity on which human food supplies rely. If a single link in such a food web is removed, the consequences for the wider system are notoriously hard to predict. A clear example is the disappearance of vultures in parts of South Asia, once dismissed as worthless scavengers; their absence allowed disease-carrying rats to multiply and eventually triggered serious public health crises that nobody had foreseen.

The second reason is that species once thought dispensable frequently turn out to have unforeseen benefits for science, medicine and industry. Research on the horseshoe crab, long dismissed as a primitive creature, produced a compound now used to test the safety of vaccines and medical implants worldwide. Beyond such practical discoveries, humans bear a moral duty to respect the living creatures with whom they share the planet, and allowing an entire species to vanish simply because it is not commercially profitable reflects an unacceptably narrow view of progress.

In conclusion, I still believe that endangered animals of every kind deserve protection, since their ecological, scientific and moral value far exceeds any narrow calculation of immediate usefulness to human beings.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段式：开头以 I firmly disagree with it 一次性表态，避免 whether 开头；Body 1 回应「生态链中断」并以南亚秃鹫案例支撑，Body 2 回应「科学价值 + 道德责任」并以鲎为反例，结尾以 I still believe that endangered animals of every kind deserve protection 再次呼应立场，立场贯穿无摇摆，字数 282 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
