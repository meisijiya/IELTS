Task 2 — Personal info online (positive-negative)

Today, many people put their personal and private information online (address, phone number, and credit card number) to facilitate everyday activities, such as banking, shopping, and socializing. Is this a positive or negative development? (2022.3.3)

Nowadays, an increasing number of people share their personal details, such as home addresses, phone numbers and credit card information, on the internet in order to make banking, shopping and social activities easier. In my opinion, this trend is largely a negative development, despite the convenience it brings.

The main problem is the risk of personal information being misused. Once data such as a credit card number or home address is stored online, it can be stolen by hackers, sold to companies that send unwanted advertisements, or even traded on illegal markets. In some serious cases, this kind of information has been used for fraud or identity theft, causing victims to lose money, damage their reputation, or suffer serious stress that affects their work and family life. Even when a well-known company promises to keep the data safe, no online system is completely free from the risk of leakage, and ordinary users often have little idea who can actually see their information or how it will be used in the future.

Admittedly, sharing personal data online does make daily life easier. Online banking saves a trip to the bank, online shopping saves the time and effort of carrying heavy bags, and social apps help friends and family stay in touch across long distances. However, these benefits come at the cost of privacy, and most users only realise the danger after their information has already been exposed and cannot be taken back.

In conclusion, while the convenience of sharing personal information online is real, the serious risks of data misuse and loss of privacy make this trend more negative than positive for individuals and for society as a whole.

TA：本题为「正面/负面」题型，立场在首段以 In my opinion, this trend is largely a negative development 明示，结尾 more negative than positive 呼应，TA 完整。

CC：四段结构清晰（立场 → 风险 → 承认好处 → 总结），衔接词 The main problem / Admittedly / However / In conclusion 自然，符合 6 分水平。LR：用 misuse、leakage、fraud、privacy 等话题词，搭配得当，无低频生词堆砌。GRA：复合句与并列句并用，从句（that、which 引导）控制稳定，整体清晰、无明显语法错误，是一份稳定的 6 分范本。

  • credit card number — 信用卡号
  • positive or negative development — 正面或负面发展
  • data misuse — 数据滥用
  • loss of privacy — 隐私泄露
  • online banking — 网上银行
  • risk of leakage — 泄露风险
  • fraud — 欺诈
