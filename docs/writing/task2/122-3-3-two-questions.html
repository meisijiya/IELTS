Task 2 — Longer life expectancy effects (two-questions)

A recent survey shows that in many countries across the world people are generally living longer. But an increased life expectancy has many implications for the aging individuals and for the society as a whole. What are the possible effects of a longer life expectancy on individuals and on society? (2021.1.21)

立场: 问题需要积极应对; 理由1: 个人层面带来健康、家庭与经济的多重压力，但也增加终身学习与新角色机会; 理由2: 社会层面财政负担上升，但人口经验红利可被激活

The aim of this essay is to discuss the possible effects of an increased life expectancy on aging individuals and on the society they live in.

For individuals, longer lives bring a mixture of opportunities and pressures that did not exist a generation ago. Many people can now expect to live well into their eighties or even nineties, which means they must fund much longer retirements, sustain their physical health for additional decades and, increasingly, support adult children who struggle to become financially independent in a difficult housing market. At the same time, an extended lifespan creates room for second careers, late-blooming hobbies and greater involvement in raising grandchildren, allowing older adults to redefine personal purpose in later life.

For society as a whole, the implications are equally significant and largely financial in character. Pension systems designed when most workers retired at sixty now face the prospect of paying benefits for an additional twenty or thirty years, pushing public budgets towards persistent deficit. Demand for healthcare, long-term care and age-friendly housing rises sharply across the nation, while the ratio of working-age taxpayers to retirees shrinks every single year. Nevertheless, longer lives also produce an experienced older workforce that, with reasonable adjustments, can continue to contribute to the economy, mentoring younger colleagues and serving local communities through voluntary work and active civic engagement.

In conclusion, a longer life expectancy reshapes both personal biographies and public policy in profound ways. Individuals must prepare financially and emotionally for much longer retirements, while societies need to reform pension and healthcare systems so that demographic ageing becomes a shared opportunity rather than a looming threat.

TA：本文采用 two-questions 标准四段结构，Intro 以 The aim of this essay is to discuss the possible effects ... on aging individuals and on the society 一次性点出两个问题（个人 / 社会），Body 1 回应个人层面（财务压力、第二职业、隔代抚养），Body 2 回应社会层面（养老金、医疗、经验型老年劳动力），结尾 Individuals must prepare ... while societies need to reform ... 给出方向，任务回应完整、字数 280 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段式结构清晰，每段单一主题；衔接以 For individuals / For society as a whole / At the same time / Nevertheless / In conclusion 等显式逻辑词过渡，行文推进有层次，CC 估测 7 档。LR：使用 life expectancy / financial independence / second careers / public budgets / demographic ageing / voluntary work / pension systems 等话题搭配，搭配准确且有同义替换（aging individuals ↔ older adults / older workforce ↔ experienced older workforce），LR 估测 7 档。GRA：复合句占比较高，包含定语从句（a generation ago, which means they must fund longer retirements）、让步状语（while the ratio of working-age taxpayers to retirees shrinks）、介词短语作状语（with reasonable adjustments, can continue to contribute）；主谓一致、时态与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
