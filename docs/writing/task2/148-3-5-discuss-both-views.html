Task 2 — Success: hard work vs money or looks (discuss-both-views)

Some people think that success of life is based on hard work and determination. Others, however, believe that there are more important factors such as having money or good appearance. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. (2024.3.9)

立场: 同意努力是底层密码; 理由1: 努力带来可迁移的技能与韧性; 理由2: 金钱外貌只在特定行业起作用且难以持久

What ultimately determines a successful life is a question that divides opinion. Some people insist that hard work and determination form the real foundation of any meaningful success, while others maintain that inherited wealth or even physical attractiveness carry greater weight in modern society.

Those who emphasise hard work point to the durable skills it produces. An individual who has spent years mastering a craft, a sport or a research field develops a level of competence that cannot be bought overnight. Determination also builds resilience, allowing people to recover from setbacks that would defeat less persistent competitors. A clear example is the many successful entrepreneurs who failed several times before their business finally took off; their disciplined habits, not their bank balances at birth, accounted for the eventual breakthrough and for the lasting influence of their work.

Advocates of the wealth-and-looks view argue that money and appearance often open doors that effort alone cannot unlock. A well-connected family can fund education, networks and political access, while attractive individuals frequently receive promotions, scholarships and social invitations unavailable to their less striking peers. Were merit the only currency that mattered, the entertainment and finance industries would not consistently reward inherited wealth and photogenic faces as they currently do in practice.

In my view, hard work and determination matter far more than wealth or appearance in the long run, because only personal effort produces capabilities that survive changing circumstances.

In conclusion, money and looks can certainly accelerate the early stages of success, but the deepest and most stable achievements still belong to those who combine clear ambition with patient, relentless discipline over many years.
