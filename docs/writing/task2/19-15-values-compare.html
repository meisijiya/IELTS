Task 2 — Dependent vs independent (discuss-both-views)

Some people think individuals are more and more dependent on each other. Others believe we are more and more independent. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. (2021.7.24)

There is a common debate today about whether modern people are becoming more dependent on one another or, on the contrary, more independent. Both views contain some truth, and this essay will discuss them before giving my own opinion.

Those who argue that we are more dependent often point to technology and the workplace. Thanks to smartphones and social apps, friends now stay in touch every day and share almost every small event in their lives, which suggests a closer personal bond than ever before. At work, modern projects usually need people from different departments, or even different countries, to cooperate closely. A single product, such as a new car, may involve designers, engineers, marketers and sales teams from many cities around the world, so no one worker can complete the task alone. From this angle, modern life clearly requires greater mutual reliance between people.

However, there are equally strong signs that people are also becoming more independent. Many routine tasks, from shopping and banking to learning a new skill, can now be done alone through the internet, without asking anyone for help or leaving the house. Living alone is also more common than in the past, and a growing number of adults choose to travel, study, or even start a business on their own. These choices show that modern individuals are more willing, and more able, to manage their own lives without constant support from family or friends.

In my view, both trends are happening at the same time. We depend on others more in our work and social networks, yet we also enjoy greater personal freedom in our private lives, and this balance is likely to continue.

TA：本题为「双方讨论」题型，需呈现两种观点并给出作者立场。文中以 Both views contain some truth 引出，正文两段分别论述依赖与独立，结尾 In my view, both trends are happening at the same time 明确表态，TA 完整。

CC：四段结构清晰（引言 → 依赖 → 独立 → 总结），衔接词 Those who argue / However / In my view 自然，符合 6 分水平。LR：用 mutual reliance、social networks、personal freedom 等常见搭配，词汇准确不堆砌。GRA：复合句与并列句并用，从句（such as、who 引导）控制稳定，整体清晰，是一份稳定的 6 分范本。
