Task 2 — Longer retirement problems (problem-solution)

Today, more people are living longer after they retire from work. What problems will it cause for individuals and society? What can be done to prevent it? (2022.6.11)

立场: 问题需要提前干预; 理由1: 个人层面易出现养老金不足与社会孤立; 理由2: 社会层面医疗与福利负担加重，应通过延迟退休与终身学习缓解

The aim of this essay is to analyse the problems that longer post-retirement lives create for individuals and society, and to suggest some realistic measures that governments and individuals can take to address them.

For retired people themselves, two problems stand out. Pension savings that were planned to last fifteen or twenty years now have to cover twenty-five or thirty, and many retirees discover that their private funds run dry well before the end of their lives, forcing them to rely on family handouts or state welfare. Equally important is the problem of social isolation: without the daily rhythm of work, many older adults lose contact with colleagues and drift into loneliness, which research consistently links to depression, dementia and a faster deterioration of physical health.

For society, the most serious consequence is the strain placed on public budgets and healthcare systems. Pension payments, long-term care facilities and age-related medical treatment absorb an ever-larger share of tax revenue, while the shrinking ratio of working taxpayers to retirees makes it harder to balance the books. The most effective response is a combination of policy and personal action. Governments can gradually raise the retirement age, encourage flexible work arrangements for older employees and fund community programmes that keep retirees socially engaged. Individuals, in turn, should plan financially for at least thirty years of retirement and embrace hobbies, voluntary work or part-time jobs that keep them mentally and socially active.

In conclusion, longer retirements bring real financial and social risks, but with gradual retirement reform and better personal preparation these problems can be mitigated, allowing older citizens to enjoy their extra years in dignity.
