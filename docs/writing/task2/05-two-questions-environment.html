Task 2 — Natural resources over-consumption (two questions)

The natural resources such as oil, forests and freshwater are being consumed at an alarming rate. What problems does this cause? How can we solve these problems? (2021.9.11)

The aim of this essay is to discuss the main problems caused by the rapid consumption of natural resources such as oil, forests and freshwater, and to suggest practical ways in which these problems can be solved.

There are two kinds of problems caused by this over-consumption. The first is environmental. Large-scale deforestation removes the trees that absorb carbon dioxide, speeds up climate change and destroys the habitats of many wild animals and plants. The heavy burning of oil and gas adds even more greenhouse gases, and the over-use of freshwater in farming and industry is lowering river and lake levels across many regions, sometimes below the level needed by local communities. The second is economic: as these resources become scarcer, energy, food and clean water all grow more expensive, and the heaviest burden falls on lower-income households who spend a larger share of their income on these basic needs.

Solving these problems requires action from both governments and individuals. Governments should pass stricter laws to protect remaining forests, place clear limits on the extraction of oil and gas, and require factories and farms to use water more efficiently. Public investment in renewable sources such as solar and wind power would also reduce dependence on fossil fuels over time, and international agreements could help poorer countries adopt cleaner technology. Individuals can play their part by using public transport whenever possible, switching off unused electronics, recycling paper and plastic, and choosing products from companies that follow sustainable practices.

In conclusion, the over-consumption of natural resources causes serious environmental and economic damage, but with stronger government policies and wider changes in everyday habits, these problems can be tackled effectively.

TA：范文采用 two-part question 标准结构，Introduction 以 The aim of this essay is to ... and ... 句型一次性点明"问题成因 + 解决方案"两问任务，Body 1 用"environmental（deforestation / greenhouse gases / over-use of freshwater）+ economic（rising prices / lower-income households）"两层证据回应第一问，Body 2 用"government action（laws / renewable investment / international agreements）+ individual habits（public transport / recycling / sustainable products）"回应第二问，结尾以 both ... and ... 总结两问答案，任务回应完整、字数落在 270–290 区间。

CC：四段式（Introduction–Body 1–Body 2–Conclusion），每段一个明确主题；衔接以 The first is ... / The second is ... / On the government side / At the individual level / In conclusion 等显式逻辑词过渡，整体连贯但偏机械，CC 估测 6 档。LR：使用 deforestation / carbon dioxide / greenhouse gases / renewable sources / fossil fuels / sustainable practices / lower-income households / habitats 等话题词，有 rapid consumption ↔ over-consumption / scarcer ↔ more expensive / pass stricter laws ↔ require factories to ... 等同义替换，未堆砌难词。GRA：简单句与定语从句（the trees that absorb ... / companies that follow ...）、条件状语（whenever possible）、被动结构混合；定冠词、单复数与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显错误，GRA 估测 6–6.5 档。
