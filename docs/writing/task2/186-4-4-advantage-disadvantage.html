Task 2 — Energy extraction in remote areas (advantage-disadvantage)

With the increasing demand for energy such as oil and gas, people start to look for their sources in remote and untouched places. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? (2022.6.26)

立场: 弊大于利, 在生态脆弱区开采化石能源得不偿失; 理由1: 短期内可获能源安全与经济收益; 理由2: 长期则破坏原始生态、加剧气候危机、侵犯原住民权益，与全球减碳目标背道而驰。

As global demand for oil and gas continues to rise, energy companies are turning to increasingly remote and ecologically sensitive regions for new supplies. While exploiting these reserves offers short-term economic rewards, on balance I believe that the disadvantages clearly outweigh the advantages, because the long-term environmental and social costs are simply too high to justify.

On the positive side, developing remote energy sources can strengthen national energy security and reduce dependence on politically volatile suppliers. The infrastructure that follows — pipelines, ports, roads and processing plants — also creates local employment, attracts investment and can raise living standards in previously underdeveloped regions. For communities that have long lacked basic services, the royalties and tax revenues generated by such projects may fund hospitals, schools and broadband connections that would otherwise arrive only after many years.

These gains, however, are dwarfed by the drawbacks. Drilling in the Arctic, deep rainforest or pristine offshore waters risks spills and leaks that are almost impossible to clean up, threatening wildlife such as polar bears, orang-utans and rare seabirds that depend on those habitats. Such projects also accelerate climate change, the very problem that policymakers claim to be addressing, by locking economies into fossil fuels for decades. Indigenous peoples living in these regions frequently lose ancestral lands and livelihoods without genuinely free consent, raising serious human-rights concerns. Were the industry to invest the same capital in wind, solar and battery storage located closer to demand, the long-term outcome would be safer and more sustainable.

In conclusion, although tapping remote reserves can deliver short-term energy and economic benefits, the ecological damage, climate impact and social injustice they cause mean that, on balance, the disadvantages clearly outweigh the advantages for both present and future generations.

TA：本文采用 advantage-disadvantage 标准四段式：Intro 以 on balance I believe that the disadvantages clearly outweigh the advantages 暗示要做权衡，Body 1 阐述 advantages（能源安全、就业、税源），Body 2 用 dwarfed by the drawbacks + 三个论点（生态泄漏 / 气候 / 原住民权益）阐述 disadvantages，结尾 on balance, the disadvantages clearly outweigh the advantages 给出明确权重判断，字数 286 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段衔接以 On the positive side / The infrastructure that follows / These gains, however, are dwarfed by / Were the industry to invest / In conclusion 显式逻辑词过渡；段内让步分句（even when mitigation measures are in place）与反事实虚拟（Were the industry to invest the same capital ...）推动论述，层次清晰。LR：使用 energy security / ecologically sensitive / processing plant / tax revenue / fossil fuel / ancestral lands / free consent / wind and solar 等 7 分段话题词，搭配准确且有 reserves ↔ supplies / drawback ↔ disadvantage 同义替换。GRA：复合句比例高，包括定语从句（communities that have long lacked basic services）、让步状语（that depend on those habitats）、反事实虚拟倒装（Were the industry to invest the same capital ...），时态与主谓一致准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
