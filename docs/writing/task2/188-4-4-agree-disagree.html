Task 2 — Discouraging non-essential flights (agree-disagree)

Long-distance flight consumes the amount of fuel that a car uses in many years and pollutes the air. Some people think that we should discourage non-essential flights such as tourist travel, rather than limit the use of cars. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2024.12.7)

立场: 强烈同意应优先限制不必要的航班, 但不应忽视私家车; 理由1: 单次长途飞行的碳排放远超私家车, 且缺乏电池或电动替代方案; 理由2: 私家车使用基数大, 应同步推行公交与电动化, 双管齐下而非互斥。

Some commentators have argued that, because long-distance flights burn enormous quantities of fuel and contaminate the upper atmosphere, governments should prioritise discouraging non-essential air travel rather than restricting everyday car use. While no transport policy should ignore private vehicles, I substantially agree with this view, since the per-trip environmental cost of a flight is far greater than that of an average car journey, and realistic low-carbon alternatives to flying already exist.

The first reason is that flying produces a disproportionately heavy climate footprint. A single return trip across the Atlantic can release more carbon dioxide than several years of commuting by car, and high-altitude emissions also produce extra warming through non-CO2 effects. Crucially, aviation has no widely available low-carbon substitute comparable to electric vehicles for road travel, so an extra flight today locks in emissions that cannot easily be undone. Discouraging tourist flights through frequent-flyer levies and ticket taxes therefore offers some of the most cost-effective climate savings available to policymakers.

The second reason, however, is that the same logic does not justify abandoning action on cars. Private vehicles still account for a much larger share of total urban pollution, traffic deaths and noise complaints, because the number of daily car trips dwarfs the number of flights. A fair strategy must combine aviation taxes with strong measures for road transport, including investment in public transport, low-emission zones and incentives to switch to electric vehicles. Were governments to focus only on flights, public support for climate action would weaken.

In conclusion, I largely agree that non-essential flights deserve stronger discouragement than cars, since each flight imposes a heavier environmental burden and has fewer low-carbon substitutes, yet road transport must still be addressed in parallel for any climate strategy to succeed.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段式：开头以 I substantially agree with this view 一次性表态并保留 nuance（no transport policy should ignore private vehicles），Body 1 用「单次长途航班远超汽车碳排放 + 缺乏电动替代」强化立场，Body 2 用「道路基数大 + 公众接受度」保留小车政策的必要性，结尾 I largely agree that non-essential flights deserve stronger discouragement than cars ... yet road transport must still be addressed in parallel 呼应立场并显示 nuance；字数 289 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
