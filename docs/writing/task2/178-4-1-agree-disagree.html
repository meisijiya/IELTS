Task 2 — Natural extinction (agree-disagree)

It is a natural process for animal species to become extinct (e.g. Dinosaurs, dodos, etc.) There is no reason why people should try to prevent this from happening. Do you agree or disagree? (2024.6.22)

立场: 不同意放任灭绝; 理由1: 当前物种消失由人类活动主导，速度远超自然进程，不能与恐龙同日而语; 理由2: 生物多样性支撑生态系统服务和人类福祉，盲目接受灭绝将付出沉重代价。

Some observers have argued that the disappearance of animal species is a natural phenomenon, citing examples such as the dinosaurs and the dodo, and that human attempts to intervene are therefore pointless. While extinction certainly occurred long before our species appeared, I firmly disagree with this conclusion, because the present wave of losses is overwhelmingly driven by human activity and is dismantling ecosystems on which our own survival depends.

The first reason is that the current extinction rate bears little resemblance to prehistoric natural turnover. Background extinction normally removes only a handful of species per million years, whereas the planet is presently losing species hundreds or even thousands of times faster, pushed by habitat destruction, pollution, over-hunting and climate change. Were we to accept this crisis as inevitable, the moral argument for restraint collapses, and short-term economic interests would continue to drive forests, wetlands and oceans past the point of recovery. These pressures did not threaten the dinosaurs, so to compare the two cases is misleading.

The second reason is that biodiversity underwrites the ecosystem services on which every human community ultimately relies. Wild species pollinate crops, purify water, recycle nutrients and regulate the climate, duties that no technology can yet replace at comparable scale. When bees decline, harvests fall; when mangrove forests vanish, coastal communities lose their protection against storms and flooding. Permitting further extinctions on the grounds that nature will take its course therefore amounts to ignoring evidence that humans are the cause and must also become the solution.

In conclusion, I still believe that concerted efforts to slow and reverse species loss are essential, because the present crisis is largely man-made and its consequences will fall on people as well as on the animals themselves.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段式：开头以 I firmly disagree with this conclusion 直接表态，Body 1 用「当前灭绝速率远高于自然背景速率」+ 反事实假设 Were we to accept this crisis as inevitable 反驳「自然过程」论，Body 2 用生态系统服务论 + 蜜蜂与红树林案例支撑人类福祉论点，结尾 I still believe that concerted efforts ... are essential 二次呼应立场，立场贯穿无摇摆，字数 277 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段衔接以 The first reason / The second reason / A clear example is / When ... / In conclusion 显式逻辑词过渡；段内使用对照结构（background extinction ... whereas the planet is presently losing species ...）和让步分句推进，行文连贯且层次清晰。LR：使用 background extinction / habitat destruction / over-hunting / ecosystem services / pollinate crops / purify water / mangrove forests 等 7 分段话题词，搭配准确且有同义替换（extinction rate ↔ background extinction / loss ↔ disappearance）。GRA：复合句比例高，包括定语从句（biodiversity that no technology can yet replace）、过去虚拟条件倒装（Were we to accept this crisis as inevitable）、让步状语（While extinction certainly occurred ...），时态与主谓一致准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
