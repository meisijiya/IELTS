Task 2 — Mobile phones in public (agree-disagree)

Some people think that the use of mobile phones should be banned in public places such as libraries, shops and on public transport. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement? (2023.10.7)

Some people suggest that mobile phones should be banned in libraries, shops and on public transport so as to protect quietness and good manners. In my opinion, I partly agree with this view. While loud or intrusive phone use should certainly be controlled, a complete ban would be both unfair and unnecessary in modern life.

There are two reasons why some control of phones in public places is reasonable. First, the sound of ring tones, video clips or speaker-phone calls clearly disturbs other people. In a quiet train carriage or a small bookshop, a single phone call can break the calm that others are trying to enjoy, and it shows a lack of respect for those around you. Second, looking at a screen also reduces people's awareness of their surroundings. A shopper who keeps checking messages may be rude to shop assistants, and a passenger who films everything on a crowded bus can invade the privacy of fellow travellers without even noticing.

However, a total ban would cause real problems. Many people rely on their phones to read tickets, check journey updates, pay for goods or stay in touch with family during a long commute. For elderly passengers or those travelling alone, a phone is also a vital link to call for help in an emergency. Banning it outright would leave these users worse off and would feel unfair to the majority of polite phone owners.

In conclusion, I believe that the best policy is not a complete ban but a clear set of social rules: phones should be set to silent in quiet areas, calls should be kept short, and loudspeaker or video use should be avoided. Courtesy, not prohibition, is what public spaces really need.

TA：本文为 Task 2「同意与否」题型，开头以 In my opinion, I partly agree 明确表态，结尾用 I believe 再次呼应，立场清晰一致，TA 满足要求。

CC：采用四段式：观点—双理由—让步—结论；衔接词 First / Second / However / In conclusion 过渡自然，段落功能划分合理，符合 6 分水平。LR：用词以常见话题词为主（public place、mobile phone、social rules），搭配少量替换（intrusive、courtesy、prohibition），未出现低频高难词。GRA：以复合句和状语从句为主，结构清楚、无明显语法错误，是一份稳定的 6 分范本。
