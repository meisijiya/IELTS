Task 2 — Reducing fossil fuels globally (agree-disagree)

The international community must act immediately to ensure all nations reduce consumption of fossil fuel (gas and oil). To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2024.5.11)

立场: 总体同意紧急行动，但应配套财政与公正转型支持; 理由1: 化石燃料是气候升温主因，拖延代价过高; 理由2: 公正转型与财政援助让全球减碳可行

Many environmental scientists now argue that the international community must act immediately to make every nation cut its consumption of fossil fuels. I largely agree that urgent action is essential, because the costs of delay grow steeper by the year, yet the policies themselves must be fair if poorer countries are to participate genuinely.

The first reason is that fossil fuels remain the dominant driver of global warming, and the window for keeping temperature rise within manageable limits is closing fast. Burning coal, oil and gas releases carbon dioxide that lingers in the atmosphere for centuries, locking in heat that melts glaciers, intensifies storms and threatens the food supply of billions. A clear example is the accelerating heatwaves across South Asia in 2023 and 2024, which damaged crops, killed outdoor workers and pushed electricity grids to the brink. Postponing serious reductions now would saddle future generations with far heavier adaptation costs.

The second reason is that the transition away from fossil fuels can be achieved without abandoning poorer nations, provided the policy mix is carefully designed. Rich countries can supply clean-energy technology and climate finance to developing economies, allowing them to leapfrog directly to solar, wind and efficient public transport instead of repeating the polluting path of industrialisation. Were governments to impose identical reduction targets on every country overnight, energy poverty would deepen and political resistance would harden. A staged, well-financed plan is more realistic and ultimately more effective.

In conclusion, I still believe that immediate international action on fossil fuels is necessary, because climate damage is accelerating rapidly, but I also hold that this action must combine ambitious targets with the financial support and technology transfer that allow every nation to share the burden fairly.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段结构，开头以 I largely agree 明确表态（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 用碳排持久性 + 2023 南亚热浪案例支持"紧急行动"，Body 2 用洁净能源跳越 + 反事实假设 Were governments to impose identical reduction targets 强调"公正转型"，结尾以 I still believe that immediate international action ... is necessary, but ... must combine ambitious targets with the financial support 收束并明确立场，字数落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段式结构清晰，每段一个主题句 + 展开理由 + 例证或反事实假设；段内以 The first reason / The second reason / A clear example is / When ... / In conclusion 等显式逻辑连接过渡，行文连贯且推进有层次，CC 估测 7 档。LR：使用 fossil fuels / global warming / climate finance / clean-energy technology / energy poverty / climate damage / adaptation costs 等话题搭配，搭配准确且有同义替换（consumption ↔ use / reduce ↔ cut），LR 估测 7 档。GRA：复合句占比较高，包含定语从句（carbon dioxide that lingers in the atmosphere）、过去虚拟条件倒装（Were governments to impose identical reduction targets）、现在分词短语（allowing them to leapfrog directly to solar）；主谓一致、时态与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
