Task 2 — Films and video games violence (discuss-both-views)

Some people believe that films and video games containing violence have negative effects on people and should be banned. Others think that they are just relaxation sources. Discuss both sides and give your own opinion. (2025.5.24)

立场: 折中（不必全面禁令，但需分级与监管）; 理由1: 暴力内容可能助长攻击性行为，尤其影响青少年; 理由2: 适度娱乐有助于成年人放松，且艺术表达不应一刀切

The question of whether violent films and video games should be prohibited continues to divide opinion. Some observers insist that such products pose a real threat to public well-being and must be banned outright, while others maintain that they are simply a legitimate form of relaxation for adults.

Those who call for a ban emphasise the documented harm that repeated exposure to graphic violence can cause, particularly among adolescents whose moral judgement is still maturing. Laboratory studies suggest that players who spend long sessions shooting virtual enemies become desensitised to real-life suffering and are quicker to endorse aggressive solutions in subsequent conflicts. Parents and teachers frequently observe that children who consume such material at home are more prone to bullying and impulsive outbursts at school, which suggests an outright prohibition would protect young viewers.

Those who oppose a ban argue that violent entertainment serves legitimate purposes. Many adults use action films or combat games as a controlled way to unwind after stressful working days, an activity that research links to lower blood pressure and improved sleep. Storytellers also point out that violent narratives have always explored serious themes such as war, injustice and revenge, and that artistic expression should not be flattened simply because some viewers find the imagery disturbing.

In my view, neither blanket prohibition nor unrestricted availability is satisfactory. A reasonable compromise is strict age classification, clear warning labels and active parental controls, which allow adults to enjoy such material while shielding children from premature exposure.

In conclusion, violent films and games do carry risks for the young, but they also offer genuine relaxation and cultural value for adults, so an age-based regulatory model is more convincing than a sweeping ban.
