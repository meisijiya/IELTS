Task 2 — Older workers in companies (advantage-disadvantage)

There are more and more older workers working in companies. Does this trend have more advantages or disadvantages for a company? (2025.8.9)

立场: 利大于弊; 理由1: 经验丰富、稳定性高、对客户与年轻员工有指导价值; 理由2: 缺点可控，体力与数字化短板可通过岗位再设计与培训弥补

As life expectancy rises and pension ages are pushed back, an increasing number of older workers remain in companies well beyond their fifties. While some employers worry about the implications for productivity and turnover, on balance I believe the advantages of this trend clearly outweigh the disadvantages for most firms.

The principal advantage is the depth of experience older employees bring to the workplace. Decades of handling clients, negotiating contracts and resolving crises give them a kind of judgement that no training manual can replicate, and they often serve as informal mentors to younger colleagues, shortening the learning curve for entire teams across the company. Companies that retain experienced staff also benefit from much lower recruitment and onboarding costs, since veteran workers require less supervision and tend to stay longer in their roles than younger hires who are still exploring their career options.

The most common drawback is the assumption that older staff are less adaptable or less productive than their younger counterparts. In physically demanding roles, stamina may indeed decline, and some older employees find it harder to master rapidly changing digital tools without extra support. These disadvantages, however, are largely manageable. Flexible scheduling, ergonomic redesign of workstations, and targeted refresher courses in technology can keep experienced workers fully effective, while companies that rely on their institutional memory often out-perform competitors driven solely by youth.

In conclusion, although an ageing workforce requires thoughtful management and the right training, the long-term benefits of stability, expertise and mentorship far outweigh the short-term costs. Were firms to design roles around the strengths of older workers rather than treating age as a liability, the advantages would be even more pronounced across the whole organisation.

TA：本文采用 advantage-disadvantage 标准四段式：Introduction 以 on balance I believe the advantages ... clearly outweigh the disadvantages 暗示要做权衡，Body 1 阐述 advantages（经验与判断力、导师角色、留任成本低），Body 2 阐述 disadvantages（体力与数字化短板），结尾 the long-term benefits ... far outweigh the short-term costs 给出明确权重判断，任务回应完整、字数 280 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段衔接以 The principal advantage is / The most common drawback is / These disadvantages, however, are largely manageable / In conclusion 显式逻辑词过渡，每段单一主题，CC 估测 7 档。LR：使用 life expectancy / institutional memory / onboarding costs / informal mentors / ergonomic redesign / refresher courses / age as a liability 等话题搭配，搭配准确且有同义替换（older staff ↔ older workers / older employees ↔ veteran workers），LR 估测 7 档。GRA：复合句占比较高，包含定语从句（teams that retain experienced staff）、让步状语（while companies that rely on their institutional memory often out-perform）、虚拟条件倒装（Were firms to design roles around the strengths of older workers ...）；主谓一致、时态与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
