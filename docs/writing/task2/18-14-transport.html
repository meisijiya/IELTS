Task 2 — Vehicle-free days (advantage-disadvantage)

Some cities have vehicle-free days, when private cars, trucks, and motorcycles are banned in the city center. Public transportation like buses, taxis, and metros are advised. To what extent do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? (2024.3.2)

Some cities now ban private cars, trucks, and motorcycles from their centres on certain days and encourage residents to use buses, taxis, or metros instead. In my view, the advantages of this policy clearly outweigh its disadvantages, although some practical problems do exist.

The main benefit is a noticeable improvement in air quality. When thousands of private vehicles are removed from central streets for even one day a week, the level of exhaust fumes drops sharply, and the air that people breathe becomes noticeably cleaner. A second benefit is reduced traffic noise. Anyone who has walked through a quiet city centre on a car-free morning knows how much calmer streets sound without the constant roar of engines and the noise of horns. Together, these two changes also tend to make central areas more attractive for pedestrians, small shops, and outdoor cafés, which can give a modest but real boost to local businesses and encourage people to spend more time outdoors.

The policy, however, is not perfect. Disadvantages include inconvenience for drivers, especially those who live outside the city centre or who depend on their vehicles for work, such as delivery drivers. Some shops may worry that fewer customers will drive in on those days, and families with young children or elderly relatives may find public transport harder to use than a private car. These concerns are real, but they can be eased with better bus routes, cheaper tickets, and clearer public information before each vehicle-free day.

In conclusion, while vehicle-free days do create short-term inconvenience, the long-term gains in air quality, noise reduction, and street life make them a worthwhile experiment for modern cities.

TA：本题为「利弊对比」题型，立场在首段以 the advantages clearly outweigh its disadvantages 明示，结尾 make them a worthwhile experiment 再次呼应，TA 完整。
