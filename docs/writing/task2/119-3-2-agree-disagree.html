Task 2 — Following local customs abroad (agree-disagree)

If people go to live in another country, they should follow the local customs and traditions. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2023.7.8)

立场: 总体同意应尊重当地习俗; 理由1: 尊重当地习俗体现礼貌并促进社区和谐; 理由2: 但移民不必全盘放弃自我，可在法律框架内保留自身文化。

Some people argue that newcomers to a country should adapt by following local customs and traditions. I largely agree with this view, since respect for host-society norms is a basic requirement for peaceful coexistence, although immigrants should not be expected to abandon every aspect of their own identity.

The first reason is that conforming to local customs is a basic courtesy that smooths the path of integration. When migrants learn everyday conventions of their new home, such as queuing politely or greeting neighbours in the local manner, they signal goodwill and earn the trust of long-term residents. A clear example is the way newly arrived professionals in Japan who master small etiquette rituals are accepted more quickly into their offices and form lasting working relationships. Without such willingness, mistrust grows on both sides and communities become harder to knit together.

The second reason is that some practices are not merely cosmetic but tied to law or public safety, which makes observance non-negotiable. Traffic rules, tax obligations and workplace regulations are not matters of personal preference, and immigrants who ignore them face serious penalties regardless of their background. Were newcomers to claim exemption from such universal rules on cultural grounds, the social order of the host country would quickly deteriorate. Admittedly, respecting local custom need not mean surrendering every trace of one's heritage; festivals, cuisine and religious observance can still flourish inside the law, and this diversity often enriches the host country.

In conclusion, I firmly believe that following the customs of one's new country is essential for mutual respect and social harmony, yet immigrants can preserve their own traditions provided these do not conflict with the law.
