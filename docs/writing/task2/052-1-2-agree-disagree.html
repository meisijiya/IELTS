Task 2 — Parents encourage physical activities (agree-disagree)

Parents should encourage children to spend less time on studying and more time on doing physical activities. To what extent do you agree? (2021.2.20)

立场: 同意家长应鼓励孩子多运动; 理由1: 运动促进身心健康并提升学习效率; 理由2: 童年是培养运动习惯与社交韧性的关键期

Some parents today push their children to study for long hours, while others urge them to spend more time on physical activities. I firmly agree with the view that parents should actively encourage children to balance study with sport, because an over-emphasis on academic work poses a genuine threat to young people's well-being and long-term success.

The first reason is that regular physical activity strengthens both body and mind in ways that extra study hours cannot. Exercise releases endorphins, improves sleep quality and sharpens concentration, all of which directly support classroom performance. A clear example is the way pupils who play a team sport at school often return to their books with better focus and higher energy than classmates who have been sitting at a desk all day. When parents limit their children's movement in the name of study, they are therefore undermining the very performance they hope to encourage.

The second reason is that childhood is the right time to build habits of movement, social cooperation and resilience, capacities that no examination ever tests. Children who learn to ride a bike, run a race or play in a band develop confidence, learn to handle both victory and disappointment, and forge friendships that often last for life. Were parents to sacrifice these formative experiences on the altar of academic competition, their children might graduate with impressive marks yet lack the emotional balance needed for adult careers and relationships.

In conclusion, I still believe that parents should encourage children to spend less time studying and more time on physical activities, because a balanced childhood produces healthier, happier and ultimately more capable adults.
