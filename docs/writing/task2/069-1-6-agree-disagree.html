Task 2 — Travel before university (agree-disagree)

Some students choose to work or travel after leaving school and before going to university. Many people, however, say that working experience is more useful in adult life than travel. Do you agree or disagree with this statement? (2024.8.3)

立场: 不同意，travel 拥有 work 无法替代的内在价值; 理由1: 旅行拓宽视野、培养跨文化适应力，这些能力在工作初期难以获得; 理由2: 工作可推迟到大学假期，而旅行的时效性与年龄阶段紧密相关。

Many people argue that students who choose to work between school and university gain more useful preparation for adult life than those who choose to travel. I disagree with this view because, while work experience is undoubtedly valuable, travel offers forms of personal growth that employment during this particular stage of life simply cannot provide.

The first reason travel holds a unique advantage is that it broadens perspective and cultivates cross-cultural adaptability. Young travellers who navigate unfamiliar cities, languages and customs develop a kind of practical intelligence that no office job can replicate. A student who spends three months backpacking across South-East Asia, for instance, often returns with stronger communication skills, greater resilience and a clearer sense of personal direction than a peer who has worked the same period in a local shop. These qualities, whose value extends throughout an entire career, are far harder to acquire once a person has settled into a professional routine.

Furthermore, the window for carefree travel narrows quickly after graduation. Once students enter the workforce, annual leave becomes limited and family responsibilities tend to accumulate, making long independent journeys increasingly difficult to arrange. Work experience, by contrast, can be obtained at almost any stage of life, whether through summer internships during university or part-time employment alongside studies. Were young people to sacrifice travel now in favour of work, they might find that the chance for genuine exploration never returns.

In conclusion, I firmly believe that travel between school and university offers intrinsic value that work experience cannot match. Students should be encouraged to explore the world during this brief interval, knowing that working opportunities will still be plentiful in the years ahead.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段式：Introduction 以 I disagree with this view because ... 一次性表明立场（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 从「拓宽视野与跨文化适应力」回应 work vs travel 命题，Body 2 从「travel 时效窗口 vs work 灵活性」切入，结尾 travel ... offers intrinsic value that work experience cannot match 重申立场，任务回应完整、字数 283 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
