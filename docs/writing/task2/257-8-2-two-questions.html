Task 2 — Tourism in developing countries (two-questions)

An increasing number of developing countries are expanding their tourist industry. Why do you think it is the case? Do you think it is a positive or negative development? (2025.6.28)

立场: 解释经济多元化与文化资源变现推动，并判断整体为积极但需保护生态; 理由1: 外汇收入与就业机会吸引发展中国家扩张旅游; 理由2: 积极为主因带来文化自豪与基础设施，但生态保护不可松懈

The aim of this essay is to examine why a growing number of developing countries are deliberately expanding their tourist industries, and to consider whether this expansion should be regarded as a positive or a negative form of national development.

Two related factors explain the trend. The first is the search for foreign currency and jobs. Tourism earns hard currency quickly and creates employment for waiters, drivers, tour guides and hotel staff who may otherwise have only subsistence work, an attractive prospect for governments under fiscal pressure. The second factor is the recognition of cultural and natural assets as economic resources. Heritage towns, beaches, wildlife reserves and traditional festivals, once ignored as backward, are now rebranded as attractions that can fund schools and hospitals.

On balance, I regard tourism as a positive development for these nations, provided growth is carefully managed. Welcoming visitors brings genuine income, lifts communities out of poverty and cultivates national pride, because citizens see their own culture valued by people who travel thousands of miles to experience it. Infrastructure built for tourists, such as better roads and cleaner airports, also benefits local residents long after the holiday-makers have gone home. Admittedly, mass tourism can damage ecosystems and inflate living costs for residents, yet these risks can be reduced through visitor caps, environmental impact fees and stronger protection of cultural sites, allowing the benefits to outweigh the costs over time.

In conclusion, developing countries are expanding tourism because it offers fast foreign exchange and turns cultural heritage into economic strength, and although environmental and social risks deserve attention, the overall impact is positive when growth is planned rather than rushed.

TA：本文采用 two-questions 标准四段结构，Intro 以 The aim of this essay is to examine ... and to consider ... 一次性点明两问，Body 1 用外汇收入与就业 + 文化资产变现两条原因回应"Why is it the case"，Body 2 用收入/就业/民族自豪/基础设施改善 + 让步承认生态风险给出正向判断"Is it a positive or negative development"，结尾以 the overall impact is positive when growth is planned 收束并明确立场，字数落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
