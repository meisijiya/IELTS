Task 2 — Career changes (single-question)

An increasing number of people are changing careers during their working life. What are the reasons for this?

立场: 多重因素促使成年人频繁更换职业; 理由1: 行业技术变革迫使原有技能贬值; 理由2: 个人价值观转向追求意义与平衡

The rising tendency for adults to switch careers several times during their working life has prompted widespread discussion. The aim of this essay is to identify the main reasons behind this career-switching pattern, which has become increasingly common in many countries.

One compelling explanation is that many industries are being reshaped by technology, causing traditional roles to deteriorate or disappear altogether. Automation, artificial intelligence and global supply chains have rewritten the skills that employers value, and workers whose expertise becomes outdated often have no choice but to retrain in a different field. A clear example is the wave of bank tellers in many countries, whose positions have shrunk as digital banking has expanded, prompting large numbers of them to pursue careers in data analysis or customer experience design. Were such workers to refuse to adapt, they would risk long-term unemployment and serious financial insecurity.

A second reason lies in shifting personal priorities. Workers today are less willing than previous generations to tolerate jobs that feel meaningless, and they are more inclined to search for roles that align with their values, interests and family circumstances. Having weighed the trade-offs between stability and fulfilment, many professionals move on once a position stops offering a sense of purpose. A clear example is the wave of teachers and nurses who, after years of gruelling schedules, retrain as wellness coaches or freelance writers in pursuit of a better work-life balance.

In conclusion, career changes are driven both by external forces such as technological disruption and by internal factors such as evolving personal values, and these two influences will continue to shape working lives for years to come.

TA：本文采用 single-question 四段结构，开头以 The aim of this essay is to identify the main reasons ... 直接点出任务，Body 1 与 Body 2 分别给出两条独立理由（行业技术变革 / 个人价值观转向），结尾以 driven both by external forces ... and by internal factors ... 重申并归因，字数 275 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段式结构清晰，每段一个主题句 + 展开理由 + 例证；段内以 One compelling explanation / A second reason / For example / Having weighed / A clear example / Were such workers to ... 等显式逻辑词过渡，行文连贯且推进有层次，CC 估测 7 档。LR：使用 career-switching / technological disruption / digital banking / data analysis / personal priorities / work-life balance / gruelling schedules / a sense of purpose 等话题搭配，搭配准确且有同义替换（workers 替换 people，roles 替换 jobs），LR 估测 7 档。GRA：复合句占比较高，包含定语从句（workers whose expertise becomes outdated）、过去虚拟倒装（Were such workers to refuse to adapt）、现在分词完成式短语（Having weighed the trade-offs）；主谓一致、时态与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
