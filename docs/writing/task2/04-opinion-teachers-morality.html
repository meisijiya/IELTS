Task 2 — Teachers and morality

Some people believe teachers should teach students to judge what is right or wrong and to behave well. Others say teachers should only teach students academic subjects. Discuss both views and give your opinion. (2025.4.26)

It is often argued that teachers ought to guide students in distinguishing right from wrong and behaving well, while others believe teachers should stick strictly to academic subjects. In my view, although parents carry the main responsibility for moral upbringing, schools and teachers should also play an important part in shaping students' character alongside their intellectual growth.

Those who favour moral teaching in schools point out that children spend a large part of their day in classrooms and naturally look up to teachers as role models. Lessons on honesty, respect and fairness can therefore reach students when habits are still being formed, and a teacher who only delivers facts may leave young people unprepared for the ethical choices they will face later in work and relationships. I partly agree with this view: when teachers complement, rather than replace, family guidance, students learn to connect what they read in textbooks with the values they practise every day.

On the other hand, supporters of an academic-only role argue that teachers are already overburdened and that moral instruction may stray into personal beliefs, especially on sensitive topics. They feel parents and communities are better placed to pass on values. While this concern is reasonable, simply removing moral content from schools does not solve it; what matters is how such lessons are handled. With clear, neutral guidelines, teachers can address issues such as bullying, cheating or discrimination without imposing any single worldview, allowing academic rigour and basic civic values to reinforce each other.

In conclusion, I believe teachers should contribute to students' moral development while continuing to focus on academic subjects, because character and knowledge grow together and neither can fully replace the other.
