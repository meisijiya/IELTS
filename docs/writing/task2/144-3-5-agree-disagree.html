Task 2 — No taxes on earned money (agree-disagree)

People believe that they should be able to keep all the money they earn and should not pay tax to the state. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2023.6.10)

立场: 坚决反对个人免缴税收; 理由1: 公共品需要集体资金; 理由2: 缴税是公民对等权利与义务的体现

Some people argue that individuals should be allowed to keep every penny they earn and that the state has no right to demand taxes. I firmly disagree with this view, because without compulsory contributions from citizens, modern societies would quickly collapse, and no meaningful freedom could long survive.

The first reason is that virtually every public service on which individuals rely depends on collective funding. Paved roads, public hospitals, clean water systems, basic policing and national defence cannot be produced by any single household, however wealthy it may be. A clear example is the rapid spread of vaccination programmes during the pandemic, which succeeded only because governments could pool resources from millions of taxpayers at short notice. Were everyone permitted to keep their full income, essential infrastructure would either collapse or fall into private hands, and ordinary citizens would lose the safety net they presently take for granted.

The second reason is that taxation is the financial expression of the social contract between citizens and the state. People who pay tax acquire a legitimate voice in how their country is run, including the right to vote for governments and to demand accountability from public officials. By contrast, a population that refuses to fund public services forfeits any moral basis for claiming those services in times of need. Were the principle of no tax to spread, civic responsibility would deteriorate rapidly, and the freedom that people cherish would soon turn into a hollow slogan.

In conclusion, I still believe that paying tax is not a regrettable loss but a fundamental duty that sustains both the practical and moral foundations of a free society.
