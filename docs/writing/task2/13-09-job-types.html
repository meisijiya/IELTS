Task 2 — Professional vs celebrity pay (agree-disagree)

Professional workers like doctors and teachers should be paid more than sports and entertainment personalities. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2023.5.27)

Some people argue that professionals in essential fields, such as doctors and teachers, should earn higher salaries than sports and entertainment personalities. While I accept that fair pay matters in every career, I disagree with this view, because the value of a job to society cannot be measured by pay alone, and market forces already play a fair role in setting wages.

There is a clear argument for paying doctors and teachers more. Their work directly affects the health, education and future opportunities of the public, so society depends on them every day. Sports and entertainment figures often provide enjoyment but do not save lives or shape young minds. From this perspective, a doctor who works fifty hours a week to look after patients deserves more reward than a footballer who plays two matches.

I still believe, however, that tying pay to social importance has serious problems. First, salary levels in a market economy are usually shaped by how much money a job brings in, not by how 'good' it feels. Sports leagues and film studios generate huge revenues, and athletes or actors help attract that money, so their high pay reflects real commercial value. Second, deciding who deserves more — doctors, teachers, nurses or firefighters — would require governments to set wages by opinion, which is unfair and impractical. If pay were fixed by such judgements, many talented people would simply choose careers that pay better, leaving essential services short of staff.

In conclusion, although I deeply respect doctors and teachers, I do not think the government should force their salaries above those of sports or entertainment stars. A fairer approach is to improve public-sector pay overall and let other industries continue to reward talent as the market dictates.

TA：本题是 agree-disagree 题型，立场在引入段以 "I disagree with this view" 明确表态，结尾用 "I do not think the government should force..." 再次呼应，前后立场一致；中段采用"让步 + 转折"经典结构，承认正方观点的同时给出反方理由，符合高分 agree-disagree 的标准写法。

CC：四段分别承担"立场 / 正方让步 / 反方论证 / 重申立场"功能，段内主题—理由—例证—小结链条完整；衔接词 While / From this perspective / First / Second / If / In conclusion 等基础搭配齐全，没有刻意炫技，符合 6 分档表现。LR：使用 professionals / essential fields / commercial value / market forces / public-sector 等话题搭配，搭配准确且重复少，没有低频词硬塞。GRA：复合句以定语从句 + 让步状语 + 条件状语为主，主谓一致、被动语态、时态控制基本无误，没有出现严重语法错误，整体是 6–6.5 分档的稳定输出。

  • professional workers — 专业人士，指医生 / 教师等职业。
  • entertainment personalities — 演艺界名人，题干对照词。
  • market forces — 市场力量，正方论证依据。
  • essential fields — 关键领域，引入段概括正方主张。
  • fair pay — 公平薪酬，反方让步切入词。
  • commercial value — 商业价值，反方核心论据。
  • generate revenues — 创造收入，体育 / 娱乐行业特征。
  • public-sector pay — 公共部门薪酬，结尾建议措辞。
  • set wages by opinion — 按主观意见定工资，反方质疑政府干预。
  • essential services — 基本公共服务，结尾与 short of staff 搭配。
