Task 2 — Doctors vs athletes pay (agree-disagree)

Professional workers like doctors and teachers should be paid more than sports and entertainment personalities. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2023.5.27)

立场: 同意; 理由1: 专业岗位承担更深远的社会责任; 理由2: 现行薪酬失衡削弱公共服务吸引力

There is a widespread debate over whether professionals such as doctors and teachers ought to earn more than sports and entertainment personalities, whose incomes today often dwarf those of public servants. I firmly agree that this should be the case, because the work of these professionals carries far deeper social value and demands years of rigorous training that entertainers rarely undergo in any comparable form.

The first reason is that doctors and teachers exert a direct influence on the long-term health and intellectual capacity of the population, responsibilities that justify far higher pay than a passing headline in a sports stream or a viral video online. A clear example can be seen during any public health crisis, when a competent doctor can save thousands of lives and a skilled teacher can shape the next generation of scientists, engineers and informed citizens. Were such professionals to be paid less than footballers whose careers often end in their thirties, young people would have little reason to spend a decade in medical school or teacher training in the first place. The second reason is that the current pay imbalance actively weakens public services, a trend that more balanced salaries would help to reverse over the long term. When a nurse earns less than a reality television contestant, hospitals struggle to recruit qualified staff, classrooms become overcrowded, and the quality of essential care deteriorates steadily for everyone who depends on the system.

In conclusion, I am convinced that society should reward doctors and teachers more generously than sports and entertainment figures, because their work secures both the wellbeing and the wisdom of the entire community.
