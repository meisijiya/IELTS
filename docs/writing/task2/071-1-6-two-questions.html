Task 2 — Long school holidays (two-questions)

In many countries, primary and secondary schools close for two months or more in the summer holidays. What is the value of long school holidays? What are the arguments in favor of shorter school holidays? (C20T2)

立场: 长假与短假各有价值; 理由1: 长假让学生恢复精力、参与家庭活动和发展课外兴趣; 理由2: 缩短假期有助于减少知识遗忘、缓解家长托管压力。

The aim of this essay is to examine the value of long summer holidays for schoolchildren and to consider the arguments in favour of making those holidays shorter.

The first question concerns the benefits of an extended break. After several months of intensive study and assessment, young people genuinely need time to rest and recover both physically and mentally. A long holiday gives families the opportunity to travel together, reinforcing bonds that busy term-time routines often strain. It also allows children to pursue hobbies and activities that the regular curriculum cannot accommodate, whether that means learning to swim, attending a music camp or simply reading for pleasure. These experiences contribute to well-rounded development in ways that an extra month of classroom instruction rarely does.

The second question asks why some educators argue for shorter holidays. The most persuasive reason is academic: research consistently shows that pupils lose a measurable amount of what they have learned during the summer, a phenomenon widely known as summer learning loss. A shorter break would mitigate this decline and keep children mentally engaged throughout the warmest months. Shorter holidays would also ease the burden on working parents, who currently face several weeks of expensive childcare arrangements when schools close. Were the summer recess reduced to perhaps six weeks, schools could redistribute the remaining time across shorter, more frequent breaks distributed throughout the academic year.

In conclusion, long school holidays offer valuable space for rest, family connection and personal development, while shorter holidays would reduce learning loss and support working families. A balanced compromise, such as a slightly shorter summer divided into shorter mid-term breaks, may ultimately serve children best.

TA：本文采用 two-questions 标准四段式：Introduction 以 The aim of this essay is to examine ... and to consider ... 一次性点出两个问题，Body 1 回应「长假价值」（休息、家庭、课外兴趣），Body 2 回应「缩短假期的论点」（学习损失、家长负担），结尾以 A balanced compromise ... may ultimately serve children best 总结两个问题的答案，任务回应完整、字数 281 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
