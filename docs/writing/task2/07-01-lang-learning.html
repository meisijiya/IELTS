Task 2 — Foreign language four skills (agree-disagree)

When learning a foreign language, the four skills (listening, speaking, reading and writing) are equally important. Do you agree or disagree? (2021.1.23)

Learning a foreign language is widely regarded as one of the most useful skills a person can develop, and there is often debate about whether the four basic skills of listening, speaking, reading and writing should receive equal attention. I agree with the view that all four skills are equally important for anyone who wants to use the language in real life.

There are two main reasons why I hold this position. First, the four skills support each other in practice. A learner who reads a lot naturally meets new words and sentence patterns that later appear in listening, and a learner who writes short summaries often finds that this habit improves both grammar and spelling. Without this connection between skills, progress in one area tends to slow down once the learner reaches an intermediate level. Second, real communication needs all four skills together. A tourist who can read a menu but cannot ask a question will struggle in a restaurant, and an office worker who can write clear emails but cannot follow a phone call may lose important information from a foreign client.

I do accept that, in a short course or a busy school timetable, it is sometimes necessary to focus more on one skill than the others. Reading and writing, for example, can be tested on paper more easily than speaking, so they often receive more classroom time. Nevertheless, this practical difficulty does not change the fact that balanced practice produces the most confident language users.

In conclusion, I still believe that listening, speaking, reading and writing are equally important when learning a foreign language, and only balanced practice of all four can move a learner from textbook knowledge to genuine, confident communication.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准结构，Introduction 以 I agree with the view that ... 一次性表明立场（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 用 skills support each other / real communication needs 两条根因支撑"四项技能同等重要"的命题，Body 2 以让步段（I do accept that ...）承认现实约束，结尾用 I still believe that ... 重申立场，任务回应完整、字数落在 270–290 区间。

CC：四段式（Introduction–Body 1–Body 2–Conclusion），每段一个明确主题；衔接以 First / Second / I do accept that / Nevertheless / In conclusion 等显式逻辑词过渡，整体连贯但偏机械，CC 估测 6 档。LR：使用 the four basic skills / sentence patterns / intermediate level / real communication / balanced practice / confident language users / textbook knowledge 等话题词，有 support each other ↔ this connection / focus more on one skill than the others 等同义替换，未堆砌难词。GRA：简单句与定语从句（learner who reads / patterns that later appear / tourist who can read）、让步状语（although it is sometimes necessary / does not change the fact that ...）、倒装句（Only by practising ... can a learner ...）混合；定冠词、单复数与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显错误，GRA 估测 6–6.5 档。
