Task 2 — Natural resource depletion (problem-solution)

The natural resources such as oil, forests and freshwater are being consumed at an alarming rate. What problems does this cause? How can we solve these problems? (2021.9.11)

立场: 资源消耗不可持续, 必须采取立法、能源转型、循环利用等多管齐下措施; 理由1: 资源耗竭引发能源与粮食安全危机、气候恶化与生物多样性丧失; 理由2: 解决方案涵盖立法管控开采、发展清洁能源、推广循环经济与公众教育。

The aim of this essay is to consider the serious problems that result from the rapid consumption of oil, forests, freshwater and similar natural resources, and to outline practical solutions that governments and consumers can adopt to reduce this pressure.

Unsustainable consumption produces problems that reach far beyond the loss of any single resource. Burning fossil fuels at current rates accelerates climate change, intensifies droughts and storms, and pushes countless species towards extinction through habitat loss. Forests that once regulated regional rainfall are replaced by plantations and farmland, reducing biodiversity and the ability of landscapes to absorb carbon. Freshwater aquifers that took thousands of years to form are emptied faster than rain can recharge them, threatening food security in many agricultural regions. Together, these pressures undermine public health, weaken economies and erode the resilience that societies will need to face future shocks.

The most effective response combines regulation, clean technology and everyday behaviour change. Governments should set legally binding targets for renewable energy, end harmful agricultural subsidies and introduce carbon pricing that reflects the true cost of extraction. Industry must be required to design products that last longer, can be repaired easily and use recycled materials wherever feasible. International co-operation on forest protection, water-sharing treaties and the transfer of green technology can extend these gains to developing economies. At the household level, simple habits such as turning off lights, eating less meat and choosing certified sustainable products reinforce official policy and create demand for greener supply chains.

In conclusion, the unchecked depletion of oil, forests and freshwater threatens both human wellbeing and ecological stability, but a coordinated mix of regulation, innovation and responsible consumption can still place the world on a more sustainable path.
