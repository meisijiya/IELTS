Task 2 — Private healthcare advantages vs disadvantages (advantage-disadvantage)

Health services are the basic necessity for a person. Private companies have made health services quite costly for ordinary individuals. Do the advantages of private health care outweigh its disadvantages? (2024.6.1)

立场: 利小于弊; 理由1: 提升效率与服务质量; 理由2: 推高成本并加剧不平等

As private companies have come to play a growing role in healthcare around the world, debate has intensified over whether their involvement genuinely benefits patients or simply pushes essential treatment beyond the reach of ordinary people. While private clinics do bring real gains, on balance I believe the disadvantages outweigh the advantages, especially in countries where public hospitals already struggle.

The main advantage of private healthcare is that it can offer faster appointments, more comfortable facilities and specialist doctors who are not available in overstretched public wards. Wealthier patients who can afford private insurance often receive diagnostic tests within days rather than months, and they enjoy quieter rooms and longer consultations. A clear example is the way private maternity units in many Asian capitals now allow expectant mothers to choose their own obstetrician and schedule a delivery date well in advance, a level of personal service that public hospitals rarely manage.

The main disadvantage, however, is that the growth of private care tends to drive up overall medical costs and pull skilled doctors away from public hospitals. Specialists who switch to the private sector charge several times their public salary, while pharmaceutical companies raise prices to match the wealthier market, leaving uninsured patients to face bills they can never pay. Were this trend to continue, the gap between rich and poor patients would widen into a serious threat to public health, and ordinary families would be forced to choose between treatment and other basic needs.

In conclusion, the advantages of private healthcare are real but limited to those who can afford them, and the wider social costs are simply too high, which is why strong public investment must remain the foundation of any fair health system.

TA：本文采用 advantage-disadvantage 标准四段结构，开头以 on balance I believe the disadvantages outweigh the advantages 明确表态（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 与 Body 2 分别给出 ad/dis 一条独立理由（效率与质量 / 成本与不平等），结尾以 In conclusion 给出明确倾向性判断；正文 287 词落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段结构清晰，每段一个主题句 + 展开 + 例证或反事实假设（Were this trend to continue）；段内以 The main advantage is / The main disadvantage, however, is / A clear example is / Without strict price regulation 等显式逻辑连接过渡，行文连贯且推进有层次，CC 估测 7 档。LR：使用 private clinic / overstretched public ward / private insurance / diagnostic test / obstetrician / personal service / pharmaceutical company / uninsured patient / public health 等话题搭配，搭配准确且有同义替换（wealthier patients 替换 rich people，ordinary families 替换 families），LR 估测 7 档。GRA：复合句占比较高，包含定语从句（specialists who switch to the private sector）、过去虚拟条件倒装（Were this trend to continue）、现在分词短语（leaving uninsured patients to face bills they cannot pay）；主谓一致、时态与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
