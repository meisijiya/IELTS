Task 2 — City dwellers lack of exercise (problem-solution)

It is difficult for people in cities to get enough physical exercise. What are the causes and what solutions could be taken to solve the problem? (2022.7.9)

原因: 通勤依赖机动车与空间不足; 方案: 城市规划 + 社区健身设施

The aim of this essay is to examine why urban residents struggle to take enough exercise and to suggest practical solutions that city authorities could introduce. Although the issue is rooted in modern lifestyles, well-designed public interventions can make daily movement far easier for ordinary people.

There are two main causes of the problem. The first is that city life has been built around cars and sedentary jobs, with long commutes and hours at a desk leaving little energy for sport. The second is that dense neighbourhoods often lack safe, attractive spaces where adults feel comfortable walking, jogging or cycling, especially after dark or in extreme weather. A clear example is the office worker who leaves home at dawn, sits at a screen all day and returns exhausted, only to discover that the local park is poorly lit and the pavement is too narrow for a comfortable run.

The most effective solution is for planners to redesign the urban environment so that exercise becomes a natural by-product of daily routines, while employers make movement easier during the working day. Building protected cycle lanes, widening pavements, planting trees for shade and turning disused lots into pocket parks would all encourage residents to walk more. At the same time, firms can offer standing desks, lunchtime yoga classes and subsidised gym memberships, and doctors can prescribe walking plans for patients with weight concerns. A clear example is Copenhagen, where decades of cycle-friendly investment have made the bicycle the first choice for nearly half of all commuters.

In conclusion, the causes of urban inactivity lie in car-dependent design and limited safe space, and the most promising remedies combine thoughtful city planning with workplace support that makes movement a normal part of every single day.
