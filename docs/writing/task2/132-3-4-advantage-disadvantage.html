Task 2 — Private cars trend (advantage-disadvantage)

More and more people buy and use their own cars. Do you think the advantages of this trend for individuals outweigh the disadvantages for the environment? (2023.12.2)

立场: 个人的便利不足以抵消环境代价; 理由1: 个人层面带来时间灵活与生活半径扩大; 理由2: 环境层面排放加剧、热岛效应与资源消耗难以逆转

The rapid spread of private car ownership has reshaped daily life in countless countries. While the benefits for individual drivers are easy to see, I believe the environmental disadvantages of this trend clearly outweigh the personal advantages, and urgent policy action is therefore required.

For individuals, owning a car brings undeniable convenience and a sense of freedom. Households can travel at any hour, carry heavy shopping or young children without timetables, and reach workplaces or relatives in areas poorly served by public transport. In rural communities and sprawling suburbs, a private car often makes the difference between social isolation and full participation in working and family life, so it is hardly surprising that many families today regard their vehicle as essential rather than luxurious.

For the environment, however, the costs are severe and increasingly difficult to reverse. Tailpipe emissions from petrol and diesel vehicles remain a major source of carbon dioxide, nitrogen oxides and fine particulate matter, all of which damage both the climate and public health seriously. Growing car use also fuels urban sprawl, paving over green spaces and producing heat-island effects that make cities hotter in summer. The extraction of lithium, cobalt and rare earths for electric vehicles adds a further layer of mining waste and habitat destruction, so even the cleanest cars still leave a substantial ecological footprint.

In conclusion, although private cars deliver real personal benefits, their environmental costs are simply too large to ignore for future generations. Were governments to invest heavily in public transport, walking and cycling while taxing pollution honestly, the balance could shift and the trend could be brought within sustainable limits.

TA：本文采用 advantage-disadvantage 标准四段式：Introduction 以 I believe the environmental disadvantages ... clearly outweigh the personal advantages 暗示要做权衡，Body 1 阐述个人 advantages（便利与自由、乡村出行），Body 2 阐述环境 disadvantages（排放、热岛、矿产开采），结尾 their environmental costs are simply too large to ignore 给出明确权重判断，任务回应完整、字数 286 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段衔接以 For individuals / For the environment, however / so even the cleanest cars / Were governments to invest heavily / In conclusion 显式逻辑词过渡，每段单一主题，CC 估测 7 档。LR：使用 car ownership / tailpipe emissions / nitrogen oxides / particulate matter / urban sprawl / heat-island effects / mining waste / ecological footprint 等话题搭配，搭配准确且有同义替换（private car ↔ private vehicle / suburban ↔ rural community），LR 估测 7 档。GRA：复合句占比较高，包含定语从句（areas that are poorly served by public transport）、让步状语（even the cleanest cars still leave a substantial ecological footprint）、虚拟条件倒装（Were governments to invest heavily ...）；主谓一致、时态与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
