Task 2 — Tourism in developing countries (two-questions)

An increasing number of developing countries are expanding their tourist industry. Why do you think it is the case? Do you think it is a positive or negative development? (2021.8.14)

立场: 解释就业压力与文化资源被发现吸引投资，并判断整体为积极但需防范过度开发; 理由1: 青年失业与文化资源被发现推动旅游投资; 理由2: 积极为主因创造就业与文化自豪，但需防止过度商业化

The aim of this essay is to explain why a growing number of developing countries are investing heavily in their tourist industries, and to consider whether this wave of expansion should be welcomed or treated with caution.

Two related factors explain the trend. The first is the urgent need for jobs. Many developing economies struggle to absorb their young people into manufacturing or office work, and tourism creates relatively quick employment in hotels, transport, handicraft sales and guiding services, lowering the political pressure caused by unemployment. The second factor is the growing recognition of culture as an export. Folk dances, historic temples and regional cuisines that were once taken for granted are now packaged as unique experiences, attracting both foreign visitors and diaspora tourists eager to revisit their roots.

On balance, I view tourism as a positive development for these nations, provided growth respects local limits. Welcoming visitors creates genuine income for households that may otherwise depend on subsistence farming, while the infrastructure built for hotels and airports, such as paved roads and reliable electricity, also improves daily life for residents. Exposure to international travellers also strengthens national pride, as citizens see their own heritage celebrated on global platforms. Admittedly, mass tourism can inflate housing prices and erode authentic traditions, yet these drawbacks can be contained through visitor caps, community-led tourism and clear rules on cultural commercialisation, ensuring the benefits endure longer than the costs.

In conclusion, developing countries are expanding tourism because it offers rapid employment and turns cultural heritage into a tradable good, and although over-commercialisation and rising living costs are real concerns, the overall impact is positive when development is planned and locally controlled.

TA：本文采用 two-questions 标准四段结构，Intro 以 The aim of this essay is to explain ... and to consider ... 一次性点明两问，Body 1 用青年就业压力 + 文化资源被发现两条原因回应"Why is it the case"，Body 2 用家庭收入 / 基础设施 / 民族自豪 + 让步承认过度商业化给出正向判断"Is it a positive or negative development"，结尾以 the overall impact is positive when development is planned and locally controlled 收束并明确立场，字数落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
