Task 2 — Social media daily use (advantage-disadvantage)

Many people use social media every day to keep in touch with friends and get social news. Do you think the advantages of it outweigh the disadvantages? (2022.1.15)

立场: 利大于弊; 理由1: 社交媒体为弱势群体提供低门槛的连接渠道; 理由2: 心理依赖与隐私风险可控，应配合教育与平台规则

Daily use of social media has become routine for billions of people who rely on it to maintain friendships and follow breaking news. This essay will weigh the main advantages and disadvantages of this habit and argue that, on balance, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks.

The main advantage is that social media gives people a low-cost, real-time way to stay connected with others. Elderly relatives who live alone can share photographs with grandchildren several times a week, while new graduates moving to unfamiliar cities can quickly locate communities organised around shared hobbies or professional interests. For people with disabilities or limited mobility, these platforms can also function as a primary social lifeline that traditional outings rarely provide, helping to reduce the isolation that otherwise affects such groups disproportionately.

There are, however, several disadvantages. Heavy users often develop a psychological dependence on the constant stream of notifications, and studies link this habit to higher reported anxiety and poorer sleep quality. Privacy is another concern, since platforms routinely collect behavioural data and use it to shape the advertising and political messages users see. A clear example is the way young job seekers have lost opportunities after employers uncovered embarrassing posts from years earlier. These risks are real but largely manageable through clearer platform rules and stronger personal digital literacy.

In my view, the advantages outweigh the disadvantages when individuals, schools and regulators treat social media as a powerful tool that requires deliberate habits rather than an activity to be abandoned outright.

In conclusion, although daily social media use brings genuine risks of dependence and privacy loss, its capacity to connect people cheaply and instantly means that its advantages still outweigh its disadvantages for the majority of users.
