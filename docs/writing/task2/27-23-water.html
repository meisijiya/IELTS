Task 2 — Water scarcity worldwide (problem-solution)

Water scarcity is a serious problem in many countries today. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can individuals and governments take to address it? (2021.2.27)

Fresh water is becoming harder to find in many parts of the world, and some countries now ration it every summer. This essay will discuss the main causes of water scarcity and the practical steps that individuals and governments can take to ease the situation.

The causes fall into two groups. On the demand side, world population is rising, cities are growing larger, and each person now uses more water than ever for showers, washing machines and private gardens. At the same time, agriculture already uses about seventy per cent of all fresh water and must produce more food to feed these bigger cities, often with irrigation methods that waste a great deal of it. On the supply side, climate change is altering rainfall patterns and lowering snow-melt, and groundwater is being pumped from aquifers faster than rain can refill them, so rivers such as the Colorado now run dry for long stretches each year.

Both governments and individuals have important roles to play. Governments should invest in leak-proof pipes, sea-water desalination plants and treated waste water that can be reused for industry or irrigation, and they should set clear water-pricing rules so heavy users pay a fair price. Individuals can help by installing water-saving toilets and showers, repairing dripping taps, watering gardens in the evening and choosing food produced with less thirsty crops. Together, these measures can substantially cut water use.

In conclusion, water scarcity is caused by both rising demand and shrinking supply, but neither is beyond our control. With combined government investment and small daily changes in household habits, countries can secure enough fresh water for their people in the coming decades.

TA：范文采用 problem-solution 标准结构，Introduction 以 This essay will discuss the main causes ... and the practical steps ... 一次性点明"原因 + 措施"两问任务，Body 1 用 demand side（population growth / urban growth / higher per-person use / agriculture）与 supply side（climate change / over-pumped groundwater / rivers running dry）回应"原因"部分，Body 2 用 government investment（leak-proof pipes / desalination / pricing）+ individual habit（water-saving fittings / repair taps / evening watering）回应"措施"部分，结尾以 can secure enough fresh water 总结两问答案，任务回应完整、字数 283 落在 270–290 区间。

CC：四段式（Introduction–Body 1–Body 2–Conclusion），每段一个明确主题；衔接以 On the demand side / At the same time / On the supply side / Both ... and / In conclusion 等显式逻辑词过渡，整体连贯但偏机械，CC 估测 6 档。LR：使用 water scarcity / fresh water / ration / desalination / reuse / infrastructure / aquifers / climate change / water-saving 等话题词，有 scarcity ↔ shortage / reduce ↔ cut 等同义替换，未堆砌难词。GRA：简单句与定语从句（water that can be reused）、分词结构（pumped from aquifers）、被动结构（is being pumped）混合；定冠词、单复数与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显错误，GRA 估测 6–6.5 档。
