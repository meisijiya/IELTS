Task 2 — Life change (agree-disagree)

People living in the twenty-first century have a better quality of life than people who lived in previous centuries. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2022.3.12)

Many people believe that those who are alive today enjoy a higher quality of life than people who lived in earlier centuries. In my opinion, I largely agree with this view, because modern advances in medicine, technology and daily convenience have removed many of the hardships that past generations had to endure.

There are three areas in which twenty-first-century life is clearly better. First, medical care has improved beyond recognition. Vaccines, antibiotics and modern surgery now save lives that would certainly have been lost a hundred years ago, so people live longer and suffer less from pain. A child born today in a developed country has a much higher chance of reaching adulthood than one born in 1900. Second, technology has made daily tasks far easier. Electricity, clean water, household appliances and mobile phones save hours of manual labour each week and give ordinary families access to information that only the rich once enjoyed. Third, transport and communication are cheaper and faster, which means families can stay in close touch even when they live far apart.

Admittedly, life today still has serious problems. The cost of housing in big cities is high, many people feel stressed or lonely, and pollution now threatens the natural environment that previous centuries took for granted. A worker in a nineteenth-century factory may have had fewer comforts, but he usually had stronger community ties and fresher air to breathe. Mental health issues have also become far more common.

In conclusion, although modern life brings new pressures, I still believe that the average person today has a better quality of life than his or her ancestors. The gains in health, comfort and connection outweigh the new problems we now face.

TA：本文为 Task 2「同意与否」题型，立场在开头以 In my opinion, I largely agree 明确表态，结尾用 I still believe 再次呼应，整文立场清晰，TA 满足要求。

CC：采用四段式：观点—三点理由—让步—结论；衔接词 First / Second / Third / Admittedly / In conclusion 过渡自然，但句式模板偏工整，符合 6 分水平。LR：用词以常见话题词为主（medical care、daily convenience、community ties），搭配少量替换（hardships、ancestors），未出现低频高难词。GRA：以并列复合句和状语从句为主，结构清楚、无明显语法错误，是一份稳定的 6 分范本。

  • largely agree — 基本同意
  • medical care — 医疗保健
  • daily convenience — 日常便利
  • community ties — 社区联系
  • modern life — 现代生活
  • past generations — 过去的世代
