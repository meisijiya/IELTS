Task 2 — Global contact vs national identity (discuss-both-views)

Some people think the increasing business and cultural contact between countries brings many positive effects. Others say it causes the loss of national identities. Discuss both of these views and give your own opinion. (2024.6.8)

立场: 倾向以开放为主，但需要主动保护核心文化符号; 理由1: 跨境合作带来经济繁荣与跨文化理解; 理由2: 国家身份可借语言/节庆与本土叙事保持活力

The accelerating exchange of business, films, cuisine and people between countries has prompted sharp debate over its effect on national identity. Some observers celebrate the openness, while others worry that distinctive cultures are being diluted into a uniform global blend.

Those who emphasise the benefits of international contact point to genuine economic and human gains. Shared standards and free trade have raised incomes, while cultural exchange has made ordinary citizens more curious about and tolerant of foreign traditions. A clear example is the way K-pop and Korean dramas have won devoted fans in Brazil, Egypt and Vietnam, generating millions in tourism and giving young Koreans pride in their creative industry. Without this cross-border conversation, communities would remain suspicious of one another and miss out on the friendship that comes from appreciating another culture.

Critics, however, argue that constant foreign influence erodes the stories, languages and customs that give a nation its sense of self. When local cinemas close because Hollywood blockbusters dominate the box office, when young people abandon national dishes for global fast food, and when regional dialects fade as English becomes the language of career success, distinctive identity quietly fades. Were governments to allow global culture to flow in unchecked, a country could eventually find itself rich but culturally unrecognisable.

In my view, international contact is overwhelmingly positive, but a nation must actively support its language, festivals and local media so that openness enriches identity rather than replacing it.

In conclusion, growing cultural and business ties deliver real prosperity and mutual understanding, yet national identity can still flourish when countries protect their languages, cuisines and stories, and the most balanced path welcomes the world while guarding what makes a nation distinctive.
