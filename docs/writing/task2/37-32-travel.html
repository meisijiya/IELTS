Task 2 — Travel abroad vs screens (discuss-both-views)

Some people think traveling abroad is necessary, while others think this is not necessary because TV and the internet can give the same information. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. (2021.6.26)

The question of whether travelling abroad is still necessary in an age of television documentaries and online travel videos has become increasingly common. While some argue that visual media can offer the same information as a real trip, others insist that seeing a place in person remains essential. This essay will discuss both views and offer my own opinion.

Those who believe travel abroad can be replaced by screens point out that modern media is now extremely rich and accessible. A student sitting at home can watch a high-definition documentary about the temples of Kyoto, take a virtual tour of the Louvre, or read detailed reviews of every restaurant in a foreign city. For someone on a tight budget or with caring duties at home, this kind of armchair travel is far cheaper, safer and more convenient. It also suits people whose main interest is in gathering facts and visual impressions, rather than in personal growth.

Supporters of real travel, however, argue that no screen can fully reproduce the experience of being physically present. Smells, sounds, daily conversations and the chance of an unexpected encounter cannot be recorded or streamed. Standing inside an ancient church, tasting street food at midnight or sharing a family meal with hosts in another country build memories and cross-cultural understanding in ways that a documentary cannot. Travel also teaches practical skills such as navigating a foreign city, speaking basic phrases and adapting to unfamiliar customs, which are valuable in a globalised workplace.

In my view, screens are excellent preparation but cannot replace travel altogether. The most rewarding experience usually comes from combining prior research with a real visit, so that travellers arrive informed and still curious to learn more.
