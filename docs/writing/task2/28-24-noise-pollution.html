Task 2 — Increasing noise pollution (problem-solution)

The level of noise around us is constantly increasing and is affecting the quality of our lives. What causes this noise, and what should be done to reduce it? (2022.10.22)

The cities and towns most of us live in are now far louder than they were a generation ago, and this rising noise is harming sleep, concentration and long-term health. This essay will examine the main causes of the noise we hear every day and suggest practical measures to bring it under control.

The causes of noise are mainly man-made and can be grouped into three areas. First, transport: traffic of cars, lorries and buses produces steady engine and horn noise that lasts most of the day, while aircraft taking off and trains passing through cities add sudden loud peaks. Second, construction: the building of new roads, apartments and shopping centres brings months of drilling, hammering and heavy machinery to residential streets. Third, leisure and commercial activity: music from nightclubs, pubs and headphones, market loudspeakers, and even household appliances such as vacuum cleaners push the background noise level higher.

Reducing this noise requires action from governments, businesses and individuals. Local governments can introduce quieter-vehicle zones, ban night-time flights over housing and impose strict noise limits on construction sites, while making sure sound-insulating materials are used in new buildings. Manufacturers should design quieter engines, machines and appliances, and mark their products with a clear noise rating so buyers can choose. On a personal level, we can use public transport instead of driving, close our windows at night, and lower the volume on personal headphones. Together, these steps can noticeably reduce the noise burden of modern life.

In conclusion, our modern world is noisy mainly because of transport, construction and leisure activities, but the problem can be tackled. With clearer rules, quieter products and a little daily care, the level of noise around us can be brought to a healthier level.

TA：范文采用 problem-solution 标准结构，Introduction 以 This essay will examine the main causes ... and suggest practical measures ... 一次性点明"原因 + 措施"两问任务，Body 1 用 transport（traffic jams / aircraft / trains）+ construction（drilling / hammering）+ leisure & commercial（nightclubs / loudspeakers / home appliances）三组来源回应"原因"部分，Body 2 用 government regulation（quieter-vehicle zones / night-flight ban / noise limits）+ manufacturer design（quieter engines / noise rating）+ personal habit（public transport / close windows / lower volume）回应"措施"部分，结尾以 brought to a healthier level 总结两问答案，任务回应完整、字数 286 落在 270–290 区间。
