Task 2 — Modern tech vs traditional methods (discuss-both-views)

Some people believe using modern technology (digital photographs, blogs) is the best way to record and remember important personal events. Others think the traditional method (making photo albums, writing diaries) is better. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. (2022.5.12)

立场: 折中（两者互补）; 理由1: 数字方式便捷、可分享，便于日常记录; 理由2: 纸质方式更具情感价值与长期保存性，适合里程碑事件

The question of whether modern technology or traditional methods offer the best way to record important personal events continues to provoke debate. Some argue that digital photographs and blogs capture moments more conveniently than ever before, while others maintain that physical albums and handwritten diaries still hold a value that screens cannot match.

Those who favour modern technology point out that smartphones and cloud storage let people capture and share everyday moments almost effortlessly. A parent can upload a video of a child's first steps to relatives across continents within minutes, and the same files are automatically backed up against loss. Digital tools also make it easy to organise thousands of images by date and location, a task that previous generations found tedious with printed albums.

Advocates of traditional methods, however, emphasise that physical keepsakes carry an emotional weight that pixels rarely replicate. Holding a leather-bound wedding album on a silver anniversary, or reading a teenager's diary decades later, offers a tangible connection to the past that a thumbnail in a cloud folder cannot deliver. Paper records also survive changes in software, whereas digital archives can become unreadable when a platform shuts down. Were families to abandon physical albums entirely, they might lose the rituals of slowing down and reflecting that make memories meaningful in the first place.

In my view, neither approach is sufficient on its own. The most thoughtful practice combines the convenience of digital tools for everyday moments with the craftsmanship of a paper album for the milestones that deserve to be remembered for a lifetime.

In conclusion, both modern technology and traditional methods have genuine strengths, so a balanced approach that uses each where it performs best offers the most rewarding way to preserve personal history.
