Task 2 — Robots in human society (discuss-both-views)

Some people believe that robots are important to humans' future development, while others think that it is a dangerous invention that will impact society negatively. Discuss both views and give your opinion. (2022.9.24)

视角1: 机器人提升生产效率与医疗能力; 视角2: 机器人取代就业并引发伦理风险; 立场: 整体利大于弊但需监管

Whether intelligent robots will prove a blessing or a curse for humanity is one of the most hotly debated questions of our age. This essay will discuss both perspectives before explaining why, on balance, I believe robots can support human progress provided governments introduce clear ethical safeguards.

Supporters point out that robots excel at repetitive, dangerous or precision-driven tasks, freeing human workers for more creative roles. In modern car factories a single robotic arm can weld, paint and inspect a vehicle body with almost zero defects, while in hospitals surgical robots allow doctors to perform minimally invasive operations that shorten patient recovery. A clear example is the da Vinci system, which has already helped millions of patients return home within days of complex surgery.

Critics, however, argue that rapid robotisation poses a threat to ordinary workers whose jobs are most easily automated, and that military or care robots raise troubling ethical questions. Whole categories of clerical, warehouse and driving work may disappear within a decade, leaving low-skilled adults with nowhere to turn, while autonomous weapons could make decisions about life and death without a human conscience. Were such trends left unmanaged, inequality would worsen, eroding the social cohesion that technological progress is supposed to enhance.

My own view is that robots are likely to be a net benefit, because the productivity gains can fund retraining programmes and better public services. The crucial task for policymakers is to update labour laws, invest in lifelong education and regulate lethal applications before ordinary citizens pay the price of unregulated innovation.

In conclusion, robots are neither inherently good nor evil, and their impact on society will depend on whether humans design the rules wisely before the machines outpace our ability to govern them.

TA：本文采用 discuss-both-views 五段结构，开头 This essay will discuss both perspectives 引出双方观点，Body 1 与 Body 2 分别阐述支持 / 反对两条立场，第四段以 My own view 明确表态，结尾给出条件性总结；全文覆盖题目全部要点，字数 288 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
