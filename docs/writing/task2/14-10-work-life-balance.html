Task 2 — Work-life balance (problem-solution)

Currently, many people fail to balance work with other parts of their lives. What are the reasons and how to solve them? (2023.7.15)

In today's competitive job market, many people find it difficult to strike a balance between their work and other aspects of life. This essay will examine the main reasons for this problem and suggest some practical solutions.

There are two major causes behind this imbalance. First, the working hours in many modern companies have become longer than before. With the rise of smartphones and laptop computers, employees are expected to answer emails and finish tasks even after they have left the office. This blurs the boundary between working time and personal time, leaving workers feeling exhausted. Over time, this constant pressure can lead to burnout and anxiety. Second, the cost of living in cities has risen sharply, so many adults have to take on a second job or do overtime to cover expenses such as rent and school fees. As a result, they have very little energy left for their families or hobbies.

To address these causes, two practical measures can be taken. Companies should introduce clearer rules about after-work communication, for example by setting a fixed time at which official messages stop, and managers should respect employees' non-working hours. At the same time, governments could offer subsidies for childcare and public housing, which would reduce the financial pressure on households and remove the need for extra work. Schools and communities can also run workshops that teach time-management skills, helping individuals to use their free hours more wisely. On a personal level, workers should also learn to set clear limits.

In conclusion, the failure to balance work and life stems mainly from long working hours and rising living costs, but both employers and governments can take concrete steps to help workers lead healthier and more balanced lives.

TA：本文为 Task 2「问题与解决方案」题型，开头先用 In today's competitive job market 同义改写原题并以 This essay will examine… and suggest… 明确点出"原因 + 方案"两条线，结尾 In conclusion 呼应，立场与结构完整。

CC：段落分明、每段一个主题，衔接词 First / Second / To address these causes / At the same time / In conclusion 串联清晰；段落之间话题推进顺畅，符合 6 分水平。LR：以常用话题词（work-life balance、burnout、subsidies）为主，配少量替换（strike a balance、time-management skills、non-working hours），未使用低频高难词。GRA：以简单复合句与并列句为主，从句与连接词使用基本准确，整体清晰、无明显语法错误，是一份稳定的 6 分范本。
