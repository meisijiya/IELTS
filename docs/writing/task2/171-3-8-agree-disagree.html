Task 2 — Better quality of life today (agree-disagree)

People living in the twenty-first century have a better quality of life than people who lived in previous centuries. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2022.3.12)

立场: 强烈同意; 理由1: 医疗与营养使寿命与健康大幅改善; 理由2: 数字技术拓宽教育、自由与全球联系

Some people argue that the quality of life enjoyed by citizens of the twenty-first century has not really improved compared with that of earlier generations. I firmly disagree with this view, because medical progress, material abundance and digital connection have produced an everyday existence that previous centuries could hardly have imagined.

The first reason is that health and longevity have improved beyond recognition. Vaccines, antibiotics and routine surgery have turned diseases that once killed millions into manageable conditions, and average life expectancy across most regions has risen by several decades. People who would once have died in childhood now reach old age in reasonable comfort, supported by modern nutrition, sanitation and emergency medicine. A clear example is the experience of ordinary parents today, who can reasonably expect to see their grandchildren grow up, a hope that was statistically unrealistic for most families two hundred years ago.

The second reason is that digital technology has widened access to education, entertainment and global contact. A teenager with a smartphone can watch lectures from world-class universities, speak with relatives across continents and run a small business from a bedroom, possibilities that earlier generations could access only through years of travel and privilege. Were governments ever to restrict these freedoms, ordinary citizens would feel an immediate and painful loss, which is itself evidence of how much the modern quality of life now depends on connectivity. The convenience alone, from mobile banking to instant translation, transforms daily routines in ways that previous centuries never approached.

In conclusion, despite genuine worries about pollution and inequality, the twenty-first century offers a substantially better quality of life than previous eras, thanks to longer healthy years and to digital tools that broaden almost every dimension of ordinary experience.
