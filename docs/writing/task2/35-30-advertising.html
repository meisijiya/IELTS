Task 2 — Advertising aim (agree-disagree)

Some people say that the main aim of advertising is to improve the sales of products that people do not really need. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.7.17)

The question of whether the main purpose of advertising is to push products that consumers do not really need has attracted much debate. While I accept that this description fits some modern marketing campaigns, I strongly disagree with it as a full picture, because advertising also performs several useful economic functions that benefit both companies and ordinary shoppers.

There are two main reasons why I hold this position. First, advertising gives consumers useful information that helps them compare prices and quality before they buy. A family shopping for a new washing machine can learn about energy ratings, warranty periods and after-sale service from a single advertisement, rather than visit several shops. Without such information, shoppers would waste time and might still make a poor choice. In this sense, advertising reduces the cost of searching, lowers transaction costs and supports more efficient markets.

Second, advertising does not always push unnecessary products. Many campaigns introduce genuinely useful items, such as low-energy light bulbs, healthy school lunches or child safety seats, which buyers may not have considered without the prompt. Admittedly, some advertisements do exaggerate or appeal to insecurity, encouraging shoppers to buy things they cannot afford. However, this problem is caused by dishonest advertisers rather than advertising itself, and stronger regulation can address it without banning the whole industry.

In conclusion, I firmly believe that the main aim of advertising is not simply to sell unnecessary products. It informs consumers, supports fair competition and often introduces useful innovations that improve daily life. What society needs is clearer rules to prevent dishonest campaigns, not a sweeping dismissal of advertising itself, because a healthy advertising sector ultimately benefits every household.
