Task 2 — Dependent or independent individuals (discuss-both-views)

Some people think individuals are more and more dependent on each other. Others believe we are more and more independent. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. (2021.7.24)

立场: 折中（技术带来协作依赖，也强化个人选择）; 理由1: 数字平台使人与人紧密协作; 理由2: 个体自主与生活方式多样化

The question of whether modern life is making people more dependent on one another or, conversely, more independent, is widely debated. Some observers argue that we are growing ever more interconnected, while others maintain that individuals now enjoy unprecedented autonomy.

Those who emphasise growing dependence point to the rapid spread of digital platforms and specialised services. A single working day may involve an online team meeting, a food delivery arranged through an app, a streaming subscription, and a home repaired by a stranger booked on a marketplace website. None of these tasks can be completed without relying on dozens of other people, often invisible to the user. The COVID-19 pandemic made this interdependence even clearer, since remote workers depended on colleagues, neighbours and delivery drivers to keep their daily routines alive.

Advocates of the independence view, however, stress that individuals today can shape their own lives with far less interference than in the past. Remote work frees employees from fixed offices, allowing them to choose where, when and how they earn a living. Digital banking removes the need to visit a local branch, and online learning lets students acquire skills without enrolling in a traditional institution. Compared with their parents' generation, young adults are far more able to construct careers, identities and households that do not follow a single standard template.

In my view, both trends are happening at the same time. Modern technology creates new forms of collaboration, but it also magnifies the freedom of the individual; the two forces are complementary rather than opposed.

In conclusion, I believe that dependence and independence are best understood as two sides of the same coin in contemporary society.
