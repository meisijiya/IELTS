Task 2 — International sporting events (discuss-both-views)

Some people think hosting major international sporting events brings a lot of advantages to a country, while others believe there are more disadvantages. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. (2023.4.8)

立场: 弊大于利; 理由1: 支持者强调旅游收入、基建升级与民族凝聚力; 理由2: 反对者指出场馆沉没成本、挤压民生支出与本地居民负担。

Hosting major international sporting events has become a status symbol for ambitious nations. Some commentators insist that the prestige and economic rewards are well worth the cost, while others argue that the disadvantages outweigh any such benefits. Both perspectives merit careful consideration.

Advocates point to several clear advantages. International exposure of this scale leads to a surge in tourism, both during the event and for years afterwards, as visitors return to explore the host region. Investment in transport, hotels and stadiums leaves behind infrastructure that benefits residents long after the closing ceremony, and the surge of national pride can foster a stronger sense of social cohesion. A clear example is how the 2008 Beijing Games accelerated the development of an entire district and projected a modern image to the world, attracting sustained investment from foreign companies in the years that followed.

Critics, however, highlight equally serious drawbacks. The cost of building world-class venues frequently overshoots original budgets, and many facilities deteriorate into white elephants once the spotlight moves elsewhere. Public funds that could improve hospitals, schools or public transport are diverted into short-lived spectacles, and local residents may be priced out of their own neighbourhoods as property values rise. Were such costs to be ignored, the long-term social benefits would be far smaller than politicians promise.

In my view, the disadvantages deserve more weight than they usually receive, because the visible glamour of hosting rarely translates into lasting gains for ordinary citizens.

In conclusion, although international sporting events can showcase a nation and upgrade infrastructure, the financial and social risks are considerable, and host countries should enter such bids with their eyes open.
