Task 2 — Social media replacing face-to-face (advantage-disadvantage)

The use of social media, such as Facebook and Twitter, are replacing face-to-face contact in this century. Do you think the advantages of this development outweigh the disadvantages? (2021.11.6)

立场: 利大于弊; 理由1: 社交媒体扩大社交范围、降低沟通成本; 理由2: 真实人际接触仍在深层关系中不可替代，但可与线上互补

Many people today prefer to maintain friendships and family ties through social media platforms such as Facebook and Twitter rather than meeting in person. This essay will weigh the main advantages and disadvantages of that shift and argue that the benefits, on balance, outweigh the drawbacks.

The main advantage is that digital platforms allow people to keep in touch with a far wider circle at almost no cost. A migrant worker on the other side of the world can share photographs with parents every week, coordinate study groups with classmates abroad, and follow the everyday milestones of old friends who would otherwise drift out of contact. For introverts, the elderly and those living in remote areas, these low-pressure channels can be a lifeline that traditional face-to-face meetings could never match in frequency or convenience.

There are, however, several disadvantages. Online exchanges tend to be shallow and easy to misinterpret, which means they rarely build the deep trust that comes from sharing a meal or working through a problem side by side. Heavy users often report feelings of loneliness precisely because their social lives have moved onto screens, and studies link excessive scrolling to weaker face-to-face conversational skills. A clear example is the way many young professionals feel anxious when forced to attend a social event without their phones as a buffer.

In my view, the sensible approach is to treat online and offline contact as complementary rather than competing. The advantages of social media are genuine, but the most meaningful relationships still require unhurried time together.

In conclusion, while replacing face-to-face contact with social media does weaken certain interpersonal skills, the broader reach and accessibility it provides mean that its advantages outweigh its disadvantages for most users.
