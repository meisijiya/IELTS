Task 2 — Gender and jobs (agree-disagree)

The different qualities of men and women mean that they are suited to different kinds of jobs. Do you agree or disagree? (2022.11.5)

Some people argue that because men and women have different qualities, they should be guided towards different kinds of work. In my opinion, I disagree with this view. Although men and women may on average have different strengths, modern workplaces need a wide range of skills, and individuals should be free to choose the job that matches their own talents.

There are two main reasons why I hold this position. First, the qualities often linked to gender, such as physical strength, communication style or emotional sensitivity, are not fixed by biology alone. Education, upbringing and personal interest shape these qualities just as much. A woman can become an excellent engineer, and a man can become a careful nurse, as countless examples in many countries already show. Schools and workplaces should therefore judge each person as an individual rather than rely on a stereotype. Second, dividing jobs by gender would waste a large amount of human talent. If half of the population is steered away from certain careers simply because of their sex, society loses the skills, ideas and energy of those workers, and the economy grows more slowly than it otherwise could.

Admittedly, some jobs do still depend on physical features that differ between men and women, such as certain heavy manual tasks. However, most modern work is intellectual, creative or service-based, and here the only things that matter are ability, training and motivation, which both men and women can develop in equal measure.

In conclusion, I believe that people should be chosen for jobs on the basis of their personal ability and interest, not on the assumption that one gender naturally suits a particular role. Equality of opportunity is what allows a society to use all its talent well.

TA：本文为 Task 2「同意与否」题型，开头以 In my opinion, I disagree 明确表态，结尾用 I believe 再次呼应，整文立场一致、回答完整，TA 满足要求。

CC：采用四段式：观点—双理由—让步—结论；衔接词 First / Second / Admittedly / In conclusion 清晰连贯，段落功能划分合理，符合 6 分水平。LR：用词以常见话题词为主（gender、quality、talent、opportunity），搭配少量替换（biological、intellectual、assumption），未出现低频高难词。GRA：以复合句和状语从句为主，结构清楚、无明显语法错误，是一份稳定的 6 分范本。
