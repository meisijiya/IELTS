Task 2 — CEO salary gap (agree-disagree)

Large companies should pay higher salaries to CEOs and executives compared to other workers. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2023.2.11)

立场: 不同意, 高管高薪有损企业与信任; 理由1: 巨大薪酬差距打击员工士气与忠诚; 理由2: 高管高薪未必反映真实业绩

There is a widespread debate over whether large companies should pay their chief executives and senior managers far more than their ordinary employees. While I accept that leadership roles carry heavy responsibility, I disagree with the view that enormous executive pay is justified, because such gaps tend to damage morale and distort public trust.

The first problem is the corrosive effect that pay gaps exert on workplace morale. When workers see that a chief executive earns several hundred times their own salary, especially during periods of wage stagnation or redundancy, feelings of resentment and disengagement quickly grow, and the sense of being part of a single team is gradually lost. A clear example is the wave of strikes and union campaigns that have recently hit major airlines and retail chains, where frontline staff highlighted the contrast between their own modest pay and the multi-million-pound bonuses awarded to senior leaders. Such imbalances rarely encourage teamwork and often push valuable employees to look for work elsewhere.

A second problem is that excessive executive pay is not always linked to genuine performance. Many boards award generous packages to chief executives whose strategies later destroy shareholder value, while frontline workers who keep the company running day to day are routinely overlooked despite their direct contribution to customer satisfaction. Were the link between contribution and reward to be clearer, executive compensation would probably align more closely with long-term success, benefiting both the company and its wider workforce.

In conclusion, I still believe that excessive executive salaries should not be the norm, because they undermine morale and weaken the connection between effort and reward that holds a company together.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段结构，开头以 I disagree with the view that enormous executive pay is justified 明确表态（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 与 Body 2 分别给出两条独立理由（士气受损 / 业绩脱钩），结尾以 I still believe that excessive executive salaries should not be the norm 重申立场，字数 287 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。
