Task 2 — Good teaching vs individual ability (agree-disagree)

A good teaching is more important for academic success than individual's ability. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2022.7.7)

立场: 个人能力比好教学更重要; 理由1: 个人天赋与认知水平决定学业上限; 理由2: 自学动力与自律可弥补教学短板

Some people believe that the quality of teaching is the most decisive factor behind academic success, outweighing the role of an individual's own ability. While I fully recognise the immense value that skilled teachers bring to any classroom, I maintain that personal ability and motivation ultimately exert a greater influence on how well a student performs.

The first reason is that innate cognitive ability sets a ceiling on what even the most inspiring teacher can achieve. A clear example is the consistent gap observed between students taught in similar classrooms: those who enter school with stronger reasoning skills and broader vocabularies almost always finish ahead of their classmates, no matter how gifted the instructor is. Were teaching quality alone to determine outcomes, this gap would have largely disappeared, yet decades of educational research continue to show that early cognitive readiness predicts later achievement far more accurately than any school-level variable.

The second reason is that motivation, discipline and the capacity for self-directed learning, all of which are internal to the student, allow capable learners to compensate for mediocre teaching. In many under-resourced regions, young people from poorly equipped schools still gain admission to leading universities because they have cultivated strong habits and a clear sense of purpose. Furthermore, in an era when high-quality lectures are freely available online, the learners who actually finish their courses and apply the material are those who already possess the inner drive to keep working when no one is watching.

In conclusion, although good teaching can substantially enhance learning, I believe that an individual's own ability and motivation remain the dominant forces behind genuine academic success.
