Task 2 — Less comfortable life for environment (agree-disagree)

The key to solving environmental problems is simple: the present generation is to accept a less comfortable life for the sake of the future generation. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2021.12.4)

立场: 同意但需要智慧地承担, 而非盲目牺牲; 理由1: 当代人确有义务为后代保留宜居地球, 部分舒适消费必须让步; 理由2: 真正可持续的「不那么舒适」应由技术创新、政策引导与公平负担来分担, 不应只落在个体消费者身上。

The claim that solving environmental problems requires the present generation to accept a less comfortable life for the sake of future generations has stirred considerable debate. While the inconvenience should not be romanticised, I substantially agree with this argument, because the climate and biodiversity crises are largely the result of present overconsumption and cannot be resolved without some restraint today.

The first reason is that the current scale of resource use is plainly unsustainable. Average per-person carbon emissions in wealthy countries remain far above the level scientists consider safe, and the natural systems on which future generations depend, from rainfall to fertile soils, are visibly deteriorating. Unless today's consumers rein in damaging habits such as frequent flying, oversized homes and excessive meat consumption, the burden will simply fall on children who had no say in those choices. Accepting a more modest standard of living is therefore not a punishment but a moral investment in the basic preconditions of human life.

The second reason is that restraint should be met intelligently rather than uniformly. Technological innovation, well-designed policy and progressive taxation can make a less-comfortable life unnecessary for many households, while ensuring the heaviest cuts fall on those who consume the most. Dense cities, efficient public transport and clean energy grids can preserve convenience while slashing emissions, and poorer families should never be asked to bear costs they cannot afford. Were governments to frame the transition as a fair shared effort rather than individual sacrifice, public support for ambitious goals would actually grow.

In conclusion, I still believe that some reduction in present comfort is essential to safeguard the future, provided that the burden is shared fairly and supported by technology and policy that make sustainable living genuinely attractive.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段式：开头以 I substantially agree with this argument 一次性表态，Body 1 用当前不可持续的资源消耗 + 代际公平论支撑（The average per-person carbon emissions ... remain far above the level scientists consider safe），Body 2 用技术创新 + 公平分担补充 nuance（Were governments to frame the transition as a fair shared effort ...），结尾 I still believe that some reduction in present comfort is essential ... provided that the burden is shared fairly 二次呼应立场并提出条件；字数 288 落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段衔接以 The first reason / The second reason / For example / Were governments to frame / In conclusion 显式逻辑词过渡；段内以「Unless today's consumers rein in ...」+ 反事实虚拟（Were governments to frame ...）+ 让步分句（while the inconvenience should not be romanticised）推动论述，层次清晰。LR：使用 carbon emission / biodiversity crisis / fertile soil / per-person consumption / progressive taxation / clean energy grid / public support / fair shared effort 等 7 分段话题词，搭配准确且有 overconsumption ↔ excessive consumption / burden ↔ cost 同义替换。GRA：复合句比例高，包括定语从句（natural systems on which both present and future generations depend）、反事实虚拟倒装（Were governments to frame the transition as fair shared effort ...）、让步分句（provided that the burden is shared fairly and supported by technology），时态与主谓一致准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
