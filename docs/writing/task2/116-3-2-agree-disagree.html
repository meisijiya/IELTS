Task 2 — Shopping habits and age (agree-disagree)

People's shopping habits depend more on the age group they belong to than any other factor. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2022.9.10)

立场: 不同意，年龄并非最强因素; 理由1: 个人可支配收入与经济状况对购物选择影响更大; 理由2: 文化背景与个人价值观同样能凌驾于代际习惯。

Some people claim that age is the most powerful factor shaping what and how consumers buy. While I accept that different generations do show distinct patterns, I disagree that age outweighs every other influence, because personal income and individual values exert just as much pressure on shopping behaviour.

The first reason is that disposable income and economic circumstances tend to dominate purchasing decisions regardless of age. A teenager from a wealthy household may prefer designer brands and overseas holidays, while an elderly pensioner living on a modest state benefit is likely to prioritise daily necessities and discount stores. Were income to be ignored, no marketing model could explain why two consumers in the same age bracket often make very different purchases. In reality, household budgets, mortgage commitments and job security shape buying choices far more immediately than the year a person was born.

The second reason is that cultural background and personal beliefs frequently override generational habits. A young vegetarian, for instance, may shop very differently from a meat-eating peer of the same age, just as a religious consumer avoids certain products that others of the same generation purchase freely. Admittedly, age does colour preferences for fashion or technology, yet these are subtle variations rather than the defining force behind consumer behaviour, and a retailer who targets customers only by age will quickly miss most of the intended market. Family upbringing and personal ethics, by contrast, often dictate far more enduring patterns of consumption.

In conclusion, although age influences taste and brand loyalty, it is income and personal values that ultimately drive the way people shop, and the claim that age outweighs every other factor is therefore overstated.
