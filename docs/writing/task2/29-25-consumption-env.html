Task 2 — Consumer goods and environmental damage (problem-solution)

The increase in the production of consumer goods results in damage to the natural environment. What are the causes of this, and what can be done to solve this problem? (2022.8.13)

The world is producing more clothes, electronic devices, furniture and packaged food than ever before, and this boom in consumer goods is leaving deep marks on the natural environment. This essay will discuss why the damage is happening and what can be done to ease it.

Two main forces drive the damage. First, demand is high and rising. New fashions appear every season, electronic products are replaced long before they wear out, and online shopping makes it effortless to buy something new on the day it is wanted. Second, making and shipping these goods is heavy on resources. Factories burn coal to power machines, take fresh water to dye textiles, and discharge chemical waste into rivers; long-distance shipping adds further emissions. Together, these forces have turned ordinary consumption into one of the biggest causes of pollution.

The problems can be reduced through action at three levels. Governments should set strict emissions rules for factories, ban the most polluting materials, and require companies to take back their old products at the end of their life. Businesses should redesign their goods so they last longer and can be easily repaired, while telling customers honestly how each item is made. As consumers, we can buy fewer but better things, choose items with clear eco-labels, repair what we already own, and resell or recycle what we no longer use. Combined, these three layers of action can slow the pressure that production puts on nature.

In conclusion, rising demand and resource-heavy production are the main causes of environmental damage from consumer goods, yet the issue is solvable. With stronger government rules, smarter business design and more thoughtful shopping, the link between buying things and harming nature can be greatly weakened.

TA：范文采用 problem-solution 标准结构，Introduction 以 This essay will discuss why ... and what governments, businesses and shoppers can do ... 一次性点明"原因 + 措施"两问任务，Body 1 用 demand side（fast fashion / early replacement / online shopping）+ production side（coal power / water use / chemical waste / long-distance shipping）回应"原因"部分，Body 2 用 government regulation（emissions rules / material bans / take-back recycling）+ business redesign（longer lasting / recycled material / repairable）+ consumer habit（fewer but better / eco-labels / repair / resell）回应"措施"部分，结尾以 greatly weakened 总结两问答案，任务回应完整、字数 283 落在 270–290 区间。

CC：四段式（Introduction–Body 1–Body 2–Conclusion），每段一个明确主题；衔接以 First / Second / Together, these / As consumers / Combined, these / In conclusion 等显式逻辑词过渡，整体连贯但偏机械，CC 估测 6 档。LR：使用 consumer goods / emissions / textile / dye / chemical waste / long-distance shipping / eco-labels / take-back / recycling 等话题词，有 harm ↔ damage / stop ↔ ban / reduce ↔ cut down 等同义替换，未堆砌难词。GRA：简单句与定语从句（goods so they last longer / items with clear eco-labels）、被动结构（are replaced long before they wear out）、分词结构（adding further to emissions）混合；定冠词、单复数与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显错误，GRA 估测 6–6.5 档。
