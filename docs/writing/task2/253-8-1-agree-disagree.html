Task 2 — Global cooperation on urgent problems (agree-disagree)

Many people feel that most of the urgent problems can only be solved by international cooperation. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2023.11.25)

立场: 总体同意，因跨境危机与全球公共品离不开多边合作; 理由1: 跨境危机（气候/疫情）超出一国能力; 理由2: 信息共享与联合科研让小国也能受益

Many people believe that the world's most urgent problems, from climate change to pandemics, can only be tackled through cooperation among nations. I largely agree with this view, because the most pressing threats today cross borders by nature, and no single country, however powerful, can address them alone.

The first reason is that the most urgent modern crises are inherently transnational. Climate change raises sea levels that flood coastlines in many countries at once, pandemics spread through global air travel within weeks, and financial instability in one market can wipe out pensions on the other side of the world. A clear example is the coordinated vaccine research during COVID-19, where scientists from dozens of countries shared data and clinical trials, producing effective vaccines far faster than any single laboratory could have managed. Without such cooperation, millions more lives would have been lost.

The second reason is that international cooperation allows knowledge and resources to reach smaller nations that lack the capacity to solve problems on their own. When richer countries fund meteorological stations in Africa, share virus samples with regional laboratories or co-operate on disaster early-warning systems, the benefits extend far beyond the donors, lifting health and safety standards across entire regions. Joint research projects also build trust between governments, lowering the risk of future conflict and making future cooperation easier. Were every country to retreat into isolation, the scientific and humanitarian gains of the last century would quickly unravel.

In conclusion, I still believe that urgent global problems require international cooperation, because crises cross borders by their very nature and shared effort delivers results that no isolated nation can match in speed or scale.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段结构，开头以 I largely agree 明确表态（避免 whether 开头），Body 1 与 Body 2 分别给出两条独立理由（跨境危机 / 信息共享），结尾以 I still believe that urgent global problems require international cooperation 再次呼应立场，全文立场贯穿无摇摆，字数落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段式结构清晰，每段一个主题句 + 展开理由 + 例证或反事实假设（Were every country to retreat into isolation）；段内以 The first reason / The second reason / A clear example is / When richer countries / In conclusion 等显式逻辑连接过渡，行文连贯且推进有层次，CC 估测 7 档。LR：使用 transnational crises / pandemics / financial instability / clinical trials / meteorological stations / early-warning systems / humanitarian gains 等话题搭配，搭配准确且有同义替换（urgent ↔ pressing / cooperation ↔ joint effort），LR 估测 7 档。GRA：复合句占比较高，包含定语从句（vaccines that saved millions of lives）、过去虚拟条件倒装（Were every country to retreat into isolation）、现在分词短语（producing effective vaccines far faster）；主谓一致、时态与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
