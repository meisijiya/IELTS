Task 2 — Olympic Games value (discuss-both-views)

Some people think that the Olympic Games is an exciting event and can bring nations together. Others, however, think that it is a waste of money. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. (2022.7.16)

立场: 值得举办但需控制成本; 理由1: 奥运促进国际理解与文化交流; 理由2: 但常因场馆浪费与债务被批评

Whether hosting the Olympic Games is a worthy use of public money or a costly distraction has sparked debate in countries around the world. Some people emphasise the inspirational and unifying power of the event, while others argue that it drains national budgets. Both views carry weight and merit closer examination.

Supporters of the Olympics point out that the Games bring athletes, spectators and media from every corner of the globe into one host city, creating an unparalleled atmosphere of shared excitement. Beyond the sporting drama, the event allows ordinary citizens to encounter cultures very different from their own, encouraging mutual respect and easing the tensions that can build up between nations during periods of political strain. A clear example is the 1988 Seoul Games, which many historians credit with helping to soften global perceptions of South Korea and to lay the groundwork for its subsequent democratic and economic rise.

Critics counter that the financial and social costs of hosting are often far higher than the promised benefits. Several recent Games have left host cities with white-elephant stadiums, spiralling debts and displaced local communities, while the actual sporting infrastructure lies unused for most of the year. Were a developing country to redirect the billions spent on a single Olympics towards hospitals, schools and climate resilience, the long-term welfare gains for ordinary citizens would almost certainly be greater than any short-lived boost in national prestige.

Having considered both perspectives carefully, I tend to support the view that the Olympics can be worthwhile when budgets are tightly controlled and venues are planned for long-term public use, but I accept that host cities should not sacrifice essential services in order to stage the event.
