Task 2 — Technology: benefits vs disadvantages (discuss-both-views)

Some people think technology mostly benefits our lives, while others believe it brings more disadvantages. Discuss both views and give your own opinions. (2025.7.5)

视角1: 技术提升效率、健康与教育; 视角2: 技术引发失业、隐私与沉迷; 立场: 利大于弊但需妥善管理

The question of whether modern technology mostly helps or mostly harms our lives has become one of the defining debates of the century. This essay will consider both sides before explaining why, on balance, I believe the advantages outweigh the disadvantages provided that governments and individuals manage the risks carefully.

Those who emphasise the benefits point to the dramatic gains in productivity, health and education that smart machines have made possible. Agricultural sensors now warn farmers of crop disease weeks before harvest, while remote surgery allows specialists in capital cities to save lives in distant rural hospitals. A clear example is the way online learning platforms have allowed students in low-income areas to study alongside peers at elite universities, narrowing gaps that once seemed impossible to close.

Critics, however, argue that the same innovations have produced an underclass of workers whose skills have been rendered obsolete, and an epidemic of anxiety among teenagers glued to their screens. Routine clerical jobs have already disappeared, and constant notifications fragment attention in ways that damage mental health. Were societies to ignore these pressures, social inequality would deepen and a generation of young people would grow up dependent on devices they cannot put down.

My own view is that the advantages of technology do outweigh the disadvantages, but only when the gains are shared widely and the harms are openly discussed. Investment in retraining, mental-health services and digital regulation can ensure that ordinary families benefit from innovation rather than simply paying its hidden costs.

In conclusion, technology is neither an unalloyed blessing nor a curse, and the outcome depends on whether societies choose to govern it wisely or simply let it run unchecked into every corner of daily life.
