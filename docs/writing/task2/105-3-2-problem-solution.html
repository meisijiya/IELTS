Task 2 — Cash instead of gifts (problem-solution)

In more and more countries, people choose to give money on special occasions rather than giving gifts chosen personally. Why might this be the case? Is it a positive or negative development? (2025.3.8)

立场: 总体积极; 理由1: 现金满足实际需求并避免浪费; 理由2: 但失去个人心意与情感联结

The aim of this essay is to explore the reasons why an increasing number of people now prefer to give cash on special occasions rather than carefully chosen presents, and to evaluate whether this shift represents a positive or negative development.

There are several compelling reasons behind the move towards cash gifts. Modern consumers often live in small flats with limited storage space, so bulky presents risk becoming clutter, while a bank transfer or envelope of money can be put towards something the recipient genuinely needs. Online shopping and digital payment platforms have also made cash gifts effortless to send across long distances, which is particularly useful when family members live in different cities or countries. A clear example is the rise of mobile payment apps in China, where red envelopes sent through WeChat have become a default way to mark weddings, birthdays and the Lunar New Year.

From my perspective, this trend is largely a positive development because it removes much of the guesswork from gift-giving and reduces wasted spending on unwanted items. Nevertheless, it does carry one notable drawback: when money replaces a thoughtfully chosen present, the personal connection between giver and receiver can weaken, and the moment of unwrapping a carefully selected item is lost. Were every special occasion to be marked only by a transfer of funds, some of the warmth and shared anticipation that small rituals bring to families and friends would inevitably fade.

In conclusion, the main reasons for the move to cash gifts are practicality and digital convenience, and on balance I consider this development to be largely positive, as long as people occasionally combine money with a personal note or small symbolic item to preserve the emotional side of the occasion.
