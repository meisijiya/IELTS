Task 2 — History vs business (agree-disagree)

Some people think that there could be more benefit to society if more people studied business than history. To what extent do you agree or disagree? (2025.1.18 / 2022.12.17)

立场: 不同意商业挤占历史; 理由1: 历史训练批判性思维与判断力; 理由2: 历史塑造公民身份与共同价值

Some people argue that society would benefit more if a larger number of students chose to study business rather than history. I firmly disagree with this view, because history offers intrinsic value that no commercial discipline can replicate, and abandoning it would impoverish both individual minds and the public life they share.

The first reason is that the study of history cultivates critical thinking and informed judgement, qualities that a narrow business curriculum seldom develops. When students examine primary sources, weigh conflicting accounts of the same event, and trace long-term causes and consequences, they learn to question confident claims rather than accept them at face value. A clear example is the way young economists who understand the Great Depression can anticipate how loose monetary policy affects ordinary households. Without this historical grounding, business graduates might prioritise short-term profit and overlook systemic risks such as financial bubbles or environmental damage.

The second reason is that history shapes a shared civic identity, a benefit that purely vocational study cannot deliver. A society whose citizens ignore how their institutions were built, and at what cost, will gradually lose the moral compass needed to defend those institutions. When young people study their nation's long struggle for democracy, they are far more likely to value press freedom, minority rights and the rule of law in their working life. Were business courses to crowd history out of the curriculum, future leaders might pursue efficiency at any price, neglecting the cultural roots that hold communities together.

In conclusion, I still believe that history deserves a central place in higher education, because it sharpens judgement and anchors citizens in the values that sustain a healthy society.
