Task 2 — Family vs outside influence (agree-disagree)

The family has a great influence on children's development, but the influence outside the home plays a bigger part in children's life. Do you agree or disagree? (2021.6.5)

People often disagree about which shapes a child more strongly: the family or the world outside the home. While I accept that family provides the first and deepest influence, I disagree with the statement that outside influences play a bigger part overall.

There are two main reasons why the family remains so important. First, parents are a child's first teachers. The way a family talks, the values it shares and the habits it follows set the basic patterns of behaviour, language and emotion that the child carries into later life. A child who grows up hearing respect and patience at home usually treats others the same way at school. By contrast, a child whose parents are harsh or absent may struggle to form trusting relationships, even when kind teachers and friends are waiting outside. Second, family influence is constant and close. Children spend their most important years with parents and siblings, so the lessons of home are repeated every day, while school and social life only cover a few hours.

Admittedly, outside influences are growing stronger. Classmates, television, the internet and neighbourhood culture all shape a child's ideas, especially during adolescence. Yet these forces do not arrive in a vacuum. A young person usually interprets them through the values first learned at home, and tends to reject messages that conflict with those values. In this sense, outside influences build on the family base rather than replace it.

In conclusion, I still believe that the family has the greatest influence on children's development. A supportive home gives children a stable foundation, which helps them choose wisely among the many voices they hear outside.

TA：本文为 Task 2「同意与否」题型，开头段以 I disagree 明确表态（避免 whether），结尾段用 I still believe 再次呼应，TA 立场清晰且保持一致。

CC：四段式结构完整，主题段分别论述家庭影响的两点理由，结尾段让步+总结；衔接词 First / Second / Admittedly / By contrast / In conclusion 清晰，符合 6 分水平。LR：用词以基本话题词（family、development、influence、child、values）为主，配以替换表达（outside the home、siblings、adolescent），未出现低频高难词。GRA：以复合句和并列结构为主，从句（that ... which ...）控制尚可，整体清晰、无明显语法错误，是一份稳定的 6 分范本。
