Task 2 — Cars and motorcycles overuse (problem-solution)

Many people continue to use cars and motorcycles even though they know that they are bad for the environment. Why is this? What can be done to reduce the use of these vehicles? (2022.5.28)

The private car and motorcycle remain the transport of choice for millions of people, even though most know these vehicles harm the environment through exhaust fumes and carbon emissions. This essay will explain why this continues and suggest what can be done to reduce the use of private vehicles.

There are several reasons why people keep driving and riding despite the harm. The most obvious is convenience: a private vehicle can take a person from door to door at any time, without waiting for a bus or carrying shopping home. In many cities and almost all rural areas, public transport is slow, crowded or simply unavailable, so owning a car is seen as a necessity rather than a choice. In addition, for many people a car or motorcycle is a symbol of success and independence, a social pressure that is hard to resist even when they understand the environmental cost.

Reducing this dependence needs action from governments, employers and individuals. Governments should invest heavily in fast, cheap and reliable public transport, and build safe cycling lanes so short journeys no longer need a motor. They could also introduce congestion charges and parking limits in city centres to make driving less attractive, while subsidising electric vehicles and shared mobility schemes. Companies can meanwhile encourage employees to work from home part of the week, and individuals can walk, cycle or carpool for the journeys where these options are practical.

In conclusion, people continue to use private vehicles mainly because of convenience, weak public transport and social status. However, by improving alternatives and making driving more expensive and less convenient, governments and individuals together can reduce the number of cars and motorcycles on the road.

TA：范文采用 problem-solution 标准结构，Introduction 以 This essay will explain why ... and suggest what can be done ... 句型一次性点明"原因 + 解决"两问任务，Body 1 用 convenience（door to door / no waiting）+ weak public transport（slow, crowded, unavailable）+ social status（symbol of success）三层证据回应第一问，Body 2 用 government action（public transport / cycling lanes / congestion charges / subsidies）+ employer / individual habits（work from home / walk, cycle, carpool）回应第二问，结尾以 both ... and ... 总结两问答案，任务回应完整、字数 282，落在 270–290 区间。
