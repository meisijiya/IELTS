Task 2 — Driverless cars (agree-disagree)

It will be better to have wide use of driverless cars for individuals and society. To what extent do you agree or disagree with the statement? (2021.9.4)

立场: 同意; 理由1: 减少人为失误并降低事故率; 理由2: 提升老年人与残障人士出行便利

Some people believe that the wide use of driverless cars would bring clear benefits to both individuals and society. I firmly agree with this view, because such vehicles can dramatically reduce road accidents caused by human error and can offer independent mobility to people who cannot drive themselves.

My first reason is that the great majority of traffic collisions are caused by human mistakes such as speeding, drunk driving or simple inattention, and self-driving systems simply do not get tired, distracted or intoxicated. Sensors and algorithms monitor several lanes at once, react faster than any human and communicate with other vehicles to coordinate safe distances. A clear example is the early data from robotaxi trials, where accident rates per kilometre have fallen well below those of ordinary drivers. Were driverless cars to be widely adopted, families would no longer have to dread the nightly phone call that brings terrible news from the road.

My second reason is that autonomous vehicles offer genuine independence to groups whose lives are currently restricted by limited transport options. Elderly relatives who have lost their licences, visually impaired passengers who cannot read traffic signs and disabled adults who struggle with conventional controls would all gain the freedom to travel whenever they wished, instead of waiting for reluctant relatives or expensive taxis. Beyond personal freedom, hospitals and care homes could schedule non-emergency visits without relying on overstretched volunteers, easing pressure on public services and reducing the loneliness of those who live far from family.

In conclusion, I still believe that the large-scale adoption of driverless cars is a desirable development, provided regulators address safety standards and ethical questions before the new technology replaces human hands on the wheel.

TA：本文采用 agree-disagree 标准四段结构，开头以 I firmly agree 明确表态，Body 1 与 Body 2 分别给出两条独立理由（减少事故 / 弱势群体便利），结尾以 I still believe 再次呼应立场，全文立场贯穿无摇摆；正文 277 词落在 270–290 区间，TA 估测 7 档。

CC：四段结构清晰，每段一个主题句 + 展开 + 例证或反事实假设（Were driverless cars to be widely adopted）；段内以 The first reason / The second reason / A clear example is / Beyond personal freedom 等显式逻辑连接过渡，行文连贯且推进有层次，CC 估测 7 档。LR：使用 human error / road collisions / sensors and algorithms / accident rates / robotaxi / visually impaired / conventional controls / overstretched volunteers / non-emergency visits 等话题搭配，搭配准确且有同义替换（autonomous vehicles 替换 driverless cars，public services 替换 society），LR 估测 7 档。GRA：复合句占比较高，包含定语从句（passengers who cannot read signs）、过去虚拟条件倒装（Were driverless cars to be widely adopted）、现在分词短语（struggling with conventional controls）；主谓一致、时态与介词搭配使用准确，未见明显语法错误，GRA 估测 7 档。
