Task 2 — Gap year before university (advantage-disadvantage)

School leavers go travelling or work before they go directly to the university. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages on their study? (2021.8.28)

立场: 利大于弊; 理由1: 间隔年让学生明确学习动机、获得实践经验，进入大学后学习更投入; 理由2: 缺点可控，半年至一年内的学术衔接损失可弥补，且能培养成熟的学习态度。

It has become increasingly common for school leavers to spend a year travelling or working before enrolling in university. While this delay carries some academic risks, on balance I believe the advantages for their subsequent studies clearly outweigh the disadvantages.

The principal benefit of a gap year is that students return to their studies with stronger motivation and clearer goals. A teenager who has spent several months in a foreign country or in a workplace often discovers what he or she genuinely wants to study, instead of choosing a major based solely on parental pressure or peer fashion. Having experienced a slice of adult life, these students tend to manage their time more responsibly and engage more deeply with academic content. Universities frequently report that gap-year entrants achieve higher grades and lower dropout rates than those who arrive directly from school.

The most common drawback is the loss of academic momentum. Students who delay entry may forget content from their final school year and find the transition to university-level work more difficult. There is also a financial cost, since earnings during the gap year are often modest and tuition fees continue to rise. Nevertheless, these disadvantages are relatively short-term and largely reversible: a refresher course or summer reading can recover lost knowledge, and a well-spent gap year usually produces mature learners who cope better with the demands of higher education.

In conclusion, although a year away from study involves some academic risk, the long-term benefits of increased motivation, practical experience and personal maturity far outweigh those temporary drawbacks. Were universities to offer structured support for incoming gap-year students, the advantages would be even more pronounced.
