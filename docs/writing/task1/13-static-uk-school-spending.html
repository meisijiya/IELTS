Task 1 — UK school spending breakdown

The three pie charts below show the changes in annual spending by a particular UK school in 1981, 1991 and 2001. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

The three pie charts compare how a UK school divided its annual spending between five categories of costs in 1981, 1991 and 2001.

Overall, teachers' salaries formed the largest share of expenditure every year, reaching exactly half of the total in 1991. In contrast, spending on other workers' salaries fell steadily, while insurance stayed the smallest category.

In 1981, teachers' salaries accounted for 40 per cent of the budget and other workers' salaries for 28 per cent. By 1991, the teachers' share had grown to 50 per cent, whereas other workers' salaries dropped to 22 per cent. Furniture and equipment and resources such as books each took 15 per cent in 1981, but by 1991 furniture spending had fallen sharply to just 5 per cent, while books rose to 20 per cent. Insurance contributed only 2 per cent and 3 per cent respectively.

By 2001, teachers' salaries remained dominant at 45 per cent. Furniture and equipment jumped to 23 per cent, overtaking resources (9 per cent) and other workers' salaries (15 per cent), while insurance more than doubled to 8 per cent, still the lowest category.

TA / TR：完整覆盖三年×五类数据（1981/1991/2001 各饼图的五个占比），重点报告最大项教师工资的变化（40% → 50% → 45%）与最显著波动（家具设备 15% → 5% → 23%、书籍资源 15% → 20% → 9%、其他员工工资 28% → 22% → 15%、保险 2% → 3% → 8%）；所有数字与饼图逐字一致，无杜撰。

CC / LR / GRA：四段式（Introduction → Overview → 1981/1991 两饼 → 2001 一饼）；衔接用 whereas / while / despite 做对比，in contrast / the most notable change 显式引出 overview 与峰值；LR 使用 account for / share of expenditure / the dominant item / overtake 等占比与比较词汇；GRA 长短句交替，百分数用 per cent 拼写且与数字配合得当。

  • dominant item — 占比最大的项目
  • overtake — 超过
  • fell sharply — 急剧下降
